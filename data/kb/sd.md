# South Dakota Government Services — GovServ Knowledge Base

## Metro

### Sioux Falls
**Sioux Area Metro (SAM) — Sioux Falls Public Transit** — Public transit across Sioux Falls: fixed-route buses, on-demand service, and ADA complementary paratransit. Paratransit fare is $2.50 per one-way ride for riders over 18; riders 18 and under ride paratransit free (per the SAM rider guide). Eligibility: All riders for fixed-route and on-demand service; Paratransit for riders unable to use fixed-route buses — eligibility determination required. Rolling (Varies — see program page). Phone: 605-460-6256. Cost: Fares apply — see rider guide. Apply: https://www.siouxfalls.gov/files/assets/public/v/1/resident-services/sam/2023-final_riders_guide.pdf

### Rapid City
**Rapid Transit System (RapidRide) — Rapid City** — Public bus service in Rapid City: six RapidRide fixed routes Monday–Friday 6:20 a.m.–5:50 p.m. and Saturday 9:50 a.m.–4:40 p.m., plus Dial-A-Ride ADA paratransit. Adult cash fare is $1.50 one-way; $0.75 for disabled and elderly riders (per the 2024 passenger handbook). Eligibility: All riders for fixed-route service; Dial-A-Ride for qualified ADA paratransit customers. Rolling (Varies — see program page). Phone: 605-394-6631. Cost: Fares apply — see handbook. Apply: https://www.rapidtransitsystem.org/assets/docs/uploads/rapid-ride-passenger-handbook2024.pdf

### Aberdeen
**Aberdeen Ride Line Transportation Services** — Curb-to-curb demand-response transit in Aberdeen, open to the general public including seniors, people with disabilities, and youth. Hours are Monday–Friday 7 a.m.–7 p.m. (last pickup 6:30 p.m.); cost is $3 per boarding, with 10-ride passes for $30 and 20-ride passes for $60. Schedule rides by 4 p.m. the prior business day. Also offers a Summit bus connection and Medicaid out-of-town medical appointment rides. Eligibility: General public — everyone, including disabled, elderly, and youth riders. Rolling (Schedule by 4 p.m. the prior business day). Phone: 605-626-3333. Cost: $3 per boarding; passes available. Apply: https://www.aberdeen.sd.us/182/Ride-Line-Transportation-Services

### Brookings
**Brookings Area Transit Authority (BATA)** — Nonprofit public transit serving the Brookings area, including demand-response rides and service from Brookings south along I-29 to Sioux Falls. Call dispatch to schedule a ride. Eligibility: Open to the general public in the Brookings area. Rolling (Varies — see program page). Phone: 605-692-2222. Cost: Fares apply — confirm with dispatch. Apply: https://dot.sd.gov/media/3835a7fb/200-46350-0193SDOnTRACKGenericBro.pdf

### Watertown
**Community Transit of Watertown/Sisseton** — Nonprofit public transit serving Watertown and surrounding counties including Codington, Day, Grant, Marshall, Roberts, and Spink. Provided about 192,000 rides across its 15-county service area in fiscal year 2025, serving seniors, veterans, students, employees, and riders needing medical, shopping, and employment trips. Eligibility: Open to the general public in the service area. Rolling (Varies — see program page). Phone: 605-882-5287. Cost: Fares apply — confirm when scheduling. Apply: http://www.communitytransitws.com/

## State
**Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP)** — Helps eligible low-income South Dakotans buy food. Apply online, by mail, or in person at a local DSS office; the DSS SNAP page lists eligibility rules and required verifications. Eligibility: South Dakota residents with limited income — see DSS page for income limits and verifications. Rolling (Varies — see program page). Cost: Free to apply. Apply: http://Dss.Sd.Gov/economicassistance/snap.aspx
**Low Income Energy Assistance Program (LIEAP)** — Helps qualifying South Dakota households pay home heating costs. Apply by email, phone, online, or at a local DSS office; income and heating-cost documentation are required. Eligibility: South Dakota households within published income guidelines — see program brochure; Heating-cost documentation required. Rolling (Varies — see program page). Phone: 800-233-8503. Cost: Free to apply. Apply: https://dss.sd.gov/economicassistance/energyassistance/lowincome.aspx
**South Dakota Medicaid** — Federal and state-funded health coverage for eligible South Dakotans, covering medically necessary services. Apply online or contact the Medicaid office for help. Eligibility: South Dakota residents who meet income and categorical requirements — see DSS Medicaid page. Rolling (Varies — see program page). Phone: 605-773-3165, 877-999-5612. Cost: Free to apply. Apply: https://dss.sd.gov/medicaid/
