# District of Columbia — GovServ (verified 2026-09-19)

## Metro

- **Metro Lift — Reduced Fares** (WMATA): 50% off Metro Bus/Rail for DC/MD/VA SNAP enrollees; excludes passes and MetroAccess. Free enrollment. Language help: 202-637-7000; TTY 202-638-3780. https://www.wmata.com/pay/fare-programs/metrolift.html
- **MetroAccess — ADA Paratransit** (WMATA): Shared-ride door-to-door paratransit for people unable to use fixed-route service; application + in-person functional assessment required. (202) 962-2700; TTY (202) 962-2033. https://www.wmata.com/service/accessibility/metro-access/upload/MetroAccess-Customer-Guide.pdf

## County

DC has no counties. The District of Columbia functions as a single city-county-state equivalent, so services below are listed under State (District-wide, including Washington city services) and Federal.

## State

- **SNAP — Food assistance** (DHS): Food-purchase help for low-income households. New ABAWD rules began June 1, 2026. Apply via District Direct or paper; ESA (202) 727-5355. https://dhs.dc.gov/page/public-benefits
- **DC Medicaid** (DHCF): Health coverage for qualified low-income/disabled residents. Apply through IMA service centers. (202) 727-5355. https://dhcf.dc.gov/service/medicaid
- **WIC** (DC Health): Nutrition help for pregnant/postpartum people, infants, kids 1-5. Call 202-442-9397 for screening. https://dchealth.dc.gov/service/apply-wic
- **TANF** (DHS): Cash aid for income-eligible families with a child. Congress Heights teams move to Anacostia Sept 23, 2026; structural changes begin Oct 1, 2027. (202) 727-5355. https://dhs.dc.gov/page/public-benefits
- **Unemployment Insurance** (DOES): Initial claims online via DCNetworks (not by phone); ID.me required. Weekly continued claim within 7 days. (202) 724-7000. https://unemployment.dc.gov/page/apply-benefits
- **LIHEAP** (DOEE): Energy-cost help. CLOSED for FY2026 — no new applications; check for updates Oct 1, 2026. Contact 311. https://doee.dc.gov/node/9412
- **Real Property Tax Relief** (OTR): Homestead Deduction, senior/disabled, disabled-veteran relief. (202) 727-4829. https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/page/real-property-tax-reliefs-credits-and-deductions
- **REAL ID / Driver Credentials** (DC DMV): Original identity, SSN, residency proof required; lawful-presence rules; Limited Purpose credential for visitor-visa holders. https://dmv.dc.gov/node/1119192
- **HomeAdvantage DC** (DCHFA): Mortgage options with/without down-payment help; start with a participating lender; DC home, primary residence. https://dchfa.org/homeownership/homeadvantage-dc/
- **Department of Aging and Community Living** (DACL): Senior centers, nutrition, aging-in-place, caregiver services. (202) 724-5626. https://dacl.dc.gov
- **Mayor's Office of Veterans Affairs** (OVA): VA claims/benefits help, advocacy, outreach. (202) 724-5454. https://ova.dc.gov
- **Department on Disability Services** (DDS): Disability supports incl. developmental disabilities and vocational rehabilitation. (202) 730-1700. https://dds.dc.gov
- **DCTAG** (OSSE): Tuition help. 2026-27 application CLOSED (ran Feb 2-Aug 21, 2026, 3 PM); annual application required. (202) 741-6406; TTY 711. https://osse.dc.gov/dctag
- **DBH Access HelpLine** (DBH): 24/7 behavioral-health help. 1-888-793-4357; TDD (202) 673-7500. https://dbh.dc.gov/page/information-
- **Small Business Services (DSLBD)**: Counseling, workshops, CBE services, commercial revitalization, DC APEX procurement help. (202) 727-3900. http://dslbd.dc.gov/page/our-divisions
- **Legal Aid DC**: Free legal help for DC matters only (family/DV, housing, immigration, benefits, reentry, consumer). Generally <=200% FPL. Intake (202) 628-1161 Mon/Wed 10 AM-3 PM. https://intake.legalaiddc.org/
- **Inclusionary Zoning (IZ)** (DHCD): Affordable rental/for-sale units in new developments; orientation + registration required; DC residents first. https://dhcd.dc.gov/node/1298716
- **Home Purchase Assistance Program (HPAP)** (DHCD): Down payment/closing-cost help for first-time buyers; interest-free loan. Apply via CBOs: (202) 667-7006. https://dhcd.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/dhcd/publication/attachments/Homebuyer%20Assistance%20Program%20Fact%20Sheet%20December%20English%202017%20b.pdf
- **Rent Control (Rent Stabilization)** (DHCD/RAD): Limits annual rent increases in covered buildings; unregistered units automatically covered. https://dhs.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/dhs/page_content/attachments/FAQ%20for%20Tenant%20Associations_STANDARD_0.pdf
- **Housing Choice Voucher Program (HCVP)** (DCHA): Federal rental assistance (~30% of income). WAITLIST CLOSED, no reopening planned. 202-535-1000. https://www.dchousing.org/wordpress/vouchers/faq/
- **Local Rent Supplement Program (LRSP)** (DCHA): Local rental subsidy for families at <=30% AMI; some need DHS referral. 202-535-1000. https://www.dchousing.org/wordpress/vouchers/faq/
- **Family Re-Housing Stabilization Program** (DHS): Up to 12 months rental help + case management for families leaving shelter. Referral by approved provider; no FY2026 extensions. rapidrehousing@dc.gov. https://dhs.dc.gov/page/family-re-housing-stabilization-program
- **Emergency Rental Assistance (ERAP)** (DHS): Help for residents under 40% AMI with eviction emergencies; once per 12 months. Appointment scheduling currently PAUSED. 202-507-6666. https://erap.dhs.dc.gov/faqs
- **Emergency Shelter / Homeless Services** (DHS): Individuals call 202-399-7093 or 311; families use VWFRC (202-526-0017, Mon-Thu 8:30 AM-4 PM, Fri 8:30 AM-12 PM). https://erap.dhs.dc.gov/faqs
- **DC 311** (OUC): One-stop city services, 24/7/365. Dial 311; outside DC (202) 737-4404; text NEW/MENU to 32311. https://311.dc.gov
- **My School DC**: Public-school lottery for PK3/PK4, out-of-boundary/selective DCPS, charters. 2026 deadlines passed (Feb 2 / Mar 2); post-lottery applications still accepted. (202) 888-6336. https://www.myschooldc.org/zh/node/37
- **DC Water Customer Assistance** (DC Water/DOEE): Income-qualified water/sewer discounts; flexible payment plans (202) 354-3600; SPLASH emergency help (202) 265-8200 Mon-Fri 9-5. http://www.dcwater.com/customer-center/financial-assistance/customer-assistance
- **UDC Community College** (UDC): Open admissions; associate degrees/certificates incl. nursing, respiratory therapy, aviation maintenance, IT. Lamond-Riggs Campus, 5171 South Dakota Ave NE. https://www.udc.edu/news/2023/05/the-university-of-the-district-of-columbia-renames-the-bertie-backus-campus-to-the-lamond-riggs-campus
- **DC Child Care Subsidy** (OSSE): Pays part/all of child care for low-moderate income families. WAITLIST since May 12, 2026 (Priority Groups 1-3 may enroll). (202) 829-2500. https://osse.dc.gov/subsidy
- **DC Futures Scholarship** (OSSE): Last-dollar scholarship + coaching for first associate/bachelor's. 2026-27 CLOSED to new applicants (returning participants only). (202) 727-2824. https://osse.dc.gov/node/1574531
- **DC Health Link** (Health Benefit Exchange): Official ACA marketplace. 855-532-5465 Mon-Fri 8 AM-6 PM; Medicaid/small-business enrollment year-round. https://www.dchealthlink.com/news/2024-01/one-week-left-sign-health-insurance-through-dchealthlinkcom-mayor-bowser-urges
- **Office of the Tenant Advocate** (OTA): Free tenant advocacy for DC renters. (202) 719-6560. https://dhs.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/dhs/page_content/attachments/FAQ%20for%20Tenant%20Associations_STANDARD_0.pdf
- **DC Parks and Recreation (DPR)** — Washington city: Free/low-cost seasonal programs; register online. (202) 673-7647. https://dpr.dc.gov/release/bowser-administration-opens-registration-spring-programs
- **DC Public Library — MLK Jr. Memorial Library** — Washington city: Adult Learning, Center for Accessibility, passport acceptance, Labs, People's Archive. 901 G St NW. https://www.dclibrary.org/location-type/central-library

## Federal

- **Washington DC VA Medical Center** (VA): Appointments, medical advice, pharmacy, mental health, homeless-veteran care. 50 Irving St NW. 202-745-8000; toll-free 877-328-2621; TTY 711. Crisis line: 988 press 1. https://www.va.gov/washington-dc-health-care/contact-us/
- **Social Security Retirement Benefits** (SSA): Apply online, by phone, or in person; up to 4 months before desired start. 1-800-772-1213; TTY 1-800-325-0778, Mon-Fri 8 AM-7 PM. http://www.ssa.gov/faqs/en/questions/KA-01891.html
- **SBA — Washington Metropolitan Area District Office** (SBA): Funding-program help, counseling, federal-contracting certifications, disaster recovery, partner referrals. https://www.sba.gov/district/washington-metropolitan
- **Lifeline Phone and Internet Discount** (FCC): Affordable phone/internet for qualifying households (income <=135% FPG or qualifying program); one benefit per household; annual recertification. 1-800-234-9473. https://www.fcc.gov/lifeline-consumers
