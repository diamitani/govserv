## State

**Food & health**
- **Food and Nutrition Services (FNS)** — monthly SNAP food benefits on an EBT card — 1-866-719-0141 — [ePASS](https://epass.nc.gov)
- **WIC Nutrition Program** — food, nutrition education, breastfeeding support; pregnant women, new moms, infants, kids under 5; 185% FPL limit — 1-844-601-6881 — [ncdhhs.gov](https://www.ncdhhs.gov/divisions/child-and-family-well-being/community-nutrition-services-section/wic/apply-wic)
- **NC Medicaid (including Expansion)** — health coverage; expansion covers ages 19-64 under 138% FPL — 919-527-6300 — [ePASS](https://epass.nc.gov)

**Money & family**
- **Work First Cash Assistance (TANF)** — cash aid plus job training for families with children; up to 24 months after entering work components — 919-527-6300 — [ePASS](https://epass.nc.gov)
- **NC Child Support Services** — establish/enforce child support; apply online with NCID within 10 business days of starting; $25 fee — 800-992-9457 — [ncchildsupport.ncdhhs.gov](https://ncchildsupport.ncdhhs.gov/ecoa/caseApplicationInfo/1.htm)

**Jobs & legal**
- **Unemployment Insurance (DES)** — weekly benefits after job loss; weekly certifications + NCWorks job searches required — 888-737-0259 — [des.nc.gov](https://www.des.nc.gov/need-help/faqs/ui)
- **Legal Aid of North Carolina** — free civil legal help for low-income residents; JusticeHub intake — 855-733-3711 — [legalaidnc.org/apply](http://legalaidnc.org/apply/)

**Housing & utilities**
- **NC Home Advantage (NCHFA)** — first-home mortgage, down-payment, and loan-pool programs via participating lenders — [nchfa.com](https://www.nchfa.com/home-buyers)
- **Energy Assistance (LIEAP / CIP)** — heating-season vendor payments; LIEAP runs Dec 1, 2026 – Mar 31, 2027; crisis help year-round; apply at county DSS — 919-527-6300 — [ncdhhs.gov](https://www.ncdhhs.gov/efs-fnsep-12-2026a2/open)

**Disability & veterans**
- **Vocational Rehabilitation for the Blind** — counseling, training, job placement for people with vision loss — [ncdhhs.gov](https://www.ncdhhs.gov/divisions/services-blind/vision-loss/vocational-rehabilitation-blind)
- **NC Military & Veterans Affairs Benefits** — State Veterans Homes, cemeteries, property tax exclusion up to $45,000 for disabled veterans, education benefits — [milvets.nc.gov](https://www.milvets.nc.gov/services)

**Other statewide**
- **NC REAL ID Driver License & ID Card** — federally compliant ID for domestic flights and federal facilities; apply in person at NCDMV — [ncdot.gov](https://www.ncdot.gov/dmv/license-id/nc-real-id/Pages/default.aspx)
- **NC DSS Benefits Contact Directory** — phone numbers for FNS, energy/crisis aid, Work First, refugee assistance, child support, foster youth — 919-527-6300 — [ncdhhs.gov](https://www.ncdhhs.gov/divisions/social-services/about-dss/dss-contact-information)

## Federal
- **Social Security Disability Benefits (SSDI/SSI)** — monthly benefits when disability affects work for 12+ months or is expected to result in death — [ssa.gov](https://www.ssa.gov/disability/eligibility)
- **VA Health Care — Durham VA Health Care System** — enroll online, by phone, mail, or in person (VA Form 10-10EZ) — 919-286-0411 — [va.gov](https://www.va.gov/durham-health-care/register-for-care/)
- **FCC Lifeline Phone & Internet Discount** — monthly discount; 135% FPG or SNAP/Medicaid/SSI participation; one per household — 1-800-234-9473 — [fcc.gov](https://www.fcc.gov/lifeline-consumers)
- **FAFSA Federal Student Aid** — required for federal grants, work-study, and loans; student and each contributor need a StudentAid.gov account — [studentaid.gov](https://studentaid.gov/articles/fafsa-for-parents/)
