# Iowa Services Guide

45 verified services covering Des Moines / Polk County, Cedar Rapids / Linn County, Davenport / Scott County, Sioux City / Woodbury County, Iowa City / Johnson County, statewide Iowa, and federal programs. All entries verified 2026-09-19.

## Metro

### Des Moines

**Report a Problem (Des Moines)** — City of Des Moines. Report potholes, graffiti, water issues, traffic signs, sewer/stormwater, trash, and snow plowing via myDSMmobile or (515) 283-4950. Utilities. Free. http://www.dsm.city/report/report_a_problem.php

**DART Half Fare and Ride to Thrive** — Des Moines Area Regional Transit. Half Fare for 65+, people with disabilities, refugees, students; Ride to Thrive for food/housing/workforce-assistance recipients (two-year enrollment). Apply in person at DART Central Station, 620 Cherry St, Des Moines. Transportation. Reduced fare. https://www.ridedart.com/fares/reduced-fare

**Des Moines Public Library Card** — Free for Iowa residents at six locations. Pre-register online, then visit within 60 days with current photo ID + proof of permanent address. Education. Free. https://www.dmpl.org/get-card

**Des Moines Municipal Housing Agency (Public Housing & Section 8)** — STATUS: paused. Section 8 waitlist closed; public housing waitlist closed for 2–5 bedroom units (only households of two or fewer accepted). HUD-funded rental assistance for Des Moines and Polk County. Housing. Free to apply. http://www.dsm.city/departments/housing/index.php

### Cedar Rapids

**CR Transit Income-Based Free Fare** — Free bus fare for adults at/below 160% FPL. Qualify with SNAP, FIP, Medicaid, Section 8, LIHEAP, or Linn County General Assistance. Temporary letter valid 30 days, exchanged for photo ID; first card free, replacement $3. Bring photo ID + proof. (319) 286-5573. Transportation. https://www.cedar-rapids.org/Transit/Documents/2026_CR%20Income-Based%20Free%20Fare%20Program.pdf

**Civil Rights Complaints (Cedar Rapids)** — File discrimination complaints (housing, employment, public accommodation) with the Civil Rights Commission. (319) 286-5036. Legal Aid. Free. https://cedar-rapids.org/i_want_to/contact/city_services_directory.php

### Davenport

**Davenport ADA Paratransit (CitiBus)** — Door-to-door rides for Davenport residents whose disabilities prevent fixed-route use (within 3/4 mile of route). $2 age 9+, under 8 free with paying adult, one PCA free. Application: 563-888-2151; schedule via River Bend Transit 563-386-1350 / 800-292-8959. Transportation. https://cityofdavenportiowa.hosted.civiclive.com/cms/One.aspx?portalId=6481456&pageId=19545407

**Davenport Housing Authority (Housing Choice Voucher)** — STATUS: paused. Waitlist closed; 2026-2027 plan states it will open in 2027. Preference for chronically homeless individuals/families. Housing. Free to apply. https://www.davenportiowa.com/UserFiles/Servers/Server_6481372/File/Departments/CPED/HCV/Annual%20Plan-%202026-2027.pdf

### Sioux City

**Sioux City Transit Reduced Fares** — Reduced fares for people with disabilities, seniors 62+, Medicare cardholders, students through high school; under 5 free with adult. Service-connected disabled veterans ride free with VA photo ID. Dispatch: 712-279-6404. Transportation. https://www.sioux-city.org/743/Fares-Easy-Steps-Guidelines-Lost-Found

**Sioux City Housing Authority (Housing Choice Voucher)** — Income-limited rental assistance (generally ~30% of adjusted income toward rent/utilities). Online application active; estimated wait about two years, subject to change. Housing. Free to apply. https://www.sioux-city.org/354/Sioux-City-Housing-Authority

### Iowa City

**Iowa City Transit** — Fare-free fixed-route buses open to the public. 13 routes Monday–Saturday, all wheelchair-accessible. 319-356-5151, ICTransit@iowa-city.org. Transportation. Free. https://www.icgov.org/government/departments-and-divisions/transportation/transit

**Iowa City Housing Authority (HCV & Public Housing)** — STATUS: paused. Not currently accepting HCV or Public Housing applications. Homeless: Shelter House 319-351-0326 (24/7); at-risk households to Iowa Housing Help. 319-356-5400. Housing. Free. https://www.icgov.org/government/departments-and-divisions/neighborhood-and-development-services/neighborhood-services/housing-authority

**Iowa City Free Medical and Dental Clinic** — Nonprofit at 2440 Towncrest Dr, Iowa City. Free care for people lacking access. Walk-in medical clinics Tue 9–2 and Thu 9–noon as available; appointments, Rx refills, vision vouchers, social worker by availability. 319-337-4459. Healthcare. Free. http://freemedicalclinic.org/

## County

### Polk County

**Harm Reduction Supplies (Polk County Health)** — Free Narcan, emergency contraception, condoms/lube, gun locks, safer-use supplies from a 24/7 vending machine at 1907 Carpenter Ave, Des Moines. No ID or appointment; confidential; while supplies last. Healthcare. Free. https://www.polkcountyiowa.gov/health-department/education-resources/harm-reduction/

**Healthy Women Program (Polk County Health)** — May provide no-cost breast/cervical cancer screening via grants. Women meeting age/clinical criteria, income at/below 250% FPL, uninsured or inadequate coverage. (515) 286-3642. Healthcare. No cost for eligible participants. https://www.polkcountyiowa.gov/health-department/healthy-women-program/

### Linn County

**Linn County Public Health Clinic Services** — 1020 6th St SE, Cedar Rapids. Free/reduced hepatitis, STD, HIV, pregnancy testing for qualifiers; TB testing; adult/child immunizations; free condoms; Healthy Home assessments. Appointments strongly encouraged. 319-892-6093 (clinic), 319-892-6000 (office). Healthcare. https://gis.linncountyiowa.gov/web-data/lccs/community-resources/Health-Resources.pdf

### Scott County

**Scott County Health Department** — Public-health services including immunization clinics (call 563-326-8618 for appointments), health alerts, and disease response. Healthcare. Free or low-cost. https://www.scottcountyiowa.gov/health

## State

**SNAP — Food Assistance (Iowa)** — Iowa HHS. Monthly EBT food benefits. Apply online, at a local HHS office, or by email/fax/mail; interview + ID/income/housing/household documents required. Food. Free. https://hhs.iowa.gov/assistance-programs/apply

**WIC — Women, Infants, and Children (Iowa)** — Iowa HHS. Supplemental foods, breastfeeding support, nutrition education, health referrals for pregnant/postpartum women and children through age 5. Apply at a local WIC clinic. Food. Free. https://hhs.iowa.gov/assistance-programs/apply

**Medicaid (Iowa)** — Iowa HHS. Health coverage for eligible low-income Iowans. Apply online, via healthcare.gov, at a local HHS office or FQHC, by paper/email/fax/mail, or 1-855-889-7985. Processing up to 45 days. Healthcare. Free to apply. https://hhs.iowa.gov/assistance-programs/apply

**Hawki — Children's Health Insurance (Iowa)** — Iowa HHS. Coverage for uninsured children in families above Medicaid limits. Online, 1-800-257-8563, or mail. Healthcare. Free to apply. https://hhs.iowa.gov/media/8116/download?inline

**HIPP — Health Insurance Premium Payment (Iowa)** — Iowa HHS. Medicaid may pay a member's employer/individual premiums when cost-effective. 515-974-3282, 1-888-346-9562. Healthcare. Free. https://hhs.iowa.gov/assistance-programs/apply

**Family Planning Program (Iowa)** — Iowa HHS. Limited family-planning coverage for eligible Iowans 12–54. Apply via local HHS office or Title X agency. 515-675-5211. Healthcare. Free for eligible members. https://hhs.iowa.gov/assistance-programs/apply

**LIHEAP — Heating Bill Assistance (Iowa)** — Iowa HHS. One-time winter heating payment for eligible low-income households via local community action agency outreach. Utilities. Free. https://hhs.iowa.gov/assistance-programs/apply

**Weatherization Assistance Program (Iowa)** — Iowa HHS. Free home energy upgrades (insulation, air sealing, furnace work) via community action agencies; prioritizes elderly, disabled, families with children. 515-281-3861. Utilities. Free for eligible households. https://hhs.iowa.gov/assistance-programs/apply

**Rent Reimbursement (Iowa)** — Iowa HHS. Up to $1,000 for income-eligible elderly/disabled renters in non-tax-exempt buildings. 1-866-468-7887, 515-420-6077. Housing. Free to apply. https://hhs.iowa.gov/assistance-programs/apply

**Child Care Assistance (Iowa)** — Iowa HHS. Helps working/schooling/training families pay for child care. Apply online, at a local HHS office, or by mail. Family & Childcare. Free to apply (co-pay may apply). https://hhs.iowa.gov/assistance-programs/apply

**Child Support Services (Iowa)** — Iowa HHS. Paternity establishment, support orders, collection and distribution. Apply online. Family & Childcare. Free to apply. https://hhs.iowa.gov/assistance-programs/apply

**Family Investment Program — FIP (Iowa)** — Iowa HHS. Monthly cash + employment/training for families with children. Apply online, at a local HHS office, or by email/fax/mail; income/citizenship/identity verified, decisions up to 30 days. Money & Taxes. Free to apply. https://hhs.iowa.gov/assistance-programs/apply

**State Supplementary Assistance (Iowa)** — Iowa HHS. Monthly payments for special needs of aged, blind, or disabled Iowans not met by SSI. Apply online or by paper. Disability. Free to apply. https://hhs.iowa.gov/assistance-programs/apply

**Unemployment Insurance (Iowa)** — Iowa Workforce Development. Temporary income for workers who lost jobs through no fault of their own. File online or at IowaWORKS; ID.me verification; weekly claims required. 866-239-0843, uiclaimshelp@iwd.iowa.gov. Employment. Free to apply. https://workforce.iowa.gov/media/740/download?inline=

**IowaWORKS Career Services** — Iowa Workforce Development. Free career help at 17 centers: job search, resumes, training referrals. Metro sites: Des Moines (200 Army Post Rd; 525 SW Fifth St), Cedar Rapids (1025 Kirkwood Pkwy SW), Davenport (1801 E Kimberly Rd), Iowa City satellite (1700 S 1st Ave), Sioux City (2508 4th St). Employment. Free. https://workforce.iowa.gov/jobs/iowaworks/map

**Iowa Vocational Rehabilitation Services** — Iowa Workforce Development. Employment services for Iowans with disabilities (counseling, training, placement, assistive tech); Pre-ETS for students 14–21 with disabilities. Apply online, by paper/mail/drop-off, or at a local office. 1-800-532-1486. Disability. Free to apply. https://workforce.iowa.gov/vr/apply-services

**REAL ID (Iowa)** — Iowa DOT. REAL ID-compliant license/ID; in-person appointment required for first REAL ID; arrives by mail in up to 30 days. Bring one identity/lawful-status doc, one SSN doc, two printed Iowa-residency docs. No extra REAL ID fee (replacement $10). Transportation. https://iowadot.gov/drivers-licenses-ids/get-or-renew-drivers-licenses-ids-permits/real-id

**Iowa Legal Aid — Free Civil Legal Help** — Free civil legal aid for income-eligible Iowans, including unemployment fact-finding hearings and appeals (deadlines short and case-specific — check notices). Intake: 800-532-1275; 60+: 800-992-8161. Legal Aid. Free. https://iowalegalaid.org/resource/fact-finding-hearings-and-appeals-for-unemployment-benefits/

**Iowa Earned Income Tax Credit** — Iowa Department of Revenue. Refundable credit = 15% of federal EITC for federal-EITC qualifiers. Low-income-exemption filers can file solely to claim it. Money & Taxes. Free (on Iowa return). https://revenue.iowa.gov/taxes/tax-guidance/individual-income-tax/1040-expanded-instructions/iowa-earned-income-tax-credit

**Iowa Veterans Trust Fund** — Iowa Department of Veterans Affairs. Benefits for veterans (90+ days active federal service, honorable discharge; injury exceptions) and eligible spouses/dependents/surviving spouses: medical travel, retraining/tuition, service-disability-related unemployment/underemployment help, dental/vision/hearing/Rx, durable home equipment, counseling/substance treatment, emergency ambulance/ER, emergency vehicle/housing. Income ≤300% FPL; liquid assets ≤$20,000. Veterans. Free to apply. https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/aco/chapter/801.14.pdf

**FirstHome Mortgage (Iowa Finance Authority)** — First-time buyer mortgage + $2,500 down payment/closing grant (with FirstHome mortgage) or second loan up to 5% of sale price, no monthly payments, repaid on sale/refinance/payoff. Homes for Iowans option for repeat buyers. Apply via IFA participating lender. 515-452-0400, 800-432-7230. Housing. https://opportunityiowa.gov/media/5558/download?inline

**Military Homeownership Assistance (Iowa)** — Iowa Finance Authority. $5,000 grant for down payment/closing costs for eligible service members and veterans. Veterans. Free grant. 515-452-0400, 800-432-7230. https://opportunityiowa.gov/media/5558/download?inline

**SHIIP — Senior Health Insurance Information Program (Iowa)** — Iowa Insurance Division. Free, confidential, unbiased one-on-one Medicare counseling from trained volunteer counselors: plan comparisons, enrollment, billing/appeals, fraud reporting (Senior Medicare Patrol). Open Enrollment Oct 15–Dec 7. 1-800-351-4664. Seniors. Free. https://iowafraudfighters.gov/2025/11/10/iowans-on-medicare-seek-coverage-lower-costs-in-2026/

**Iowa Business Entity Filings (Secretary of State)** — Register and maintain an Iowa business: articles, biennial reports, mostly online via Fast Track Filing. 515-281-5204. Small Business. Fees vary. http://sos.iowa.gov/businesses/business-entity-forms-and-fees

## Federal

**Social Security Benefits (Federal)** — SSA. Monthly payments: retirement (62+, 10+ years of taxes), disability (SSDI), survivors, family, and SSI (65+/disabled/blind with little income and resources). Seniors. Free to apply. https://www.ssa.gov/benefits/

**Medicare (Federal)** — CMS. Health insurance for 65+ meeting citizenship/residency requirements, or under 65 with disability, ESRD, or ALS. Some enroll automatically; others must sign up. Seniors. Costs vary by part; Part A premium-free for most. https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare

**VA Disability Compensation (Federal)** — VA. Monthly tax-free payments for service-connected illness/injury, including PTSD. File online, phone, mail, or fax; VSO reps can help. Veterans. Free to apply. https://www.va.gov/disability/

**IRS Free File (Federal)** — IRS. Free federal e-filing. Iowa covered: guided software for ages 17–85 with ≤$89,000 AGI or active-duty military; Fillable Forms at any income. Start at IRS.gov/freefile. Money & Taxes. Free for qualifiers. https://www.irs.gov/filing/free-file-do-your-federal-taxes-for-free
