## State

- **Maine Home Energy Assistance Program (HEAP)** (Utilities): heating-cost help for homeowners/renters. 2026-27 applications: 8/3/2026 through funds exhaustion or 5/29/2027; ECIP crisis help 11/1/2026-4/30/2027. Phones: 1-800-452-4668, (207) 626-4600. Info: https://mainehousing.org/programs-services/energy/energydetails/liheap
- **Maine Unemployment Insurance** (Employment): temporary employer-funded benefits; weekly certification required. File promptly when unemployed. Info: https://maine.gov/unemployment
- **Maine State Grant Program** (Education): need-based undergraduate grant; 2026-27 max $2,500 full-time ($1,250 half-time); SAI max 10,000 (subject to adjustment); FAFSA required. Info: https://www.famemaine.com/affording-education/pay-for-school/maine-grant-tuition-programs/maine-state-grant-program/
- **Maine Veterans Assistance** (Veterans): accredited Veteran Service Officers plus VEFAP emergency help for urgent expenses. Info: https://www.maine.gov/veterans/maine-veterans-assistance
- **Maine CareerCenters** (Employment): free job-search help statewide: JobLink, workshops, veterans/disability resources. Info: https://mainecareercenter.gov/
- **Maine Vocational Rehabilitation (VR)** (Disability): employment support for people with physical, mental, or emotional disabilities. Info: https://maine.gov/rehab/dvr
- **Property Tax Fairness Credit** (Money & Taxes): refundable credit for homeowners/renters with high property-tax/rent burden; claim on Form 1040ME + Schedule PTFC/STFC. Info: http://www11.maine.gov/revenue/taxes/tax-relief-credits-programs/income-tax-credits/property-tax-fairness-credit
- **211 Maine** (Healthcare): free confidential 24/7 referral helpline (food, housing, utilities, health care); all languages; text ZIP to 898-211. Phones: 211, 1-866-811-5695, out-of-state 1-877-463-6207. Info: http://211maine.org/contact/
- **Maine REAL ID Renewal** (Transportation): most expiring REAL IDs renewable online; first-time applicants in person. Fees vary by credential. Phone: 207-624-9000 x52114. Info: https://www1.maine.gov/sos/news/mainers-can-now-renew-real-ids-online
- **CoverME.gov - Maine Health Insurance Marketplace** (Healthcare): official marketplace; tax credits available; special enrollment generally within 60 days of a qualifying life event (incl. recent MaineCare loss). Info: https://www.coverme.gov/
- **MaineHousing First Home Loan** (Housing): first-time homebuyer mortgage program; income/purchase-price limits apply. Info: https://www.mainehousing.org/programs-services/homebuyer
- **MaineHousing First Generation Homebuyer Program** (Housing): assistance for first-generation buyers; see page for current terms. Info: https://www.mainehousing.org/programs-services/homebuyer
- **Salute ME / Home Again (Veterans Homebuyer Program)** (Veterans): MaineHousing homebuyer assistance for veterans and active-duty military; see page for terms. Info: https://www.mainehousing.org/programs-services/homebuyer
- **Maine Homeowner Assistance Fund (HAF)** (Housing): up to $50,000/household for mortgage, tax, insurance, HOA, lot-rent, or utility arrears (COVID-19 hardship). STATUS: winding down - complete applications after January 10, 5 PM are waitlisted. Hotline: 1-888-664-2569. Info: https://www.ptla.org/maine-homeowner-assistance-fund-haf-faq
- **Maine Foreclosure Prevention Hotline** (Housing): connects homeowners facing foreclosure to housing counselors and free lawyers (PTLA intake hours). Phone: 1-888-664-2569. Info: https://www.ptla.org/maine-homeowner-assistance-fund-haf-faq
- **Maine Centralized Section 8 / Housing Choice Voucher Application** (Housing): one application joins 20 Maine PHAs' voucher waitlists; HUD income limits + citizenship/immigration rules. NOTE: form labeled v4/2024 - confirm current acceptance. Phone: (866) 466-7328. Apply: https://spha.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Full-App-Maine-CWL-4_19_24.pdf
- **Maine Equal Justice - Legal Helpline** (Legal Aid): free legal help for low-income Mainers denied benefits (MaineCare, SNAP, TANF); impact litigation. Helpline: (866) 626-7059 x205; office: (207) 626-7058; 126 Sewall St, Augusta. Info: https://maineequaljustice.org/site/assets/files/4283/whoweare_twopager_policyteam.pdf
- **Legal Services for Maine Elders** (Seniors): free legal advice for Mainers 60+ (health care, benefits, pensions, consumer, abuse, guardianship). Phones: 1-800-750-5353, 207-623-1797. Info: https://mainelse.org/content/about-legal-services-maine-elders

## Federal

- **Social Security Retirement Benefits** (Seniors): monthly benefits on your earnings record; apply online/phone/in person up to 4 months before benefits start. Phones: 1-800-772-1213, TTY 1-800-325-0778. Info: http://www.ssa.gov/faqs/en/questions/KA-01891.html
- **VA Maine Health Care - Registration** (Veterans): enrollment required before appointments; apply online/phone/mail/in person; see page for documents. Info: https://www.va.gov/maine-health-care/register-for-care/
- **FCC Lifeline Program** (Utilities): monthly phone/broadband discount for eligible low-income households. Phone: 1-800-234-9473. Info: https://www.fcc.gov/lifeline-consumers
- **HUD Maine - Housing Resources** (Housing): housing/homelessness referrals, counseling, public housing, eviction, disaster, crisis lines. Phones: housing counseling (800) 569-4287, PHA help (800) 955-2232, general assistance (800) 442-6003, Bangor HUD (207) 945-0467. Info: https://www.hud.gov/me
- **HUD-Approved Housing Counseling** (Housing): free referrals to HUD-approved counselors for renters, buyers, homeowners facing foreclosure. Phone: (800) 569-4287. Info: https://www.hud.gov/me
- **SBA Maine District Office** (Small Business): funding, free counseling, contracting certifications, disaster recovery; offices in Augusta, Bangor, Portland. Info: https://www.sba.gov/district/maine
- **Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA)** (Education): required for federal grants, work-study, loans. Info: https://studentaid.gov/articles/fafsa-for-parents/?fbclid=IwAR3Hf6Cul3VN_S9Pyj8y8RqhLei2pLM0xTQgll75CvT3UmRXNxNJLs11CH8
