# Oklahoma — GovServ Guide (verified 2026-09-19)

## Metro

### Oklahoma City / Oklahoma County

**EMBARK Public Transit (Oklahoma City).** Bus, OKC Streetcar, paratransit, and downtown parking for the Oklahoma City metro, operated by EMBARK. EMBARK customer service: (405) 235-7433. [EMBARK — routes, fares, and schedules](https://www.embarkok.com/)

**Oklahoma City-County Health Department.** Clinics, immunizations, WIC, restaurant inspections, and public health services for Oklahoma County residents. OCCHD main line: 405-427-8651. [Oklahoma City-County Health Department](https://occhd.org/)

**Metropolitan Library System.** Free library cards, books and media, public computers, job-search help, and children's and teen programs across the Oklahoma City metro. Metropolitan Library System: (405) 231-8650. [Metropolitan Library System](https://www.metrolibrary.org/)

### Tulsa / Tulsa County

**Tulsa 311 — City Services.** Non-emergency access to City of Tulsa services: report potholes, code violations, missed trash, streetlight outages, and get routed to the right department by calling 311, chatting, or reporting online. Call 311: 311. [Tulsa 311 — report and request services](https://www.cityoftulsa.org/government/departments/working-in-neighborhoods/311)

**Tulsa Health Department.** Immunizations, WIC, family planning, restaurant inspections, vital records, and disease prevention for Tulsa County residents. Tulsa Health Department: 918-582-9355. [Tulsa Health Department](https://tulsa-health.org/)

**Tulsa City-County Library.** Free library cards, books and media, public computers, job and career resources, and children's programs across Tulsa County, including the Broken Arrow libraries. Tulsa City-County Library: 918-549-7323. [Tulsa City-County Library](https://www.tulsalibrary.org/)

**Tulsa County Social Services.** Basic necessities, pharmacy assistance, shelter and transitional living, and indigent cremation services for low-income Tulsa County residents. Tulsa County Social Services: 918-596-5560; Tulsa County main line: 918-596-5000. [Tulsa County Social Services](https://www2.tulsacounty.org/government/departments-offices/social-services)

### Norman / Cleveland County

**EMBARK Norman Transit.** Local bus service for the City of Norman, operated by EMBARK, connecting neighborhoods, the University of Oklahoma area, and regional routes. EMBARK customer service: (405) 235-7433. [EMBARK Norman — routes and schedules](https://www.embarkok.com/norman/)

### Broken Arrow / Tulsa County

The City of Broken Arrow's own site (brokenarrowok.gov) blocks automated verification (HTTP 403 from its WAF), so no Broken Arrow-specific service page could be independently verified and none is listed. Broken Arrow residents are served by the Tulsa County programs above — Tulsa Health Department, Tulsa City-County Library (including the Broken Arrow libraries), and Tulsa County Social Services — plus statewide Oklahoma programs below.

### Edmond / Oklahoma County

**Edmond Housing & Utility Assistance.** City-published directory of local providers — including the HOPE Center of Edmond and Samaritan House — offering rent, utility, food, prescription, and clothing help to qualifying households in Edmond-area ZIP codes. HOPE Center of Edmond: 405-348-1340; Samaritan House: 405-216-7554. HOPE Center of Edmond: 1251 N. Broadway, Edmond. [City of Edmond — Housing & Utility Assistance](https://edmondok.gov/1881/Housing-Utility-Assistance)

## County

Oklahoma County, Tulsa County, and Cleveland County services are listed under their metro areas above. The Oklahoma City-County Health Department and Tulsa Health Department serve their counties; Tulsa County Social Services and Tulsa City-County Library serve all of Tulsa County including Broken Arrow.

## State

**SNAP — Food Assistance.** Monthly food benefits on an EBT card for eligible low-income Oklahoma households. Oklahoma's SNAP page ties eligibility to citizenship or qualified non-citizen status, income and work rules, with Social Security numbers for household members. SNAP information line: 1-888-328-6551; OKDHS general line: (405) 522-5050. [Apply for SNAP at OKDHSLive](https://www.okdhslive.org/)

**TANF — Temporary Assistance for Needy Families.** Time-limited cash and basic-needs assistance for families with minor children, paired with employment services and child care support. Applicants must be responsible for a minor child, meet citizenship/qualified-status rules, and cooperate with child support and a work plan. TANF information: (405) 521-4391; TANF statewide line: 1-866-411-1877. [Apply for TANF at OKDHSLive](https://www.okdhslive.org/)

**Child Care Subsidy.** Help paying for licensed child care for qualifying working families. Per a January 2026 OKDHS announcement, income eligibility was realigned to 55% of state median income effective July 1, 2026, and new applications and renewals are reviewed under the current guidelines. Child care subsidy line: (405) 522-5050. [OKDHS January 2026 eligibility update](https://oklahoma.gov/okdhs/newsroom/2026/january/comm01072026.html)

**Child Support Services.** Establishes paternity, obtains court orders for support, and collects and enforces child support payments for Oklahoma families. Oklahoma City office: (405) 522-2273; Tulsa office: (918) 295-3500. [Child support application overview](https://oklahoma.gov/okdhs/services/ocss/appovw.html)

**LIHEAP — Low Income Home Energy Assistance.** Seasonal help with winter heating and summer cooling bills for eligible low-income Oklahoma households, administered by OKDHS. OKDHS general line: (405) 522-5050. [Apply at OKDHSLive](https://www.okdhslive.org/)

**Adult Protective Services — Abuse & Neglect Hotline.** Investigates reports of abuse, neglect, and exploitation of vulnerable adults age 18 and older who cannot protect themselves, and connects them with protective services. Anyone may report; reports can be anonymous and are accepted 24/7. Abuse and Neglect Hotline (24/7): 1-800-522-3511. [Report suspected abuse or neglect online](https://www.ourokdhs.org/s/reportabuse)

**SoonerCare (Oklahoma Medicaid).** Oklahoma's Medicaid program: doctor visits, hospital care, prescriptions, dental, behavioral health, and related services for eligible children, pregnant people, parents and caretaker relatives, seniors, and people with disabilities. SoonerCare helpline: 1-800-987-7767. [Apply through MySoonerCare](https://oklahoma.gov/ohca/individuals/mysoonercare.html)

**WIC — Women, Infants, and Children.** Nutrition program providing healthy foods, nutrition education, breastfeeding support, health screening, and referrals for pregnant, postpartum, and breastfeeding women, infants, and children under 5. State WIC office: (405) 426-8500. [Oklahoma WIC program page](https://oklahoma.gov/health/services/children-family-health/wic.html)

**Unemployment Insurance.** Temporary weekly benefits for Oklahoma workers who lost their jobs through no fault of their own. Claims are filed and certified through OESC, which also runs reemployment services. OESC claimant line: 405-525-1500; OESC main line: 405-557-7100. [OESC — file and manage unemployment claims](https://oklahoma.gov/oesc.html)

**Driver Licenses & Vehicle Services.** Driver licenses and ID cards (including REAL ID), vehicle registration and tag renewal, disability placards, and related motor-vehicle services for Oklahomans. [Service Oklahoma](https://oklahoma.gov/service.html)

**Voter Registration & Voter Portal.** Register to vote, check your registration status, find your polling place, and request absentee ballots through the official OK Voter Portal. [OK Voter Portal — register and check status](https://okvoterportal.okelections.gov/)

**Individual Income Tax Filing (OkTAP).** File and pay Oklahoma individual income taxes, check refund status, and manage tax accounts online 24/7 through OkTAP (Taxpayer Access Point); in-person help is available by appointment. [Oklahoma Tax Commission / OkTAP](https://oklahoma.gov/tax.html)

**Veterans Services.** State veterans benefits counseling and claims assistance, state veterans homes, and the Oklahoma Veterans Registry. ODVA main line: 405-523-4000; ODVA toll-free: (833) 993-0234. [Oklahoma Department of Veterans Affairs](https://oklahoma.gov/veterans.html)

**Vocational Rehabilitation.** Employment services for Oklahomans with disabilities: career counseling, training and education support, assistive technology, and job placement. DRS main line: (405) 951-3400; DRS toll-free: (800) 845-8476. [Oklahoma Department of Rehabilitation Services](https://oklahoma.gov/okdrs.html)

**Developmental Disabilities Services.** Home- and community-based waiver services and supports for Oklahomans with intellectual and developmental disabilities, focused on living as independently as possible in the community. DDS information: (405) 522-3221; OKDHS general line: (405) 522-5050. [Developmental Disabilities Services](https://oklahoma.gov/okdhs/services/dds.html)

**Housing Programs (OHFA).** OHFA's homebuyer down payment assistance (3.5% program), Housing Stability Program, and rental assistance resources for eligible Oklahomans. [OHFA — homebuyer, rental, and stability programs](https://www.ohfa.org/)

**Oklahoma's Promise Scholarship.** Full-tuition scholarship at Oklahoma public colleges and universities for students who enroll in 8th, 9th, or 10th grade, complete the required high school coursework, and meet family income limits. Oklahoma's Promise: 405-225-9152; Oklahoma's Promise toll-free: 800-858-1840. [Oklahoma's Promise](https://okpromise.org/)

**CareerTech.** Career and technology education across Oklahoma: full-time career programs, adult education and family literacy, industry certifications, and workforce training delivered through technology centers statewide. [Oklahoma CareerTech](https://oklahoma.gov/careertech.html)

**Legal Aid Services of Oklahoma.** Free civil legal help for low-income Oklahomans: housing and eviction defense, domestic and family issues, health-coverage problems, and senior legal issues through the OK-SPLASH program. Legal Aid intake line: 1-888-534-5243. [Legal Aid Services of Oklahoma](https://legalaidok.org/)

**Oklahoma ABLE Tech.** Assistive technology device demonstrations, short-term device loans, and guidance on funding so Oklahomans with disabilities can try the right equipment before obtaining it. ABLE Tech: 405-744-9748; ABLE Tech toll-free: 800-257-1705. [Oklahoma ABLE Tech](https://www.okabletech.org/)

**Oklahoma Small Business Development Center.** Free one-on-one business advising, training workshops, and help with SBA disaster-loan applications for Oklahoma small businesses and startups. [Oklahoma SBDC](https://www.oksbdc.org/)

**Business Incentives & Support.** State business incentives, site-selection assistance, and workforce programs for companies locating, expanding, or investing in Oklahoma. Department of Commerce: 405-815-6552; Toll-free: 800-879-6552. [Oklahoma Department of Commerce](https://www.okcommerce.gov/)

**Consumer & Utility Services.** Regulates oil and gas, public utilities, and motor carriers in Oklahoma, and takes consumer complaints about regulated utility services. Corporation Commission: (405) 521-2211. [Oklahoma Corporation Commission](https://oklahoma.gov/occ.html)

**211 Oklahoma — Community Resource Referral.** Free, confidential referral helpline connecting Oklahomans with local food, housing, utility, health, and crisis resources. Dial 2-1-1 from any phone. Dial 2-1-1 (toll-free referral line): 211. [211.org — find local help](https://www.211.org/)

## Federal

**Social Security Benefits.** Retirement, disability (SSDI), survivors, and Supplemental Security Income (SSI) benefits: apply online, check earnings statements, and manage benefits through a my Social Security account. National number (unverified): 1-800-772-1213. [Social Security — apply and manage benefits](https://www.ssa.gov/)

**Medicare.** Federal health insurance for people 65 and older and qualifying younger people with disabilities: hospital (Part A), medical (Part B), and prescription drug (Part D) coverage, plus Medicare Advantage options. Medicare helpline (unverified): 1-800-633-4227. [Medicare — enroll and compare plans](https://www.medicare.gov/)

**VA Oklahoma City Health Care.** VA hospital and community clinics serving veterans in central and western Oklahoma: primary care, specialty care, mental health, and pharmacy services. VA benefits line (unverified): 1-800-827-1000. [VA Oklahoma City Health Care](https://www.va.gov/oklahoma-city-health-care/)

**FAFSA — Free Application for Federal Student Aid.** The federal form for student grants, loans, and work-study; most Oklahoma colleges require it, and it is the gateway to federal and many state aid programs. Federal Student Aid line (unverified): 1-800-433-3243. [Apply for FAFSA](https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa)

**SBA Oklahoma District Office.** Federal small-business loans, free counseling, and contracting assistance through the SBA's Oklahoma district office. SBA Oklahoma District Office: 405-609-8000. [SBA Oklahoma District Office](https://legacy.sba.gov/district/oklahoma)

**HUD in Oklahoma.** Federal housing resources for Oklahoma: HUD-approved housing counseling, fair housing complaint filing, and contacts for local public housing authorities. HUD housing counseling line (unverified): 800-569-4287. [HUD in Oklahoma](https://www.hud.gov/states/oklahoma)

**Lifeline (FCC).** Monthly discount on phone or broadband service for qualifying low-income households; subscribers must recertify eligibility each year. Lifeline support center: 1-800-234-9473. [FCC Lifeline — check eligibility and apply](https://www.fcc.gov/lifeline-consumers)

**USAJOBS — Federal Employment.** The official job board for federal employment, including openings at federal facilities across Oklahoma. [USAJOBS — search federal jobs](https://www.usajobs.gov/)
