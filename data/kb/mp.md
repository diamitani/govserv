# Northern Mariana Islands (MP) — GovServ Service Guide

All entries verified 2026-09-19 from official sources. The CNMI has municipalities, not counties — island municipalities are covered under Metro.

## Metro

### Saipan

**Joeten-Kiyu Public Library (Saipan)** (Education) — Public library: books, periodicals, digital media, public computers, community programs for all ages. Tue-Thu 10am-6pm; Fri 1:30pm-7pm; Sat 9:30am-4pm; closed Sun/Mon and observed holidays. 670-235-7322/7318. https://www.cnmilib.org/joeten-kiyu-public-library-to-resume-normal-operating-hours/

**CNMI Small Business Development Center — Saipan** (Small Business) — No-cost business consulting, training, and workshops. (670) 237-6888; 3290 Beach Road Plaza Unit 11, Garapan, Saipan; Mon-Fri 8am-5pm. https://cnmisbdc.com/

**Commonwealth Health Center (Saipan)** (Healthcare, CHCC) — The CNMI's main hospital in Saipan: 24/7 emergency services, outpatient clinics (typically Mon-Fri 7:30am-4:30pm), outpatient pharmacy, public-health programs. Medical records by written request (duplication fees may apply). 670-234-8950; pharmacy (670) 236-8798; medical records (670) 236-8356. https://www.chcc.health/

### Tinian

**CNMI Small Business Development Center — Tinian** (Small Business) — No-cost business consulting, training, and workshops. (670) 237-9211; NMC Tinian Center, San Jose Village, Tinian. https://cnmisbdc.com/

**Tinian Health Center (Lucia "Chiang" Villagomez Arizapa Health Center)** (Healthcare, CHCC) — Tinian's only medical facility: outpatient/primary care, 24/7 ER, pharmacy, lab, radiology, dental, immunization, WIC, telehealth, inter-island medical referrals, rotating specialty outreach (pediatrics, podiatry, OB-GYN, ENT). Mon-Fri 7:30-11:30am and 12:30-4:30pm; ER 24/7. (670) 433-9337 / 9263 / 9333 / 9233. https://www.chcc.health/tinian-healthcenter.php

### Rota

**CNMI Small Business Development Center — Rota** (Small Business) — No-cost business consulting, training, and workshops. (670) 237-6783; NMC Rota Center, Songsong Village, Rota. https://cnmisbdc.com/

## County

The CNMI has no county governments — it is organized into island municipalities (Saipan, Tinian, Rota). Municipal services are listed under Metro above; territorial programs are under State.

## State

**Nutrition Assistance Program (NAP) — CNMI** (Food) — Food assistance for low-income, zero-income, and needy households on Saipan, Tinian, and Rota. Rolling. Disaster NAP (D-NAP) activates only after a declared disaster. Saipan (670) 237-2800; Tinian (670) 433-9330; Rota (670) 532-9354; support@cnminap.gov.mp. Apply: https://cnminap.gov.mp/

**Women, Infants, and Children (WIC) — CNMI** (Food, CHCC) — Nutrition, breastfeeding support, supplemental foods, and referrals for pregnant/breastfeeding/postpartum women, infants, and children under five (nutritional-risk and income requirements; at or below 185% of federal poverty guidelines). Bring enrollees, adult and child ID, proof of income, proof of address. Rolling. (670) 664-4084; Upper Navy Hill (Fuetsa Loop), Saipan. https://www.childcare.gov.mp/woman-infants-and-children-program-wic/

**Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP) — CNMI** (Utilities) — Power subsidies, shutoff/reconnection crisis aid, monthly energy-bill assistance, weatherization, energy-related repairs. U.S. citizen or permanent resident; low/no income; elderly or disabled; applicant should be household head and utility account holder (or landlord documentation). Bring citizenship docs, Social Security cards, marriage certificate where applicable, CUC bill, income docs. (670) 287-1475. https://www.childcare.gov.mp/low-income-home-energy-assistance-program-liheap/

**CNMI Medicaid (and CHIP)** (Healthcare, CNMI State Medicaid Agency) — Health coverage for eligible low-income residents; CHIP covers uninsured children in moderate-income families. No public application website — contact the administrator by phone or visit the Capitol Hill office. CNMI residents are not eligible for Marketplace/SHOP enrollment. Rolling. (670) 664-4890; chcmc@pticom.com. Info: https://www.healthcare.gov/northern-mariana-islands/

**Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) — Northern Marianas Housing Corporation** (Housing) — Federally funded rental assistance administered by NMHC, the CNMI's Section 8 public housing agency. Contact NMHC for current application and waiting-list status (not confirmed as of 2026-09-19). (670) 234-9447; nmhc@nmhc.gov.mp; Saipan, MP 96950. https://nmhcgov.net/

**Vocational Rehabilitation (OVR) — CNMI** (Disability) — Employment services for people with disabilities: assessment, counseling, training, assistive technology, job coaching and placement. Eligibility determinations generally within 60 days of a completed application; some services may require financial participation. (670) 322-6537/8; Navy Hill, Saipan. https://www.ovrgov.net/faq-s

**Micronesian Legal Services — Marianas Office (Free Civil Legal Aid)** (Legal Aid) — Free civil legal help for eligible Saipan, Tinian, and Rota residents: family, consumer debt/bankruptcy, employment, immigration/naturalization, housing, probate, victim/survivor protection. Income below 125% of federal poverty guidelines for Hawaii (200% in some circumstances); asset limits apply. Rolling intake. (670) 234-6243 / (670) 234-7729; marianas@mlscnet.org; Chalan Piao, Saipan, Mon-Fri 8am-5pm. https://micronesianlegal.org/?page_id=81

**Office on Aging — Senior Services** (Seniors, DCCA) — Transportation, meals, home care, and caregiver support for CNMI elders. 670-233-1321 / 670-233-1322. Contact via: https://www.fema.gov/assistance/individual/disaster-survivors/state-local-referral-lists/commonwealth-northern-mariana-islands

**Northern Marianas College Financial Aid (FAFSA)** (Education) — Federal student aid (Pell, TEACH, work-study, FSEOG) and scholarships for NMC students; school code 015746. Effective Fall 2025, all students must submit an online FAFSA before registering (international, DACA, early-admission, non-degree, and undocumented students exempt). Mon-Thu 9am-3pm. https://www.marianas.edu/fao?view=article&id=232%3Aboard-upcoming-meetings&catid=36

**Disaster Unemployment Assistance (DUA) — CNMI** (Employment, CNMI DOL) — CONDITIONAL: temporary benefits for workers jobless due to a declared disaster who are not eligible for regular UI. No active program as of 2026-09-19; activates only under a presidential disaster declaration. CNMI has no regular unemployment-insurance program. DOL 670-664-3190 / 670-322-0996. Contact via: https://www.fema.gov/assistance/individual/disaster-survivors/state-local-referral-lists/commonwealth-northern-mariana-islands

**Northern Mariana Protection and Advocacy Systems (NMPASI)** (Disability) — The CNMI's protection-and-advocacy system for people with disabilities. 670-235-7273 / 7274 / 9506. Contact via: https://www.fema.gov/assistance/individual/disaster-survivors/state-local-referral-lists/commonwealth-northern-mariana-islands

**CHCC Immunization Program** (Healthcare) — Vaccinations, temporary health certificates, immunization records/summaries, community outreach. Mon-Fri 7:30am-6pm, CHCC Main Campus, Public Health. 1 (670) 236-8745. https://www.chcc.health/immunization.php

**CHCC Behavioral Health Crisis Lines** (Healthcare) — Suicide prevention 670-664-4673; youth mental health 670-664-4604; substance abuse prevention 670-323-6560/6561. Emergencies: 911. Free. Contact via: https://www.fema.gov/assistance/individual/disaster-survivors/state-local-referral-lists/commonwealth-northern-mariana-islands

## Federal

**SBA Disaster Loans** (Small Business) — CONDITIONAL: low-interest federal disaster loans for homeowners, renters, businesses, and nonprofits in declared disaster areas. Available in the CNMI only when an SBA disaster declaration is issued. 800-659-2955; TTY 800-877-8339. Contact via: https://www.fema.gov/assistance/individual/disaster-survivors/state-local-referral-lists/commonwealth-northern-mariana-islands
