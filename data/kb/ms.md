# Mississippi Services Guide

35 verified services covering Jackson, Gulfport, Hattiesburg, Hinds / Harrison / DeSoto / Forrest Counties, statewide Mississippi, and federal programs. All entries verified 2026-09-19.

## Metro

### Jackson

**Report a City Issue (Jackson)** — City of Jackson. Report non-emergency issues: potholes, missed trash, streetlight outages, code concerns. Call 311 or 601-960-1193 (Solid Waste). Utilities. Free. https://jacksonms.gov

**JTRAN Bus Service (Jackson)** — STATUS: service delays and/or route suspensions until further notice due to a workforce stoppage. Fixed-route buses. Discount fares for 60+, ages 6-17, Medicare cardholders, people with disabilities with ID. 601-952-1000. Transportation. Fare required; discount fares for qualifiers. https://ridejtran.com/

**Mississippi Regional Housing Authority VI — Rental Assistance (Jackson)** — HUD-funded rental assistance for Jackson and surrounding counties. Open waiting lists: Cade Courtyard (Jackson, 1-2 BR), Hickman Heights (Jackson), Leonard Court (Jackson, 1-4 BR). Head of household 21+; HUD income limits; background check. Submit one application only. Housing. Free to apply. https://mrhavi.myhousing.com/

### Gulfport

**SeeClickFix Service Requests (Gulfport)** — City of Gulfport. Report potholes, sidewalk damage, drainage, traffic signs, graffiti, park maintenance, sanitation, code compliance online; emergencies 911. Utilities. Free. https://mygulfport.us/seeclickfix/

**Gulfport Public Library** — Harrison County Library System. 1708 25th Avenue, Gulfport, MS 39501. 228-871-7171. Free public library access. Education. Free. http://harrison.lib.ms.us/libraries/gulfport-public-library/

### Hattiesburg

**Hub City Transit (Hattiesburg)** — City of Hattiesburg. Fixed-route buses and ADA paratransit. Weekdays only, Monday-Friday 6 a.m.-6:30 p.m. 1001 S. Tipton St, Hattiesburg. +1-601-545-3356. Transportation. Fare required. https://hubcitytransit.com/

## County

### Hinds

**Jackson/Hinds Library Card** — Free for Hinds County residents with picture ID or similar residency proof; children and teens need a parent signature; nonresident fees posted. Education. Free for residents. https://www.jhlibrary.com/librarycards/

**Hinds County Government Services** — Official county website for county offices, departments, and online services including property tax resources. Money & Taxes. Free. https://hindscountyms.com/

### Harrison

**Coast Transit Authority (Harrison County)** — Fixed-route and demand-response buses plus the Beachcomber route for the Mississippi Gulf Coast, including Gulfport and Biloxi. Transportation. Fare required. https://coasttransit.com/

### DeSoto

**North Mississippi Rural Legal Services — Free Civil Legal Help (North Mississippi)** — Free civil representation for low-income people in 39 northern Mississippi counties including DeSoto. Online intake suspended; call 1-800-898-8731, Mon-Thu 9:30-3:30. Legal Aid. Free. https://nmrls.com/

**Area Agency on Aging Services (DeSoto County)** — North Delta Planning and Development District. For adults 60+: adult day care, caregiver support, homemaker services, transportation, ombudsman, elder-rights protection, and SHIP Medicare counseling. 662-561-4100. Seniors. Free. https://www.desotocountyms.gov/876/Area-Agency-on-Aging

### Forrest

**Mississippi Center for Legal Services — Free Civil Legal Help (South Mississippi)** — Free civil legal assistance for eligible low-income people in 43 central/southern Mississippi counties including Forrest, Lamar, and Harrison. Online intake suspended; call intake hotline 1-800-519-2915. Legal Aid. Free. https://mscenterforlegalservices.org/apply-for-help/

## State

**SNAP — Food Assistance (Mississippi)** — MDHS. Monthly EBT food benefits. Apply via Access MS; client services 800-948-3050. Food. Free. https://www.access.ms.gov

**TANF — Cash Assistance for Needy Families (Mississippi)** — MDHS. Temporary cash aid for needy families with a child under 18; work/school requirements apply. Apply via Access MS; 800-948-3050; hearings 601-359-4921. Money & Taxes. Free. https://www.access.ms.gov

**LIHEAP — Heating and Cooling Bill Assistance (Mississippi)** — STATUS: limited funding; available in all 82 counties while funds last. MDHS Community Services. 1-800-421-0762. Utilities. Free. http://www.mdhs.ms.gov/community/liheap/

**WIC — Women, Infants, and Children (Mississippi)** — MSDH. Supplemental foods, nutrition education, breastfeeding support for qualifying pregnant/breastfeeding/postpartum women, infants, children under five. Proof of income, residence, identity. 1-800-338-6747. Food. Free. https://msdh.ms.gov/msdhsite/index.cfm/41,25323,128,html

**Medicaid (Mississippi)** — Division of Medicaid. Health coverage for eligible Mississippians. Apply via Access MS or the Division of Medicaid. 800-421-2408; 601-359-6050. Healthcare. Free to apply. https://medicaid.ms.gov

**CHIP — Children's Health Insurance Program (Mississippi)** — MSDH. Coverage for uninsured children under 19 not eligible for Medicaid; possible copay for some higher-income families. Apply via Access MS. 1-800-884-3222. Healthcare. Free to apply. https://www.access.ms.gov

**Unemployment Insurance (Mississippi)** — MDES. Weekly benefits for workers who lost jobs through no fault of their own. Requires MDES registration, 18-month work history, current contact details, driver license/state ID, Social Security card. 601-493-9427. Employment. Free. http://mdes.ms.gov/unemployment-claims/

**Child Care Payment Program (Mississippi)** — STATUS: currently accepting applications only for six exception groups; other families may join the waitlist/email process. MDHS. Helps eligible families pay for child care; a parent copay may apply. 800-877-7882. Family & Childcare. Free to apply; copay may apply. http://www.mdhs.ms.gov/eccd/providers/

**Vocational Rehabilitation (Mississippi)** — MDRS. Individualized employment services, counseling, assistive technology, transition, education, training, assessment. Disability. Free. http://www.mdrs.ms.gov/vocational-rehabilitation

**Aging and Adult Services (Mississippi)** — MDHS. For adults 60+ and vulnerable adults: in-home services, caregiver support, adult day care, ombudsman, elder protection. 601-359-4915; abuse hotline 844-437-6282. Seniors. Free. http://www.mdhs.ms.gov/contact/

**Driver Services — Licenses and IDs (Mississippi)** — DPS Driver Service Bureau. Licenses, permits, state IDs, renewals, reinstatements. Transportation. Fees vary. https://www.driverservicebureau.dps.ms.gov/

**Business Formation and Filing (Mississippi Secretary of State)** — Online business formation, foreign registrations, annual reports, amendments, registered-agent changes, certificates. 601-359-1633. Small Business. Filing fees vary. https://www.sos.ms.gov/business-services/business-faqs

**State Financial Aid for College (Mississippi)** — Office of State Financial Aid. State-funded grants, scholarships, loan-repayment programs; apply via MAAPP. Education. Free to apply. https://www.msfinancialaid.org/

**Free E-File for State Taxes (Mississippi)** — DOR. Free/reduced-fee e-file options for Mississippi individual income tax. Refund status via TAP or 601-923-7801; general 601-923-7700. Money & Taxes. Free or reduced-fee options. http://www.dor.ms.gov/individual/e-file-information

**Child Support Services (Mississippi)** — MDHS. Call the child support call center at 877-882-4916 for case questions and help getting started. Family & Childcare. Free. http://www.mdhs.ms.gov/contact/

**Mississippi Veterans Affairs** — Mississippi Veterans Affairs Board. Benefits and services for Mississippi veterans and families. 1-800-698-2411. Veterans. Free. https://www.msva.ms.gov/

**Mississippi Free Legal Answers — Virtual Legal Clinic** — ABA. No-cost online civil legal advice for qualifying Mississippians: family, housing, consumer, employment, disability, education, civil rights. Legal Aid. Free. https://ms.freelegalanswers.org/

**WIN Job Centers (Mississippi)** — MDES. Free job-search help, resume/interview workshops, training referrals. Jackson (Hinds CC Academic & Technical Center, 3925 Sunset Dr), Gulfport (10162 Southpark Dr), Hattiesburg (1911 Arcadia St). Employment. Free. http://mdes.ms.gov/win-job-centers/

## Federal

**HUD Housing Counseling** — HUD. Help obtaining, sustaining, and retaining homes through HUD-participating agencies and certified counselors: foreclosure/eviction help, homebuying, rental, credit, reverse mortgage. 800-569-4287 (TTY 202-708-1455). Housing. Foreclosure/eviction/homeless counseling always free. http://www.hud.gov/stat/sfh/housing-counseling

**Social Security Benefits and Services** — SSA. Apply for retirement, disability (SSDI/SSI), survivors, and Medicare benefits; estimate benefits; check application/appeal status; replace a card. Seniors. Free. https://www.ssa.gov/

**VA Gulf Coast Veterans Health Care System (Biloxi)** — VA. Biloxi VA Medical Center, 400 Veterans Avenue, Biloxi, MS 39531. Primary care, behavioral health, inpatient, specialty, long-term care for enrolled veterans. 228-523-5000; toll-free 800-296-8872. Veterans. Free for enrolled veterans. http://va.gov/gulf-coast-health-care/contact-us

**Lifeline — Discounted Phone and Internet** — FCC/USAC. Up to $9.25/month toward phone or internet service ($34.25 on Tribal lands). Income at/below 135% FPL or participation in SNAP, Medicaid, or other federal programs. Utilities. Benefit applied to service. https://www.usac.org/lifeline/

**SBA Mississippi District Office — Small Business Help** — SBA. Funding programs, counseling, federal contracting certifications, disaster recovery. Jackson (210 E. Capitol St., Suite 900) and Gulfport (Hancock Bank Plaza, 2510 14th St., Suite 103). Small Business. Free. https://www.sba.gov/ms
