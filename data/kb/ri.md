# Rhode Island — GovServ Guide (verified 2026-09-19)

## Metro

### Providence / Providence County

**PVD311 — Providence Service Requests.** Providence's 311 system for submitting non-emergency service requests to the city, via the PVD311 portal, app, or phone. [Submit a PVD311 service request](https://311.providenceri.gov/)

**Providence Housing Authority.** Manages 2,606 public housing units and 2,432 Housing Choice Vouchers, plus 8 AMETEK affordable housing developments, serving low-income Providence families. [Providence Housing Authority](https://www.provhousing.org/)

**Providence Public Library.** Open to all, with free programs, books and media, and a computer lab with public wifi access. [Providence Public Library](https://www.provlib.org/)

### Warwick / Kent County

**Warwick Public Library.** Free library services including 10,000+ free online video courses in multiple languages and lending of nontraditional items such as craft tools, kitchen appliances, health devices, home improvement tools, and learning kits. [Warwick Public Library](https://warwicklibrary.org/)

**Warwick Housing Authority — Housing Choice Vouchers.** Warwick participates in Rhode Island's centralized Housing Choice Voucher waitlist, per HUD's Rhode Island Resource Guide. Check the guide for how centralized applications work. [HUD RI Resource Guide — centralized voucher information](https://HUD.Gov/sites/dfiles/State/documents/RI-Resource-Guide.pdf)

### Cranston / Providence County

**Cranston Public Library.** Free movies, television shows, and music streaming with a library card, research databases, on-demand K-12 tutoring through Tutor.com, and local history collections. Central Library: 140 Sockanosset Cross Rd, Cranston, RI 02920. Hours: Mon-Wed 9 AM-8 PM; Thu-Sat 9 AM-5 PM. 401-943-9080 [Cranston Public Library](https://www.cranstonlibrary.org/)

### Pawtucket / Providence County

**City of Pawtucket — City Services.** Official city portal for Pawtucket municipal services, departments, news, and events, under Mayor Donald R. Grebien. City Hall: 137 Roosevelt Avenue, Pawtucket, RI 02860. 401-728-0500 [City of Pawtucket](https://www.pawtucketri.gov/)

**Pawtucket Housing Authority.** Administers Housing Choice Vouchers and public housing in Pawtucket; dedicated to providing quality housing to low-income residents. 401-725-9116 [Pawtucket Housing Authority](https://www.pawthousing.org/)

**Pawtucket Public Library.** Library catalog, ebooks and audiobooks, mobile printing, a bookmobile schedule, and a passport acceptance office. Status note: the library posted that it will be closed Saturday 9/19 and Monday 9/21 — check the site for reopening information. [Pawtucket Public Library](https://pawtucketlibrary.org/)

**Blackstone Valley Community Health Care.** Federally qualified community health center accepting new primary-care patients, with 7 locations including Pawtucket and Central Falls. Services include family medicine, pediatrics, dental, optometry, behavioral health, OB/GYN, and pharmacy; walk-in Express Health offers same-day sick visits in Central Falls. [Blackstone Valley Community Health Care](https://www.bvchc.org/)

### East Providence / Providence County

**East Providence Housing Authority.** Serves eligible low-income families, older adults, and people with disabilities through public housing and the Housing Choice Voucher program. The authority owns and manages 419 public housing units and administers 445 vouchers; residents generally pay 30% of adjusted income toward rent. [East Providence Housing Authority](https://www.ephousing.org/)

**City of East Providence — City Services.** Official city portal for East Providence municipal services, departments, and community information. [City of East Providence](https://www.eastprovidenceri.gov/)

## County

**Community Action Partnership of Providence County (CAPP).** LIHEAP heating assistance and weatherization services for eligible Providence County residents; residents must be approved for LIHEAP to access weatherization. Office: 518 Hartford Avenue, Providence. 401-273-2000 [CAPP LIHEAP application portal](https://cappri.org/programs/low-income-home-energy-assistance-liheap)

**Westbay Community Action — Kent County.** Community action agency serving Kent County since 1966: heating assistance, childcare, emergency and supplemental food, senior nutrition programs, and case-management services to help families build self-sufficiency. 401-732-4660 [Westbay programs and services](https://www.westbaycap.org/)

## State

**Rhode Island Department of Human Services (DHS) — Benefits Hub.** Central entry point for Rhode Island benefit programs including SNAP, RI Works cash assistance, Medicaid, child care assistance, and energy assistance. Applications can be submitted online through the HealthyRhode customer portal. 1-855-697-4347 [Apply for benefits on HealthyRhode](https://healthyrhode.ri.gov/)

**SNAP — Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program.** Monthly food benefits for eligible low-income households. A state update dated 2026-02-17 describes narrower noncitizen eligibility rules, and new ABAWD work requirements began 2026-03-01 for some adults ages 18-64, requiring 80 hours of work per month with listed exemptions. 1-855-697-4347 1-800-745-5555 [Apply for benefits on HealthyRhode](https://healthyrhode.ri.gov/)

**RI Works — Cash Assistance.** Temporary cash assistance for families with dependent children or pregnant individuals, with work requirements and support services to help families reach self-sufficiency. 1-855-697-4347 1-800-745-5555 [RI Works eligibility and how to apply](https://dhs.ri.gov/programs-and-services/ri-works-program/eligibility-how-apply?language=en)

**HealthyRhode — Health Coverage and Benefits Portal.** The state's customer portal for applying for health coverage, including Medicaid, and DHS benefit programs. Also available as the HealthyRhode mobile app. [Apply on HealthyRhode](https://healthyrhode.ri.gov/)

**HealthSource RI — Health Insurance Marketplace.** Rhode Island's official health insurance marketplace for individuals, families, and small businesses. Losing Medicaid is a qualifying life event with a 60-day enrollment window before and after the event. [Shop and enroll at HealthSource RI](https://www.healthsourceri.com/)

**WIC — Women, Infants, and Children.** Nutrition support at no cost for pregnant people, breastfeeding people, postpartum people up to six months after delivery, and caregivers of children under age five, subject to program guidelines. 401-222-4642 401-222-4623 [WIC program page](https://health.ri.gov/wic)

**LIHEAP — Low Income Home Energy Assistance.** Heating assistance for eligible households, administered through local Community Action Program (CAP) agencies; new applicants generally apply in person at their local CAP agency. The application period closed on 2026-04-15 and is expected to reopen on 2026-10-01. Households must be at or below 60% of Rhode Island median income. [LIHEAP program page](https://dhs.ri.gov/programs-and-services/energy-assistance-programs-heating/low-income-home-energy-assistance-program?language=en)

**CCAP — Child Care Assistance Program.** Helps eligible families pay for child care so parents can focus on work, training, or education. The income limit for CCAP eligibility was raised to 261% of the federal poverty level for families applying starting January 1, 2025, and DHS processes CCAP applications within 30 days. 1-855-697-4347 [Apply for CCAP on HealthyRhode](https://healthyrhode.ri.gov/)

**Unemployment Insurance.** Temporary income support for workers who lose their jobs through no fault of their own and meet the state's wage and base-period requirements. [Unemployment Insurance information](https://www.dlt.ri.gov/individuals/unemployment-insurance)

**RIHousing — First-Time Homebuyer Loans.** First-time homebuyer mortgage loans and down-payment assistance from the state's housing finance agency. First-time RIHousing borrowers must complete homebuyer education before closing. Note: the RI REALTORS Homebuyer Grant is closed because all funds are reserved. [RIHousing homebuyer programs](https://www.rihousing.com/homebuyers/)

**RIHousing — Homebuyer Education.** Homebuyer education courses for prospective buyers. Completion is required before closing for first-time RIHousing borrowers. [RIHousing homebuyer education](https://www.rihousing.com/homebuyers/)

**RISLA — Student Loans.** Nonprofit education lender. RI Advantage is an exclusive student-loan interest-rate discount for any student attending a college or university in Rhode Island and for Rhode Island resident students attending college anywhere in the country. Borrower benefits include income-based repayment options and payment forbearance. [RISLA student loan options](https://www.risla.com/student-loans)

**DMV — Driver's License Renewal.** Renew Rhode Island driver's licenses and state identification cards through the Division of Motor Vehicles, online or in person. [RI DMV license services](https://www.dmv.ri.gov/)

**DMV — Vehicle Registration Renewal.** Renew vehicle registrations online through the RIDMV online services portal. [Renew registration online](https://ridmvservices.ri.gov/)

**Business Registration — Secretary of State.** Register business entities and file business documents with the Rhode Island Secretary of State's business services portal. [Register a business](https://business.sos.ri.gov/corp)

**Vocational Rehabilitation (ORS).** Employment services for people with disabilities: counseling, training, education support, and job-placement assistance through the Office of Rehabilitation Services. [ORS vocational rehabilitation](https://ors.ri.gov/)

**Services for the Blind and Visually Impaired (ORS).** Specialized services for Rhode Islanders who are blind or visually impaired, administered by the Office of Rehabilitation Services. [ORS services for the blind and visually impaired](https://ors.ri.gov/)

**Assistive Technology Access (ORS).** Access to assistive technology devices and services that help people with disabilities live and work more independently, coordinated through the Office of Rehabilitation Services. [ORS assistive technology](https://ors.ri.gov/)

**Office of Healthy Aging — Aging & Disability Resource Center.** State agency connecting older adults, adults with disabilities, and caregivers to services, information, and referrals. Public comments on the draft 2026-2029 State Plan on Aging are accepted through 4:00 PM on 2026-09-30. [Office of Healthy Aging](https://oha.ri.gov/)

**Rhode Island Legal Services.** Free civil legal help for low-income Rhode Islanders, including housing, family, public benefits, and consumer matters. 401-274-2652 800-662-5034 [Apply for legal help](https://www.rils.org/)

**Disability Rights Rhode Island.** Protection and advocacy for the legal rights of people with disabilities in Rhode Island, including help with discrimination, benefits, and access issues. 401-831-3150 [Contact Disability Rights Rhode Island](https://drri.org/)

**RI 211 — United Way.** Free statewide helpline connecting Rhode Islanders to food, housing, employment training, utility help, and other community services, by phone, online chat, and the web. [Find help through RI 211](https://www.uwri.org/)

**Rhode Island Community Food Bank — Food Finder.** Find free food assistance through the Food Bank's Food Finder and Get Assistance tools, which connect Rhode Islanders with member agencies and pantries statewide. [Find food assistance](https://rifoodbank.org/)

**RIVETS — RI Office of Veterans Services.** The state's coordination center for veterans, active-duty service members, National Guard and Reserve members, and their families — help with benefits, referrals, and veteran services. [RI Office of Veterans Services](https://vets.ri.gov/)

**RIPTA — Public Transit.** Statewide public bus service across Rhode Island, with current service alerts and route information posted on the agency website. [RIPTA routes and fares](https://www.ripta.com/)

**RIPTA Reduced Fare Bus Pass.** Reduced-fare bus passes for low-income seniors age 65 and over and low-income persons with disabilities. Qualifying riders receive a photo ID pass valid for two years with a $10 processing fee. Seniors and riders with disabilities also receive half-fare boarding during off-peak hours, and a Medicare card can be used to board. [Reduced fare bus pass program information](https://vets.ri.gov/find-your-benefits/find-your-benefits/bus-pass-program-individuals-disabilities)

**SBA Rhode Island District Office.** Help with SBA funding programs, counseling, federal contracting certifications, and disaster recovery. The Rhode Island District Office serves the entire state; the Providence office is at 380 Westminster St., Room 511, open Monday-Friday 8:00 AM-4:30 PM, closed on federal holidays. 401-528-4561 [SBA Rhode Island District Office](https://www.sba.gov/district/rhode-island)

## Federal

**VA Providence Healthcare System.** VA health care for eligible veterans at the Providence VA Medical Center, 830 Chalkstone Avenue, Providence, plus affiliated clinics. [VA Providence Healthcare System](https://www.va.gov/providence-health-care/)

**Lifeline — Discounted Phone and Internet.** Federal discount on monthly phone or broadband service for eligible households: income at or below 135% of the federal poverty guidelines, or participation in programs such as SNAP or Medicaid. [Lifeline program information](https://www.usac.org/lifeline/)

**HUD Housing Counseling.** Free or low-cost counseling from HUD-approved agencies on buying a home, renting, avoiding foreclosure, and homelessness prevention. [Find a HUD-approved housing counselor](https://www.hud.gov/counseling)

**FAFSA — Free Application for Federal Student Aid.** The free application students complete to access federal grants, loans, and work-study for college or career school. [Complete the FAFSA](https://studentaid.gov/)

**Housing Choice Vouchers — RI Centralized Waitlist.** Rhode Island's participating housing authorities, including Warwick, use a centralized application process for the Housing Choice Voucher program, as described in HUD's Rhode Island Resource Guide. [HUD RI Resource Guide — centralized voucher information](https://HUD.Gov/sites/dfiles/State/documents/RI-Resource-Guide.pdf)
