# Arkansas Government Services — GovServ Knowledge Base

38 verified services. Verified 2026-09-19.

## Metro

### Little Rock

**Little Rock 311 — City Services** — Report potholes, broken streetlights, and other non-emergency city issues. Eligibility: anyone reporting a Little Rock city issue. Rolling — dial 3-1-1, use the free app, or the online dashboard. Phone: 311 / 501-371-4510. Apply: https://littlerock.gov/contact-us/

**Rock Region METRO — Little Rock Transit** — Public transit for the Little Rock metro: fixed-route buses with route selection, trip planning, schedules, fares/passes, and real-time arrivals. Eligibility: open to public. Rolling. Apply: https://rrmetro.org/

### Fayetteville

**Fayetteville Public Library** — Free public library: books, events for adults and youth, catalog resources. Eligibility: open to public; card needed to borrow. Rolling. Apply: https://faylib.org/

**Ozark Regional Transit** — Safe, reliable, affordable public transit for the four-county Northwest Arkansas region (including Fayetteville and Springdale), connecting people to jobs, schools, and health care. Eligibility: open to public. Rolling. Apply: https://www.ozark.org/

### Fort Smith

**Fort Smith Transit — Fixed-Route Bus** — City buses Mon–Sat 7am–6pm (last transfer 5pm); $1.25 regular, 50¢ reduced (65+, certified disabled, Medicare), 75¢ UAFS students; free for veterans with service-connected disabilities, children 7 and under, and transfers; monthly passes $35. Bike racks and free Wi-Fi on board. Eligibility: open to public; Reduced Fare Card needed for reduced fares. Rolling. Phone: 783-6464 (Fort Smith area). Apply: https://www.fortsmithar.gov/resident-services/transportation-and-mobility/transit-overview-bus-bike-walk-or-ride/rider-guide-tips

**Fort Smith Public Library** — Free library system: Main library plus Dallas, Miller, and Windsor branches, programs for all ages. Eligibility: open to public; card needed to borrow. Rolling. Apply: https://fortsmithlibrary.org/

### Springdale

**Springdale Public Library** — Free "Library in the Park": books, digital resources (Libby, Hoopla, Kanopy, NYT), technology, programs. Cards free for anyone living, working, owning property, or attending school in Springdale or Washington County (photo ID + proof of address). Open Mon–Thu 9am–8pm, Fri–Sat 9am–6pm, Sun 1pm–5pm. Rolling. Phone: 479-750-8180. Apply: https://springdalelibrary.org/

### Jonesboro

**GOJO — Jonesboro Public Transit** — City bus system (formerly JET/JETS) from the Regional Transfer Center at Caraway Rd & Matthews Ave; weekday routes plus Saturday Green Route 9:27am–4pm, no Sunday service. $1.25 base fare; reduced fares available. Paratransit for eligible riders; A-State students ride free with current school ID. Eligibility: open to public. Rolling. Phone: 870-935-5387. Apply: https://jonesboroar.gov/724/Saturday-Map-Schedule

## County

### Pulaski County

**Central Arkansas Library System** — Free public library system serving Pulaski and Perry Counties: books, e-books, public computers, events, and the Butler Center for Arkansas Studies. Eligibility: open to public; card needed to borrow. Rolling. Apply: https://cals.org/

**Pulaski County Health Unit** — Local public-health services: contagious-disease testing, child screenings, immunizations, wellness education. Eligibility: open to public. Rolling — call ahead for clinic hours. Apply: https://www.healthy.arkansas.gov/health-units/detail/pulaski-county-health-unit-central-little-rock

**Metropolitan Housing Alliance — Public Housing & Vouchers** — Public housing and Housing Choice Vouchers for low-income residents in the Little Rock area. Eligibility: low-income household; income limits and waitlist status vary. Rolling — public-housing waitlist was closed; check current status. Apply: https://www.metropolitanhousingalliance.org

## State

**SNAP — Arkansas** — Helps households buy food; eligibility considers household size, income, and assets. Apply through Access Arkansas. Rolling. Apply: https://access.arkansas.gov/Learn/Home

**Transitional Employment Assistance (TEA) — Arkansas** — Short-term help for limited-income households with a child under 18: can include cash, job training, childcare, and transportation. Rolling. Apply: https://access.arkansas.gov/Learn/Home

**Arkansas Medicaid** — Health coverage for eligible adults, children, pregnant women, and people with disabilities. Online applications cover SNAP, TEA, Medicaid, ARKids, TEFRA, and other categories. Rolling. Phone: 1-800-482-8988 (unverified). Apply: https://humanservices.arkansas.gov/apply-for-services/

**ARKids First** — Health coverage for Arkansas children: preventive, medical, eye, and dental care. ARKids A has no charge; ARKids B has some copayments. Rolling. Phone: 1-888-474-8275 (Mon–Fri, 8am–4:30pm). Apply: https://humanservices.arkansas.gov/divisions-shared-services/medical-services/healthcare-programs/arkids/

**Arkansas WIC (Women, Infants, and Children)** — Nutrition benefits and education for pregnant/breastfeeding/postpartum women, infants, and children under 5. Arkansas residency + income guidelines + nutritional-risk screening; interpreters available. Rolling — call to schedule. Phone: 501-661-2508 / 1-800-235-0002. Apply: https://healthy.arkansas.gov/programs-services/community-family-child-health/wic-women-infants-children/

**Arkansas LIHEAP** — Heating/cooling bill help delivered through community organizations in all 75 counties; no application fee. Typically first-come in winter (Jan–Apr 30) and summer (Jul–Sep 30) periods. Eligibility: income guidelines apply — see your county organization. Rolling — check current dates. Apply: https://adeq.state.ar.us/energy/assistance/liheap.aspx

**Arkansas Unemployment Insurance** — Temporary wage replacement for workers who lost jobs through no fault of their own. File through EZARC (Mon–Fri 6am–6pm, Sun 7am–6pm); Arkansas LAUNCH account, identity proof, and weekly certification required. Rolling — file promptly after job loss. Phone: 1-844-908-2178 (Arkline 501-907-2590). Apply: https://dws.arkansas.gov/workforce-services/unemployment/

**Arkansas Academic Challenge Scholarship** — Lottery-funded college scholarship for Arkansas residents. Annual July 1 deadline; requires residency, education/test criteria, FAFSA, and an eligible institution. Phone/Apply info: https://sams.adhe.edu/Scholarship/Details/ACST

**Arkansas Rehabilitation Services — Vocational Rehabilitation** — Helps people with disabilities get or keep jobs: referral, counselor application, assessment, eligibility decision within 60 days, employment plan within 90 days. Rolling. Phone: 1-800-330-0632 / 501-296-1600 (metro offices: Little Rock 501-686-2800, North Little Rock 501-833-1490, Fayetteville 479-582-1286, Fort Smith 479-755-3300, Jonesboro 870-972-0025). Apply: https://dws.arkansas.gov/ar-rehabilitation-services/field-services/vocational-rehabilitation-vr-process-at-a-glance/

**Arkansas Department of Veterans Affairs** — Advocacy and access to state/federal veterans benefits, long-term nursing care, burial honors; veteran career services at Arkansas Workforce Centers. Rolling. Apply: https://www.veterans.arkansas.gov

**Legal Aid of Arkansas** — Free legal help for low-income Arkansans (housing, family, consumer, benefits). Apply online; helpline Mon–Thu 9–11am and 1–3pm, Tue 5:15–7:15pm. Rolling. Phone: 1-800-952-9243 (fair housing 1-870-338-9834). Apply: https://arlegalaid.org/contact-us/apply-for-legal-aid.html

**Starting a Business — Arkansas Secretary of State** — Official steps: guidance booklet, FAQ, name search, online/paper entity filing, then EIN. Posted corporation/LLC processing estimate: 3–5 business days. Rolling. Apply: https://www.sos.arkansas.gov/business-commercial-services-bcs/for-new-business

**Revenue Office Appointments — Arkansas DFA** — Book online appointments for vehicle registration, driver-license renewal, and other Revenue Office services; walk-ins remain. Rolling. Apply: http://dfa.arkansas.gov/news/eliminate-waiting-book-a-revenue-office-appointment/

**ADFA Homeownership Programs** — State home loans through participating lenders: StartSmart, Move-Up, and down-payment assistance up to $15,000. Eligibility: income, purchase-price, and first-time-buyer rules vary. Rolling. Apply: https://adfa.arkansas.gov/homeloans

**Disability Rights Arkansas** — Free, confidential advocacy, legal help, and information for Arkansans with disabilities; intake response stated within 48 hours. Rolling. Phone: 800-482-1174. Apply: https://disabilityrightsar.org/how-to-get-help/

**Arkansas Child Care Assistance (CCDF)** — Help paying for child care for children birth through 12. Parents must work, attend school, or train totaling at least 30 hours/week; income checked on a sliding-fee chart. Rolling. Phone: 501-682-8947 / 1-800-322-8176. Apply: https://dese.ade.arkansas.gov/Offices/office-of-early-childhood/help-paying-for-child-care

**Arkansas Workforce Centers** — Free job-search and employment services at centers statewide (Little Rock, North Little Rock, Fayetteville, Fort Smith, Jonesboro, and more). Rolling. Phone: Little Rock 501-682-0228, North Little Rock 501-376-4119, Fayetteville 479-521-5730, Fort Smith 479-783-0231, Jonesboro 870-935-5594. Apply: https://dws.arkansas.gov/workforce-services/arkansas-workforce-centers/

**Arkansas SHIIP — Free Medicare Counseling** — Unbiased Medicare counseling for seniors via the State Health Insurance Assistance Program at the Arkansas Insurance Department; also partners on Medicare fraud prevention. Rolling. Apply: https://insurance.arkansas.gov/site/assets/files/4058/medicare_protection_toolkit_02272026.pdf

**DHS County Offices — In-Person Assistance** — In-person help with SNAP, Medicaid, and TEA cases at DHS offices in every Arkansas county; DHS workers statewide can access your case. Rolling. Phone: 501-682-8375 / statewide 855-372-1084 / TDD 501-682-8820. Apply: https://humanservices.arkansas.gov/contact-us/county-offices-map/

## Federal

**Social Security — Retirement, Disability & SSI** — Federal retirement, disability, survivor, and Medicare enrollment services. Apply online, track applications and appeals, replace Social Security cards. Eligibility varies by benefit. Rolling. Apply: https://www.ssa.gov

**VA Health Care** — Health care for Arkansas veterans: primary care, specialists, mental health, prescriptions, and more; priority groups set eligibility and costs. Rolling. Apply: https://www.va.gov/health-care

**FAFSA — Federal Student Aid** — Free Application for Federal Student Aid unlocks grants, loans, and work-study; 2026–27 form available now. Rolling — school/state deadlines vary; file early. Apply: https://studentaid.gov

**Lifeline — Phone & Internet Discount** — Federal discount up to $9.25/month on qualifying phone/internet service (one per household). Qualify via income (≤135% FPL) or SNAP, Medicaid, SSI, housing assistance, or Veterans Pension. Rolling. Apply: https://www.fcc.gov/lifeline-consumers

**HUD-Approved Housing Counseling** — Free or low-cost housing counseling: buying a home, avoiding foreclosure, rental counseling. Rolling. Apply: https://www.hud.gov/program_offices/housing/sfh/hcc

**Small Business Administration — Counseling & Loans** — Federal small-business help: counseling, SBA-backed loans, contracting expertise, disaster recovery. Rolling. Apply: https://www.sba.gov

**USAJOBS — Federal Employment** — Official federal job site: search openings, build a profile, set alerts, apply. No application or testing fees. Rolling — apply before each announcement's closing date. Apply: https://www.usajobs.gov

**988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** — Free, confidential crisis support for mental-health struggles, emotional distress, or substance-use concerns. Call or text 988, 24/7/365. Rolling. Apply: https://988lifeline.org
