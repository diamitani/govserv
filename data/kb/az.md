## State

### Statewide

#### Nutrition Assistance (SNAP)

Arizona's SNAP program: monthly food benefits for low-income households to buy groceries.

**Who qualifies:** Arizona residents with limited income and resources; Varies — see program page

**Contact:** 1-855-432-7587

**Apply:** [Apply online via Health-e-Arizona Plus](https://healthearizonaplus.gov)

#### AHCCCS Health Insurance (Arizona Medicaid)

Arizona's Medicaid program: low-cost or free health coverage, including long-term care (ALTCS) for eligible residents.

**Who qualifies:** Arizona residents with limited income; Varies — see program page

**Contact:** 1-888-621-6880

**Apply:** [How to apply for AHCCCS](https://azahcccs.gov/members/getcovered/apply.html)

#### Arizona WIC — Women, Infants, and Children

Nutrition support, breastfeeding help, and healthy foods for eligible women, infants, and children.

**Who qualifies:** Varies — see program page

**Contact:** 1-800-252-5942

**Apply:** [Arizona WIC program page](https://www.azdhs.gov/prevention/azwic/)

#### Cash Assistance (TANF)

Temporary cash assistance for low-income families with children (Arizona's TANF program).

**Who qualifies:** Low-income families with dependent children; Varies — see program page

**Contact:** 1-855-432-7587

**Apply:** [Cash Assistance program page](https://des.az.gov/ca)

#### Vocational Rehabilitation (VR)

Employment services, counseling, and training for Arizonans whose disability affects their ability to work.

**Who qualifies:** A disability that affects the ability to work; Need for VR services to prepare for, obtain, or keep employment

**Contact:** 1-800-563-1221

**Apply:** [Vocational Rehabilitation program page](https://des.az.gov/services/employment/rehabilitation-services/vocational-rehabilitation-vr)

#### Division of Developmental Disabilities (DDD)

Long-term services and supports for Arizonans with developmental disabilities and their families.

**Who qualifies:** Arizona residents with a developmental disability; Varies — see program page

**Contact:** 1-844-770-9500

**Apply:** [Division of Developmental Disabilities](https://des.az.gov/ddd)

#### Child Care Assistance

Help paying for child care for eligible working families in Arizona.

**Who qualifies:** Working families with limited income; Varies — see program page

**Contact:** 1-833-947-6396 · 1-800-308-9000

**Apply:** [Child Care Assistance program page](https://des.az.gov/services/child-and-family/child-care)

#### AZ Job Connection

Free statewide job board: search job openings, post a resume, and find training resources.

**Who qualifies:** Open to all job seekers

**Contact:** 602-542-2460

**Apply:** [AZ Job Connection](https://azjobconnection.gov/)

#### AZ MVD Now — Driver and Vehicle Services

Online driver licenses and IDs, vehicle registration, plates, and title services.

**Who qualifies:** Arizona drivers and vehicle owners; Varies by transaction — see program page

**Contact:** https://azmvdnow.gov/

**Apply:** [AZ MVD Now online services](https://azmvdnow.gov/)

#### Veteran Benefits Counselors

Free help from accredited counselors filing VA claims and accessing earned veterans benefits.

**Who qualifies:** Veterans, service members, and their families

**Contact:** 602-535-1215

**Apply:** [Veteran Benefits Counselors](https://dvs.az.gov/services/veteran-benefits-counselors-vbcs)

Independent help resolving problems with DES programs or services.

**Who qualifies:** Anyone with an unresolved DES issue

**Contact:** https://des.az.gov/how-do-i/request-assistance-ombudsman

**Apply:** [Request Assistance (Ombudsman)](https://des.az.gov/how-do-i/request-assistance-ombudsman)
