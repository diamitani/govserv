## Metro

### Las Vegas

**RTC of Southern Nevada — Transit Fares & Paratransit** — RTC of Southern Nevada runs fixed-route bus service across Las Vegas and the valley: $2 regular and $1 reduced single rides on residential routes. Paratransit provides shared-ride door-to-door service for riders functionally unable to use fixed routes ($3 base cash fare); paratransit ID holders ride fixed and express routes free. Eligibility: Anyone can ride fixed-route buses (regular fare); Reduced fares: active-duty military and veterans, youth ages 6–17, K–12 students, seniors 60+, people with disabilities, Medicare-eligible riders and mobility-trained riders; Paratransit: people functionally unable to use fixed-route service (separate eligibility process) Rolling — Fares apply year-round; apply for reduced-fare or paratransit eligibility any time. Apply: https://www.rtcsnv.com/ways-to-travel/fares-passes/?intent=info&agency_id=RTCSNV Cost: $2 regular / $1 reduced single ride (residential routes); paratransit $3 base cash fare

## County

### Clark

**Clark County Social Service — Rental & Utility Assistance** — Clark County Social Service assists residents facing financial hardship with rent/housing costs, utilities and related expenses. Core programs: Eviction Prevention (up to 12 months of past-due rent plus 3 months of future rent — one-time lifetime benefit) and Fixed Income Rental Assistance (up to 12 months of rent, plus a 13th month to relocate); both programs can also help with utilities. Apply online through the CHAP portal; decisions are typically reached within 30 days of assignment to a caseworker. Eligibility: Clark County resident facing financial hardship and lacking sufficient income or resources for rent/utilities; Eviction Prevention: behind on rent with a viable plan to pay future rent (no eviction filing required); Fixed Income Rental Assistance: behind on rent with a household member on fixed income (Social Security, SSI, VA pension/disability, pensions, permanent disability) Rolling — Apply any time via the CHAP portal; landlords are typically paid within 14 business days of approval. Phone: 702-455-4270 Apply: https://chap.clarkcountynv.gov/ Cost: Free

## State

**SNAP — Food Benefits (Nevada)** — The Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program helps low-income Nevada households buy groceries. Approved households receive an EBT card; benefits are processed within 30 days of application, or within 7 business days if the household qualifies for expedited service. Eligibility: Nevada resident; Low-income household; eligibility based on household size, income, expenses and citizenship status; Expedited service within 7 business days for households with little or no money Rolling — Apply any time; EBT card issued no later than 30 business days from the date the office received the application (7 business days if expedited).; Federal SNAP changes — Nevada Legal Services reports that under recent federal law changes, SNAP recipients aged 18-64 not meeting specific criteria may lose benefits as of May 1, 2026. Free legal help is available if you were denied or terminated — see Nevada Legal Services. Phone: 1-800-992-0900, 1-800-326-6888 Apply: https://dwss.nv.gov/SNAP/Food Cost: Free
**Nevada Medicaid — Health Coverage** — Nevada Medicaid provides free or low-cost health coverage to eligible low-income Nevadans, including families with children, pregnant people, older adults, and people with disabilities. Apply online through Access Nevada; Nevada Check-Up covers children in families with income too high for Medicaid. Eligibility: Nevada resident; U.S. citizen or eligible immigration status; Income at or below program limits (varies by eligibility group and household size); You can apply and receive Medicaid even if you already have insurance Rolling — Apply any time; online application takes about 45 minutes for a typical household. Phone: 1-800-992-0900, 1-800-326-6888, 855-768-5465 Apply: https://www.medicaid.nv.gov/ Cost: Free
**TANF — Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (Nevada)** — TANF provides temporary cash assistance and support services (child care, transportation) to low-income Nevada families with dependent children. Nevada administers five TANF benefit categories; federal rules include a 60-month lifetime time limit. Eligibility: Low-income Nevada family with dependent children (or relative caregivers caring for them); Must meet work participation, child support cooperation, immunization and school attendance requirements; 60-month federal lifetime limit on cash assistance Rolling — Apply any time via the Application for Assistance (online, mail, fax or in person at a DWSS district office). Phone: 1-800-992-0900, 1-800-326-6888 Apply: https://dwss.nv.gov/TANF/Financial_Help/ Cost: Free
**Energy Assistance Program (EAP / LIHEAP) — Nevada** — Nevada's Energy Assistance Program (funded by the federal LIHEAP block grant and the state Universal Energy Charge) helps eligible low-income households with annual heating and cooling costs. Eligibility: Nevada resident; At least partly responsible for home heating or cooling costs (paying a utility company, fuel supplier or landlord directly); Total gross monthly income of all household members may not exceed the program income chart (see the current EAP application for the chart) Rolling — Applications accepted during the program year; prior-year recipients may not reapply until approximately 11 months after they received their last benefit. Apply: https://dwss.nv.gov/Energy/2_Apply_for_Assistance Cost: Free

Weekly certifications required to keep receiving payments. Phone: (775) 684-0350, (702) 486-0350, (888) 890-8211, (800) 603-9671 Apply: https://detr.nv.gov/Page/Unemployment_Insurance_Benefits Cost: Free

**Nevada Housing Division — Homebuyer & Homeowner Programs** — The Nevada Housing Division helps Nevadans become and stay homeowners: the statewide Homebuyer Program (Home is Possible) offers low- and moderate-income buyers a fixed-rate 30-year loan with down payment and closing-cost assistance; the Mortgage Credit Certificate (MCC) gives first-time homebuyers and qualified veterans a federal income tax credit of up to 30% of mortgage interest paid each year; and the Foreclosure Mediation Program lets owner-occupants who receive a Notice of Default elect mediation within 30 days. Eligibility: Low- or moderate-income Nevada homebuyer (Home is Possible); First-time homebuyer or qualified veteran (Mortgage Credit Certificate); Owner-occupant served with a Notice of Default (Foreclosure Mediation Program — 30-day window to elect) Rolling — Homebuyer and MCC programs are available statewide on a rolling basis.; Foreclosure mediation — 30-day election — Homeowners of owner-occupied houses have 30 days after being served with a foreclosure notice to elect mediation. ## Federal

**Social Security — Retirement, Disability & SSI (Federal)** — Apply for Social Security retirement and spouse's benefits, disability benefits, SSI and Medicare through your personal my Social Security account. You can apply for retirement benefits up to 4 months before you want them to start; create or sign in with a Login.gov or ID.me credential. Eligibility: U.S. workers and qualifying family members; retirement, disability and SSI each have program-specific requirements Rolling — Apply online any time; retirement applications accepted up to 4 months before benefits start. Sign up for Medicare 3 months before your 65th birthday even if not retiring. Phone: 1-800-772-1213, 1-800-325-0778 Apply: https://www.ssa.gov/forms/ssa-1.html Cost: Free
**FAFSA — Federal Student Aid (Free Application)** — The Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) is the single application for federal grants, work-study and student loans for college or career school. The 2026–27 FAFSA is available now for attendance between July 1, 2026 and June 30, 2027; online forms are typically processed within days. Eligibility: U.S. citizens and eligible noncitizens enrolling in college or career school; dependency status determines whose information is required 2026–27 FAFSA federal deadline — The 2026–27 FAFSA covers attendance July 1, 2026 – June 30, 2027. State and school deadlines are often much earlier — check yours. Phone: 1-800-433-3243 Apply: https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa Cost: Free
**VA Health Care Enrollment (Federal)** — Enroll in VA health care — the nation's largest integrated health system — for comprehensive medical benefits including preventive, primary and specialty care, prescriptions, mental health care, home health and more. Most veterans qualify for cost-free care; some pay modest copays. Once enrolled, coverage is for life with no annual re-enrollment. Eligibility: Veterans who served in active military service and did not receive a dishonorable discharge; Minimum service requirements vary (generally 24 consecutive months for post-1980 enlistees, with exceptions); all veterans are encouraged to apply Rolling — Apply any time — online (about 30 minutes), by phone, by mail, or in person at a VA medical center. Phone: 1-877-222-8387 Apply: https://choose.va.gov/health/ Cost: Free to apply; most veterans qualify for cost-free care (some pay modest copays)
