# California — Government Services

Verified 2026-09-18 · 41 services
Every link below was fetched from an official page on 2026-09-18. Phone numbers appear as printed on those pages.

**Start here:** dial **2-1-1** anywhere in California for free, confidential help finding local services.

## Los Angeles County
- **211 LA County** — free service navigation across LA County — 1-800-339-6993 — [211la.org](https://211la.org/)
- **LA County DPSS** — CalWORKs, CalFresh, Medi-Cal, homeless and job services — [dpss.lacounty.gov](https://dpss.lacounty.gov/)
- **Metro LIFE** — free/discounted transit: unlimited free rides first 90 days, then 20 free rides a month — 866-827-8646 — [metro.net/riding/life](https://www.metro.net/riding/life/)

## San Francisco
- **211 Bay Area** — free, confidential, 24/7 referral service — dial **211** — [211bayarea.org](https://211bayarea.org/)
- **SFHSA** — food, cash, employment, and health assistance — (415) 557-5000 — [sfhsa.org](https://www.sfhsa.org/)
- **SF 311** — non-emergency city services, 24/7, 160+ languages — dial **3-1-1** — [sf311.org](https://sf311.org/)

## San Diego County
- **211 San Diego** — free information and referral service — dial **2-1-1** — [211sandiego.org](https://211sandiego.org/)
- **San Diego County HHSA** — health and social services for county residents — [HHSA](https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/sdc/hhsa.html)
- **San Diego Public Library** — free books, tech help, hotspots, programs — [sandiego.gov/public-library](https://www.sandiego.gov/public-library)

## Riverside County
- **211 Inland SoCal** — 24/7 helpline for Riverside and San Bernardino counties — dial **2-1-1** — [inlandsocaluw.org/211](http://inlandsocaluw.org/211)
- **Riverside County DPSS** — cash, food, health care, housing, and job services — [rivcodpss.org](https://www.rivcodpss.org/)

## Sacramento County
- **211 Sacramento** — coordinated information and referral — dial **2-1-1** — [211sacramento.org](https://211sacramento.org/)

## California Statewide

**Food & health**
- **CalFresh** — monthly food benefits on an EBT card — 1-877-847-3663 — [getcalfresh.org](https://www.getcalfresh.org)
- **WIC** — nutrition support for pregnancy, infants, and children under 5; 185% FPL limit — [CDPH WIC](https://www.cdph.ca.gov/Programs/CFH/DWICSN/Pages/AboutWIC/ProgramOverview.aspx)
- **Medi-Cal** — free or low-cost health coverage, apply year-round — (916) 552-9200 — [dhcs.ca.gov](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/how-to-get-the-care-you-need/)
- **Covered California** — health insurance marketplace; financial help with premiums — [coveredca.com](https://coveredca.com/)
- **CalWORKs** — temporary cash aid for families with children — [cdss.ca.gov](https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/cdss-programs/calworks/services)

**Money & taxes**
- **CalEITC** — refundable state tax credit; claim with Form FTB 3514, up to 4 years back — [ftb.ca.gov](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/personal/credits/california-earned-income-Tax-credit.html)

**Jobs & family**
- **Unemployment Insurance (EDD)** — weekly benefits after job loss — 1-800-300-5616 — [edd.ca.gov](https://edd.ca.gov/en/unemployment/step-2-apply)
- **State Disability Insurance (EDD)** — wage replacement for non-work-related illness/injury; file days 9–49 — 1-800-480-3287 — [edd.ca.gov](https://edd.ca.gov/en/disability/step-2-apply/)
- **Paid Family Leave (EDD)** — wage replacement to bond with a new child or care for an ill family member — 1-877-238-4373 — [edd.ca.gov](https://edd.ca.gov/en/disability/paid-family-leave/step-2-apply/)

**Education**
- **Cal Grant** — state financial aid, no repayment; priority deadline March 2 each year — (888) 224-7268 — [csac.ca.gov](http://www.csac.ca.gov/cal-grant)

**Housing & veterans**
- **CalHFA MyHome** — down-payment and closing-cost assistance for first-time buyers — [calhfa.ca.gov](http://www.calhfa.ca.gov/homebuyer/programs/myhome.htm)
- **CalVet Home Loans** — state home loans for eligible veterans — 866-653-2510 — [ca.gov](https://www.ca.gov/departments/163/services/64/)

**Seniors, disability & utilities**
- **CDA Senior Services** — meals, caregiver support, fall prevention, senior employment training — 800-510-2020 — [aging.ca.gov](https://aging.ca.gov/Programs_and_Services/)
- **IHSS** — in-home help for aged, blind, or disabled Medi-Cal recipients — [cdss.ca.gov](https://cdss.ca.gov/in-home-supportive-services)
- **Vocational Rehabilitation (DOR)** — counseling, training, job placement for Californians with disabilities — [dor.ca.gov](https://www.dor.ca.gov/Home/HowToApply)
- **LIHEAP** — help paying heating/cooling bills; funding limited — [csd.ca.gov/energybills](https://csd.ca.gov/energybills)

**Other statewide**
- **REAL ID (DMV)** — apply online, bring originals and confirmation code to a DMV office — [dmv.ca.gov](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/real-id/)
- **CalOSBA** — small business guides, permits, financing, free consulting statewide — [calosba.ca.gov](https://calosba.ca.gov/for-small-businesses-and-non-profits/business-learning-center/)

## Federal
- **Social Security Retirement** — monthly retirement benefits; apply online — [ssa.gov](https://www.ssa.gov/)
- **Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI)** — benefits when you can no longer work — [ssa.gov](https://www.ssa.gov/)
- **Supplemental Security Income (SSI)** — payments for aged, blind, or disabled people with limited income — [ssa.gov](https://www.ssa.gov/)
- **Medicare** — federal health insurance for 65+ and qualifying disabilities — [medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov/)
- **VA Disability Compensation** — tax-free monthly payments for service-connected conditions — [va.gov/disability](https://www.va.gov/disability/)
- **SBA** — loans, disaster aid, certifications, free counseling — [sba.gov](https://www.sba.gov/)
- **IRS Free File** — free federal tax filing (guided for AGI ≤ $89,000; fillable forms any income) — [irs.gov/freefile](https://www.irs.gov/freefile)
- **FEMA Disaster Assistance** — help for declared-disaster counties; apply online — [disasterassistance.gov](https://www.disasterassistance.gov/)
- **HealthCare.gov** — federal marketplace (Californians use Covered California) — [healthcare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov/)
