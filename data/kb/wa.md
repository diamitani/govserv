# Washington — Government Services

Verified 2026-09-18 · 40 services · Capital: Olympia
Every link below was fetched from an official page on 2026-09-18. Phone numbers appear as printed on those pages; numbers marked (unverified) come from a state directory listing.

**Start here:** call **2-1-1** (Crisis Connections) anywhere in Washington for free, confidential help finding local services — dial 211 or text.

## Metro

**Seattle**
- **Seattle 311 — Customer Service Bureau** — report potholes, streetlight outages, graffiti, illegal dumping, abandoned vehicles; track requests online or via the Find It, Fix It app — (206) 684-2489 (unverified) — [seattle.gov/customer-service-bureau](https://www.seattle.gov/customer-service-bureau/)

**Spokane**
- **My Spokane 311** — utility billing and payment arrangements, solid-waste cart changes, pothole and road-hazard reports, code and parking enforcement — dial 311 (city) or 509-755-2489 (outside city limits) — [my.spokanecity.org/account/help](https://my.spokanecity.org/account/help/)

**Tacoma**
- **TacomaFIRST 311** — customer support center: nuisance issues, garbage/recycling, parking, permits; crime reports go to (253) 287-4455 — dial 311 (city) or (253) 591-5000 (outside city limits) — [tacoma.gov](https://tacoma.gov/government/departments/police/tpd-services/)

**Bellevue**
- **ARCH East King County Down Payment Assistance** — second-mortgage down-payment help for first-time buyers in Bellevue, Kirkland, Redmond and other Eastside cities (program updated to up to $65,000 at/below 80% AMI) — [archhousing.org](https://www.archhousing.org/s/Executive-Board-Agenda-Packet-June-2026-h8ns.pdf)

**Olympia**
- **Intercity Transit — Zero-Fare** — all buses and Dial-A-Lift are free in Olympia, Lacey, Tumwater, and Yelm: just get on and go, no pass or payment — [intercitytransit.com](https://www.intercitytransit.com/news/intercity-transit-receives-first-prize-national-competition)

## County

**King County**
- **ORCA LIFT — Reduced Transit Fares** — $1 fares on Metro, Sound Transit Link/Sounder, and regional buses for riders at or below 200% FPL; free first card — 800-756-5437 (enrollment help), 206-553-3000 (Metro) — [KingCounty.gov](https://KingCounty.gov/en/dept/metro/fares-and-payment/reduced-fares/orca-lift)
- **King County Public Health Centers** — medical, dental, teen health, WIC, and immunizations in Seattle, Bellevue/Eastgate, Kirkland, Kent, Renton, Auburn, and more; uninsured welcome — [KingCounty.gov](https://KingCounty.gov/ru-ru/dept/dph/health-safety/health-centers-programs-services/public-health-centers)

**Pierce County**
- **Pierce Transit Discounted Fares** — ORCA LIFT $1 rides / $31 monthly local; $1 fares for 65+, disabled, and Medicare riders; youth 18 and under ride free — (253) 581-8000 — [piercetransit.org/fares-passes](https://piercetransit.org/fares-passes/)
- **TPCHD Opioid Treatment Services** — methadone/buprenorphine treatment at the Pacific Avenue Public Health Clinic; walk-ins welcome, same-day-medication goal; free naloxone kits, no ID — (253) 649-1406 (naloxone), (253) 649-1626 (billing) — [tpchd.org](https://tpchd.org/health/behavioral/opioid-treatment-services/)
- **Pierce County Veterans Services** — emergency vouchers for food, rent, utilities, medical, and burial for low-income veterans and families; Veterans Resource Center, 1501 Market St, Tacoma — (253) 798-7449, 800-992-2456 — [piercecountywa.gov/veterans](https://www.piercecountywa.gov/veterans)

**Spokane County**
- **STA Reduced & Zero Fare Programs** — 50% off for 60+/disabled (Honored Rider), veterans and military (Stars & Stripes), Basic Food recipients (Opportunity pilot); free rides for youth 18 and under — (509) 328-7433 — [spokanetransit.com/reducedfare](https://www.spokanetransit.com/reducedfare/)
- **Spokane County Veterans Services** — emergency vouchers for food, rent, burial, employment help, transportation, and utilities facing shut-off — (509) 477-3690 — [spokanecounty.gov](https://www.spokanecounty.gov/1136/Eligibility-Criteria-Types-of-Services-A)
- **SRHD Opioid Treatment Services** — medication-assisted treatment at 312 W 8th Ave, Spokane: counseling, case management, jail-based admissions — (509) 324-1420 (enrollment), 509-324-1500 (main) — [srhd.org](https://srhd.org/news/spokane-regional-health-district-srhd-opioid-treatment-services-program-increases-access-to-treatment-with-expanded-hours)

**Snohomish County**
- **Snohomish County Veterans Resources** — claims help, emergency financial aid, food, homeless supports, and utility assistance; county campus, 3000 Rockefeller Ave, Everett — (425) 388-7255 (unverified) — [dva.wa.gov](https://dva.wa.gov/resources?resource_type=All&services%5B%5D=487)

## State

**Food & health**
- **Basic Food (SNAP)** — monthly food benefits on an EBT card; apply online or at a Community Services Office — [dshs.wa.gov](https://www.dshs.wa.gov/esa/community-services-offices/basic-food?banner_hide=1)
- **WIC** — nutrition support, breastfeeding help, and food packages for pregnancy, infants, and children under 5 — [doh.wa.gov](https://doh.wa.gov/you-and-your-family/wic/apply-wic)
- **Apple Health (Medicaid)** — free or low-cost health coverage; apply or renew online through Washington Healthplanfinder — [hca.wa.gov](https://www.hca.wa.gov/free-or-low-cost-health-care/apply-or-renew-coverage)

**Money, jobs & family**
- **TANF — Temporary Assistance for Needy Families** — temporary cash assistance for families with children — [dshs.wa.gov](http://www.dshs.wa.gov/esa/community-services-offices/temporary-assistance-needy-families)
- **Unemployment Insurance** — weekly benefits after job loss; file online with eServices, search jobs at WorkSource — [esd.wa.gov](https://esd.wa.gov/get-financial-help/unemployment-benefits/applying-unemployment-benefits/how-apply-unemployment-benefits)
- **Paid Family & Medical Leave** — up to 12 weeks of paid leave to bond with a new child or care for yourself or a seriously ill family member — [paidleave.wa.gov](https://paidleave.wa.gov/get-ready-to-apply/)
- **Working Connections Child Care** — helps eligible families pay for child care so parents can work or attend school — [dcyf.wa.gov](https://dcyf.wa.gov/services/earlylearning-childcare/getting-help/wccc)
- **211 — Crisis Connections** — free, confidential connection to local help for food, housing, utilities, and more — dial **211** — [crisisconnections.org/211](https://crisisconnections.org/211)

**Education & training**
- **Washington College Grant** — state financial aid for college and apprenticeships for eligible residents — [wsac.wa.gov/wcg](https://wsac.wa.gov/wcg)
- **Division of Vocational Rehabilitation** — counseling, training, and job placement for people with disabilities — [dshs.wa.gov](https://dshs.wa.gov/sites/default/files/publications/documents/22-366.pdf)

**Seniors, disability & legal**
- **ALTSA — Aging & Long-Term Support** — Medicaid long-term services, caregiver support, and adult protective services; find local Area Agency on Aging contacts — [dshs.wa.gov](https://www.dshs.wa.gov/altsa/home-and-community-services/who-contact-find-local-services)
- **Property Tax Exemption (Seniors / Disability / Disabled Veterans)** — reduces or freezes property taxes for qualifying seniors, people retired due to disability, and disabled veterans — [dor.wa.gov](https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/property-tax/property-tax-exemption-seniors-people-retired-due-disability-and-veterans-disabilities)
- **Northwest Justice Project** — free civil legal aid for low-income Washingtonians (housing, family, benefits, employment) — [nwjustice.org](https://nwjustice.org/getlegalhelp)

**Housing & energy**
- **Washington Home Advantage (WSHFC)** — below-market mortgage loans and down-payment assistance for first-time buyers — [heretohome.org](http://heretohome.org/)
- **LIHEAP** — help paying heating and energy bills; apply through your local Community Action agency — [commerce.wa.gov](https://www.commerce.wa.gov/community-opportunities/liheap/)

**Veterans**
- **WDVA — Veterans Benefits** — state benefits for veterans, service members, and families: claims help, counseling, homes, and the state veterans cemetery — [dva.wa.gov](https://dva.wa.gov/veterans-service-members-and-their-families/benefits-veteranmilitary-spouses-and-family-members)

**Licenses**
- **Driver License / ID (DOL)** — pre-apply online for a standard or enhanced driver license or ID card, then visit an office — [dol.wa.gov](https://dol.wa.gov/pre-apply-online?contentpromo=)
- **Business License (DOR)** — register your business and get a Washington business license through My DOR — (360) 705-6741 (unverified) — [dor.wa.gov](https://dor.wa.gov/manage-business/my-dor-help/renew-or-update-business-license/apply-new-business-license)

## Federal
- **Social Security Retirement** — monthly retirement benefits; apply online (typically starting at 62) — [ssa.gov/retirement](https://www.ssa.gov/retirement)
- **Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI)** — monthly payments for people with a disability that limits ability to work — [ssa.gov/disability](https://www.ssa.gov/disability)
- **Supplemental Security Income (SSI)** — monthly payments for aged, blind, or disabled people with little or no income — [ssa.gov/ssi](https://www.ssa.gov/ssi)
- **Medicare** — federal health insurance for 65+ and qualifying disabilities — [medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare)
- **VA Disability Compensation** — monthly tax-free payments for service-connected conditions — [va.gov/disability](https://www.va.gov/disability/)
- **SBA Loans** — 7(a), 504, and microloans for small businesses; Seattle District Office serves Washington — [sba.gov/funding-programs/loans](https://www.sba.gov/funding-programs/loans)
- **IRS Free File** — free federal tax filing (guided for WA residents with AGI ≤ $89,000; fillable forms for any income) — [irs.gov/freefile](https://www.irs.gov/freefile)
- **FEMA Disaster Assistance** — help for counties in declared disasters; check the current list (e.g., August 2026 Eastern Washington wildfires) and apply online — [disasterassistance.gov](https://www.disasterassistance.gov/)
