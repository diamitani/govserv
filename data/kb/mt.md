# Montana Government Services — GovServ Knowledge Base

31 verified services. Verified 2026-09-19.

## Metro

### Billings

**MET Transit — Billings Public Bus** — Fixed-route public bus service in Billings with reduced fares for eligible riders. The city's fare payment page lists current fixed-route fare categories and reduced-fare options. Eligibility: All riders; Reduced fares for eligible riders — see city fare schedule. Rolling (Service runs continuously; check the city page for current fares and schedules). Phone: 406-657-8218. Cost: Fares apply — see city fare schedule. Apply: https://billingsmt.gov/3075/Pay
**Billings Public Library — Library Card** — Free library cards for Montana residents, with access to books, e-books, audiobooks, research databases, and 24/7 express lockers. Start the application online, then visit the Circulation Desk with photo ID to complete it. Eligibility: Montana residents — free card; Non-residents of Montana — card available for $60 per year. Rolling (Cards issued year-round; online applications must be validated at the library within 90 days). Phone: 406-657-8258. Cost: Free for Montana residents; $60 per year for non-residents. Apply: https://billingslibrary.org/326/How-to-get-a-card
### Missoula

**Mountain Line — Missoula Public Transit** — Missoula's public transit system covering the city and surrounding areas. All Mountain Line services are zero-fare — board and ride for free. Eligibility: All riders. Rolling (Service runs continuously; check the official site for routes and schedules). Cost: Free — all services are zero-fare. Apply: https://mountainline.com/your-trip/how-to-ride/
**Missoula Public Library — Library Card** — Free library cards for Montana residents, usable at Missoula Public Library branches and partner libraries across the state. Begin the application online or in person; patrons under 15 need a parent or guardian's permission, and limited-use cards are available for people without a permanent address. Eligibility: Montana residents — free card; Patrons under 15 need a parent or guardian's permission; Limited-use cards for patrons without a permanent place of residence; Temporary cards for visitors — ask staff. Rolling (Cards issued year-round at any branch). Phone: 406-721-2665. Cost: Free. Apply: https://www.missoulapubliclibrary.org/home/services/library-cards/
### Great Falls

Eligibility: All riders. Rolling (Service runs continuously; check the official site for routes and schedules). Cost: Fares apply — see official site. Apply: http://www.gftransit.com/
**Great Falls Public Library — Library Card** — Free library cards for Cascade County residents and taxpayers, with e-books, magazines, and digital resources through Montana Library2Go. Active military personnel stationed at Malmstrom Air Force Base and their dependents are eligible with military ID; limited-use cards are available for those without proof of address. Eligibility: Cascade County residents and taxpayers — free card; Active military at Malmstrom Air Force Base and dependents — eligible with military ID; Limited-use cards for applicants without proof of address. Rolling (Cards issued year-round at the library). Phone: 406-453-0349. Cost: Free. Apply: https://greatfallslibrary.org

## County

### Cascade County

**Cascade City-County Health Department — Cancer Screening Program** — Low- or no-cost cancer screening tests for qualifying Cascade County residents through the Montana Cancer Control Programs, including clinical breast exams, mammograms, Pap tests, and colorectal screenings, plus referrals and payment assistance for services not provided on site. Eligibility: Qualifying residents of Cascade County; Breast cancer screening: qualifying women ages 40 to 64; Cervical cancer screening: women ages 21 and over; Colorectal cancer screening: qualifying adults ages 45 to 64. Rolling (Call for an enrollment form or appointment). Phone: 406-791-9272. Cost: Low or no cost for those who qualify. Apply: https://www.cascadecountymt.gov/341/Screening-Program
### Missoula County

**Missoula Public Health — Immunization Clinic** — County public health clinic offering routine vaccines for all ages, walk-in services, travel consults, and pop-up immunization clinics. The clinic uses a sliding fee scale so no one is turned away. Eligibility: Open to the public — all ages; Sliding fee scale; no one turned away. Rolling (Walk-in services Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday; Wednesday by appointment — call ahead). Phone: 406-258-3363. Cost: Sliding fee scale. Apply: https://missoulapublichealth.org/
### Silver Bow County

**Butte Family Planning Clinic** — Affordable reproductive and sexual health services for teens and adults of every sex and gender: clinical exams, cancer screening, STI screening and treatment, HIV testing and PrEP, pregnancy testing, and birth control options including IUDs, implants, and emergency contraception. All services are on a sliding fee schedule and confidential; telehealth appointments available. Eligibility: Teens and adults of every sex and gender. Rolling (Appointments scheduled year-round). Cost: Sliding fee scale; services confidential. Apply: http://www.co.silverbow.mt.us/3317/Services
### Yellowstone County

**RiverStone Health — Community Health Center** — Yellowstone County's public health department and federally qualified health center, providing primary, dental, and behavioral health care, immunizations, WIC, and public health services. Evening and Saturday appointments available at the South Side clinic; staff can help with Medicaid and health insurance enrollment. Eligibility: Open to the public; serves patients regardless of ability to pay; Varies by program — see program page. Rolling (Appointments scheduled year-round; call the clinic line). Phone: 406-247-3382. Cost: Sliding fee scale; varies by service. Apply: https://riverstonehealth.org/clinic-services/care-in-your-community/
## State

**Montana SNAP — Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program** — Monthly food benefits for low-income households, issued on an electronic benefits card. Apply online at apply.mt.gov, by phone, or at a county Office of Public Assistance; current income limits and benefit amounts are published on the program page for October 1, 2025 through September 30, 2026. Eligibility: Montana residents; Household income within published SNAP limits — see program page; Resource limits apply — see program page. Rolling (Applications accepted year-round). Phone: 1-888-706-1535, 1-866-850-1556. Cost: Free. Apply: https://dphhs.mt.gov/hcsd/SNAP
**Healthy Montana Kids (HMK / CHIP)** — Free or low-cost health coverage for eligible Montana children through age 19, covering doctor visits, dental, vision, and more. Coverage is delivered through private managed care plans; check eligibility by county on the program page. Eligibility: Montana children through age 19; Household income within published HMK limits — see program page. Rolling (Applications accepted year-round). Phone: 1-888-706-1535, (855) 258-3489, (800) 362-8312. Cost: Free or low cost. Apply: https://dphhs.mt.gov/HMK/
**Montana TANF — Temporary Assistance for Needy Families** — Temporary cash assistance and employment support for low-income Montana families with dependent children, including the TANF Pathways employment program run with MAXIMUS. Apply online at apply.mt.gov or through a county Office of Public Assistance. Eligibility: Montana families with dependent children; Income and resource limits apply — see program page; Work participation and child support cooperation requirements apply — see program page. Rolling (Applications accepted year-round). Phone: 1-888-706-1535, (844) 680-4700. Cost: Free. Apply: https://www.dphhs.mt.gov/hcsd/tanf
**Montana WIC — Women, Infants, and Children** — Nutrition support for pregnant and postpartum women, infants, and children under 5: healthy foods, nutrition education, and breastfeeding support. New applicants visit a local WIC clinic to apply; current participants can use the WIC Participant Portal for benefits and appointments. Eligibility: Montana residents who are pregnant, breastfeeding, or recently postpartum; Infants and children under age 5; Income within published WIC guidelines — see program page. Rolling (Applications accepted year-round at local WIC clinics). Phone: 1-800-433-4298. Cost: Free. Apply: https://dphhs.mt.gov/ecfsd/WIC/WICParticipantPortal
**Montana LIHEAP & Weatherization Assistance** — Helps low-income households pay heating costs during the heating season, plus year-round weatherization assistance to make homes more energy efficient. Applications go through local LIHEAP offices listed on the program page. Eligibility: Montana households with income at or below published limits — see program page; Weatherization available year-round; heating assistance follows the October 1–April 30 season. Seasonal (October 1 – April 30) (Heating assistance applications accepted October 1 through April 30; weatherization applications accepted year-round, per the program page). Cost: Free. Apply: https://dphhs.mt.gov/hcsd/energyAssistance/
**Montana Unemployment Insurance** — Weekly benefits for workers who are unemployed or have reduced hours through no fault of their own and meet Montana wage and eligibility requirements. File a new claim online or by phone; gather employment history, banking information, and any applicable military, immigration, union, or federal-employment documents first. Eligibility: Workers unemployed or with reduced hours through no fault of their own; Meet Montana wage and work requirements — see program page. Rolling (File as soon as you lose work; weekly claim filing requirements apply once approved). Phone: (406) 444-2545. Cost: Free. Apply: https://uid.dli.mt.gov/claimants/
**Montana Vocational Rehabilitation & Blind Services** — Helps Montanans with disabilities find, keep, or advance in jobs: career counseling, training, assistive technology, and job placement. Contact the local VR office or the statewide consumer line to start the application process. Eligibility: Montanans with a physical or mental disability that affects work; Desire to work and need for VR services to reach an employment goal. Rolling (Applications accepted year-round; contact a local office to begin). Phone: 1-877-296-1197, (406) 444-1710. Cost: Free. Apply: https://dphhs.mt.gov/detd/vocrehab/VRBSServices
**Best Beginnings Child Care Scholarship** — Helps qualifying Montana families pay for licensed child care while parents work, attend school, or train for jobs. Families pay an income-based copayment; a Montana Okta account is required before using the Parent Portal. Check the official page for current policy updates. Eligibility: Montana families within published income guidelines — see program page; Child in licensed or registered child care; Parents working, attending school, or in job training — see program page. Rolling (Applications accepted year-round through the Parent Portal). Cost: Income-based copayment — see program page. Apply: https://prod-dphhs.mt.gov/ecfsd/ChildCare/BestBeginningsScholarships
**Montana Board of Housing — Home Loans** — Affordable mortgage programs for qualifying first-time Montana homebuyers, including the Regular Bond Program for buyers who qualify for FHA, VA, USDA Rural Development, or HUD-184 financing. Work with a participating lender; income and purchase-price limits apply. Eligibility: Qualifying first-time homebuyers; Qualify for an FHA, VA, RD, or HUD-184 loan; Income and purchase-price limits apply — see program page. Rolling (Apply through a participating lender — see program page). Cost: Loan terms vary — see program page. Apply: https://commerce.mt.gov/Housing/Homeownership/Home-Loans
**Montana Legal Services Association (MLSA)** — Free civil legal assistance for eligible low-income Montanans statewide — housing, family, consumer, benefits, and other civil issues. Apply online; the HelpLine is staffed Tuesday through Thursday, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m., and online intake generally gets a response within 3 to 5 business days. Eligibility: Income-eligible Montana residents with civil legal issues; Varies by program — see eligibility screening. Rolling (HelpLine open Tuesday–Thursday, 9 a.m.–1 p.m.; seek help before legal deadlines pass). Phone: 1-800-666-6899. Cost: Free. Apply: http://www.mtlsa.org/apply-for-services/
**Montana SHIP — State Health Insurance Assistance Program** — Free, confidential, one-on-one Medicare counseling for beneficiaries, their families, and caregivers — plan comparisons, enrollment help, fraud and billing questions, and low-income subsidy screening. Eligibility: Medicare beneficiaries, families, and caregivers in Montana. Rolling (Counseling available year-round). Phone: 1-800-551-3191. Cost: Free. Apply: https://dphhs.mt.gov/sltc/aging/SHIP
**Job Service Montana — Employment & Training** — Free job-search and skills-development help through 18 Job Service offices statewide: career planning, resume help, training referrals, and employer connections. Check the office list and services on the Workforce Services page. Eligibility: Montana job seekers and workers — all ages; Businesses hiring in Montana. Rolling (Walk-ins and appointments at 18 offices statewide). Cost: Free. Apply: https://wsd.dli.mt.gov/?type=InfoBox
**Montana Veteran Affairs Division — Benefits Assistance** — Free help for Montana veterans, surviving spouses, and dependents with state and federal veterans benefits — disability claims, pensions, education, burial, and state veteran benefits. Nine veteran service offices across Montana provide in-person assistance. Eligibility: Montana veterans; Surviving spouses and dependents of veterans. Rolling (Offices serve walk-ins and appointments statewide). Cost: Free. Apply: https://veterans.mt.gov/
**Quit Now Montana** — Free, confidential help quitting tobacco and nicotine for all Montanans regardless of insurance: personalized quit plan, coaching by phone or live chat, and cessation support that varies by program — including special programs for pregnant callers, American Indians, and youth. Eligibility: Montana residents who use tobacco or nicotine products; Special programs for pregnant people, American Indians, and youth. Rolling (Sign up any time; coaching available 24/7). Phone: 1-800-784-8669. Cost: Free. Apply: https://quitnowmontana.com/
## Federal

**VA Montana Health Care** — Health and support services for eligible veterans through the Fort Harrison VA Medical Center and VA clinics across Montana, including primary care, mental health, and specialty services. Manage care and appointments online or in person. Eligibility: Veterans meeting VA health care eligibility — see VA eligibility page. Rolling (Enrollment open year-round). Cost: Varies by eligibility — see VA. Apply: https://www.va.gov/montana-health-care/
**HUD Housing Counseling** — Free or low-cost counseling from HUD-approved agencies: buying a home, avoiding foreclosure, renting, reverse mortgages, and homelessness assistance. Search the official HUD list for counselors serving Montana. Eligibility: Open to anyone — some programs have income limits; see counselor. Rolling (Counseling available year-round). Cost: Free or low cost. Apply: https://www.hud.gov/counseling
**SBA Montana District Office** — Free help starting and growing a small business across all 56 Montana counties: business planning, loan guarantees, disaster assistance, and free counseling. Main office in Helena with a virtual office in Billings. Eligibility: Small businesses and aspiring entrepreneurs in Montana. Rolling (Counseling available year-round; loan programs have their own terms). Phone: 406-441-1081. Cost: Free counseling; loan terms vary. Apply: https://legacy.sba.gov/district/montana
**FCC Lifeline — Communications Discount** — Monthly discount on phone or broadband service for eligible low-income households — up to $9.25 per month, up to $34.25 on Tribal lands. Apply through your provider or the National Verifier; one benefit per household. Eligibility: Household income at or below 135% of the federal poverty level, or participation in SNAP, Medicaid, SSI, Federal Public Housing Assistance, Veterans Pension, or Tribal programs; One benefit per household. Rolling (Applications accepted year-round through the National Verifier). Phone: 1-800-234-9473. Cost: Free to apply; discount applied by your provider. Apply: https://www.fcc.gov/lifeline-consumers
