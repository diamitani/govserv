## Metro

### Denver
- **Denver 311** — non-emergency city services; online 24/7, agents Mon–Fri 7am–5pm MT — 3-1-1 or 720-913-1311 — [denvergov.org](https://www.denvergov.org/311)
- **TRUA rent & utility help** — up to 6 months rent aid (≤80% AMI, Denver city/county, active eviction/demand required); portal CLOSED, reopens 10:00am Tue Oct 20 – 9:59am Wed Oct 21, 2026 — CARE Center 303-838-1200 — [denvergov.org](https://www.denvergov.org/RentAssistance)
- **Denver Public Library — Central Library** — free books, computers, classes — 720-865-1111 — [denverlibrary.org](https://www.denverlibrary.org/content/central-library)
- **RTD LiVE** — 40% transit discount, ages 20–64 in RTD area at ≤185% FPL; apply via PEAK with a photo — [rtd-denver.com](https://staging.rtd-denver.com/fares-passes/fares/discounted-fares)

## State

- **SNAP food benefits** — monthly EBT food aid; apply on PEAK, by phone, or at your county office — Food and Energy Assistance 1-800-536-5298 — [colorado.gov/PEAK](https://colorado.gov/PEAK)
- **Health First Colorado & CHP+** — free/low-cost health coverage; no open enrollment, apply any time — [healthfirstcolorado.gov](https://www.healthfirstcolorado.gov/is-there-an-open-enrollment-period-for-health-first-colorado/)
- **Colorado WIC** — nutrition and food benefits for pregnant/postpartum people, infants, kids under 5; referrals contacted within 10 days; citizenship not required — [coloradowic.gov](https://www.coloradowic.gov/eligibility/apply)
- **Colorado Works (TANF)** — monthly cash aid + work services for families with kids — CDHS 303-866-5700 — [cdhs.colorado.gov](https://cdhs.colorado.gov/colorado-works)
- **Unemployment Insurance** — temporary wage replacement; file online — 303-318-9000 / 1-800-388-5515; Spanish 303-318-9333 / 1-866-422-0402 — [cdle.colorado.gov](https://cdle.colorado.gov/ui/claimants/start-a-claim)
- **LEAP heating assistance** — seasonal (typically Nov 1–Apr 30); confirm current dates — 1-866-432-8435 — [cdhs.colorado.gov](https://cdhs.colorado.gov/leap)
- **Child Care Assistance (CCCAP)** — help paying child care while working/job-seeking/in training; county income rules — [colorado.gov/PEAK](https://colorado.gov/PEAK)
- **Driver licenses & IDs** — new Colorado license/ID; check documents first — [dmv.colorado.gov](https://dmv.colorado.gov/newcard)
- **Business registration** — form entities, file periodic reports, search records — 303-894-2200 — [sos.state.co.us](https://www.sos.state.co.us/biz/FileDoc.do)
- **Colorado Legal Services** — free civil legal aid for low-income and older Coloradans; no criminal/traffic — 303-837-1313 — [coloradolegalservices.org](https://www.coloradolegalservices.org/)
- **211 Colorado** — multilingual confidential referrals: food, shelter, rent, child care — dial 2-1-1 — [unitedwaydenver.org](https://unitedwaydenver.org/community-programs/2-1-1/)
- **Workforce centers** — free job search, career help, training referrals statewide — [co.colorado.gov](https://co.colorado.gov/workforce-centers)
- **County Veterans Service Offices** — VA claims help in every county — Arapahoe 303-738-8045, Adams 720-523-2770 — [vets.colorado.gov](https://vets.colorado.gov/services-benefits/county-veterans-service-offices)
- **Vocational Rehabilitation (DVR)** — employment services for people with disabilities — Aurora office 303-671-4160 — [dvr.colorado.gov](https://dvr.colorado.gov/contact-us)
- **988 crisis line** — call/text 988 24/7; old 1-844-493-8255 now routes to 988 — [bha.colorado.gov](https://bha.colorado.gov/988)
- **Adult financial programs** — cash aid for older adults and people with disabilities (Old Age Pension, Aid to the Needy Disabled) — CDHS 303-866-5700 — [cdhs.colorado.gov](https://cdhs.colorado.gov/adult-financial-programs)
- **State Unit on Aging** — meals, transportation, caregiver support via Area Agencies on Aging — 303-866-2800 — [cdhs.colorado.gov](https://cdhs.colorado.gov/i-want-to)
