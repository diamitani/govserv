# Massachusetts — GovServ Guide (verified 2026-09-19)

## Metro

### Boston / Suffolk County

**Boston 311 — City Service Requests.** Report non-emergency city issues such as potholes, missed trash, graffiti, and streetlights. Free and available 24/7 by app, web, or phone. 617-635-4500 [Boston 311 department page](https://www.boston.gov/departments/boston-311)

**Boston Public Library eCard.** Free eCard for eligible Massachusetts residents, qualifying students, workers, and property owners — access BPL's digital collections and online resources. eCards do not include physical borrowing. [BPL eCard application](https://www.bpl.org/ecard/)

**MassHire Downtown Boston Career Center.** Free career services in downtown Boston: job search help, resume and interview coaching, training referrals, and hiring events. (617) 399-3100 [Career center location page](https://www.mass.gov/locations/masshire-downtown-boston-career-center)

### Worcester / Worcester County

**Worcester 311 — City Service Requests.** Non-emergency city service requests for Worcester — potholes, trash, streetlights, and more. Online requests accepted 24/7; call 311 or 508-929-1300. 508-929-1300 [Worcester 311 page](https://www.worcesterma.gov/311)

**Worcester Public Library Card.** Free library card for borrowing and digital access. Bring a completed application and proof of your current address. [Library card FAQ](https://mywpl.libanswers.com/circulation/faq/390291)

**MassHire Worcester Career Center.** Free career services in Worcester: job search assistance, workshops, training referrals, and employer connections. [Career center location page](https://www.mass.gov/locations/masshire-worcester-career-center)

### Springfield / Hampden County

**Springfield 311 — Citizen Service Center.** Non-emergency city services for Springfield — report issues and request services online 24/7, or call 311 (413-736-3111). 413-736-3111 [Springfield contact-us page](https://www.springfield-ma.gov/cos/contact-us)

**Springfield City Library Card.** Free library card for Springfield residents — get a physical card in person or a CW MARS eCard online. Out-of-state general cards are $35 annually; Massachusetts residents are not subject to that fee. [Get a library card](https://www.springfieldlibrary.org/library/get-a-library-card/)

**MassHire Springfield Career Center.** Free career services in Springfield: job search help, resume and interview coaching, training referrals, and hiring events. (413) 858-2800 [Career center location page](https://www.mass.gov/locations/masshire-springfield-career-center)

### Cambridge / Middlesex County

**Cambridge 311 — SeeClickFix Service Requests.** Report non-emergency issues in Cambridge via SeeClickFix. Not monitored 24/7 — for immediate or public-safety matters call 617-349-3300; for request follow-up call 617-349-4300. 617-349-3300 617-349-4300 [Cambridge 311 page](http://cambridgema.gov/311)

**Career Source Cambridge (MassHire).** Free career services in Cambridge: job-search assistance, coaching, training referrals, career fairs, and a resource room. [Career Source Cambridge location page](https://www.mass.gov/locations/career-source-cambridge)

### Lowell / Middlesex County

**Lowell 311 — City Service Requests.** Non-emergency city service requests for Lowell. Business-hours phone 978-674-4311; urgent after-hours non-emergency line 978-937-3200. Online submissions require an account. 978-674-4311 978-937-3200 [Lowell 311 page](http://lowellma.gov/1977/Lowell-311)

**Lowell Pollard Memorial Library Card.** Free library card for Lowell residents — bring proof of name and residency. Massachusetts residents may also qualify for an eCard. [Library cards and loan rules](http://lowelllibrary.org/services/library-cards-loan-rules/)

**MassHire Lowell Career Center.** Free career services in Lowell: job search help, resume and interview coaching, training referrals, and hiring events. (978) 458-2503 [Career center location page](https://WWW.mass.gov/locations/masshire-lowell-career-center)

## County

Suffolk, Worcester, Hampden, and Middlesex County services are covered above. County-level legal-aid entries:

**Greater Boston Legal Services (Suffolk County).** Free civil legal aid for low-income residents of Suffolk County and Greater Boston — housing, family, benefits, and consumer cases. Find help through the Massachusetts legal-aid directory. 617-371-1234 [Massachusetts legal-aid directory](https://mlac.org/help/)

**Community Legal Aid — Worcester County.** Free civil legal aid for low-income residents of Worcester County — housing, family, benefits, and consumer cases. Statewide intake line 855-252-5342. 855-252-5342 [Massachusetts legal-aid directory](https://mlac.org/help/)

**Community Legal Aid — Hampden County (Springfield).** Free civil legal aid for low-income residents of Hampden County, including Springfield — housing, family, benefits, and consumer cases. Statewide intake line 855-252-5342. 855-252-5342 [Massachusetts legal-aid directory](https://mlac.org/help/)

**Northeast Legal Aid — Middlesex County (Lowell).** Free civil legal aid for low-income residents of northern Middlesex County, including Lowell. Cambridge-area residents can also contact the De Novo Center for Legal Advocacy (617-661-1010). 978-458-1465 617-661-1010 [Massachusetts legal-aid directory](https://mlac.org/help/)

## State

**TAFDC — Transitional Aid to Families with Dependent Children.** Monthly cash assistance for families with dependent children who have low income, plus work supports and child care referrals. [How to apply for TAFDC](https://www.mass.gov/how-to/transitional-aid-to-families-with-dependent-children-tafdc)

**EAEDC — Emergency Aid to the Elderly, Disabled and Children.** State-funded cash assistance for elderly people, people with disabilities, and children who do not qualify for other cash programs. [EAEDC information and application](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/emergency-aid-to-the-elderly-disabled-and-children-eaedc)

**SNAP — Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program.** Monthly food benefits on an EBT card for low-income households. Apply online at DTAConnect.com, by phone, fax, mail, or in person at a DTA office; DTA decides most applications within 30 days. 877-382-2363 [SNAP benefit application (PDF)](https://Www.mass.gov/doc/snap-benefit-application/download)

**WIC — Women, Infants & Children Nutrition Program.** Nutrition support, breastfeeding help, and food benefits for pregnant and postpartum women, infants, and children under 5. (800) 942-1007 [How to apply for WIC](https://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-for-the-women-infants-children-wic-nutrition-program)

**MassHealth / CMSP — Apply or Enroll in Health Coverage.** Apply for MassHealth, the Children's Medical Security Plan (CMSP), and related coverage with help paying costs. Application forms available in English and Spanish. (800) 841-2900 711 [Apply or enroll in CMSP/MassHealth](https://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-or-enroll-in-cmspmasshealth)

**Chapter 115 — Veteran Financial Assistance.** Needs-based financial assistance for eligible veterans and their dependents, administered with local Veterans' Service Officers. [How to apply for Chapter 115 assistance](https://www.mass.gov/how-to/how-to-apply-for-chapter-115-veteran-financial-assistance)

**PFML — Paid Family and Medical Leave (Family Leave).** Paid leave to care for a family member with a serious health condition, for most Massachusetts workers including some self-employed and unemployed applicants. (833) 344-7365 [PFML family leave information](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/pfml-about-family-leave-to-care-for-a-family-member)

**HEAP — Home Energy Assistance Program.** Help paying winter heating bills for eligible low-income households, delivered through local Community Action Agencies. [How to apply for HEAP](https://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-for-home-energy-assistance-heap)

**Unemployment Insurance (UI) Benefits.** Temporary income for workers who lost their jobs through no fault of their own and meet earnings requirements. (877) 626-6800 [How to apply for unemployment insurance benefits](http://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-for-unemployment-insurance-benefits)

**SHINE — Serving the Health Insurance Needs of Everyone.** Free, unbiased Medicare counseling for Massachusetts residents — help understanding Medicare options, enrollment, and benefits. [SHINE program page](https://www.mass.gov/shine-program)

**Class D Learner's Permit.** Apply for a passenger (Class D) learner's permit to begin driving in Massachusetts. The permit application fee is $30. [How to apply for a Class D learner's permit](https://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-for-a-passenger-class-d-learners-permit)

**MASSGrant / MASSGrant Plus — State College Financial Aid.** Need-based state grants for Massachusetts undergraduate students. File the FAFSA or MASFA each year — no separate MASSGrant application. Awards vary and cannot exceed your calculated financial need. (617) 391-6070 [MASSGrant / MASSGrant Plus information](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/massgrant-massgrant-plus)

**MassHire JobQuest — Job Search Portal.** The state's online job board and career portal. A MyMassGov account is required to create a profile, apply for jobs, and register for workshops. [Find your next job with MassHire JobQuest](https://mass.gov/find-your-next-job-with-masshire-jobquest)

**Massachusetts Small Business Development Center (MSBDC) Advising.** No-cost, confidential one-on-one business advising. A signed Request for Advising and an appointment are required — no walk-ins. Pre-launch applicants may need to complete the online 'How to Start a Business' series first. [MSBDC Request for Advising](http://www.msbdc.org/rfa.html)

**MBTA Senior CharlieCard.** Reduced-fare CharlieCard for riders 65 and older on MBTA subway, bus, commuter rail, and ferry services. [Senior CharlieCard](https://www.mbta.com/fares/reduced/senior-charliecard)

**MBTA Transportation Access Pass (TAP).** Reduced-fare pass for riders with disabilities on MBTA services. Call 617-222-3200 for help with the application. 617-222-3200 [Transportation Access Pass](https://www.mbta.com/fares/reduced/transportation-access-pass)

## Federal

**Social Security Retirement Benefits.** Monthly retirement benefits, typically available from age 62 with at least 10 years of covered work. Apply online with a my Social Security account. 1-800-772-1213 [SSA retirement page](https://www.ssa.gov/retirement)

**SSDI — Social Security Disability Insurance.** Monthly benefits for people with a qualifying disability or blindness who have sufficient recent work history paying into Social Security. 1-800-772-1213 [SSA disability page](https://www.ssa.gov/disability)

**VA Disability Compensation — File a Claim.** Tax-free monthly payments for service-connected disabilities. File online, by mail, in person, by fax, or with accredited help. Starting your application online preserves your claim date if you complete it within 365 days. [How to file a VA disability claim](https://www.va.gov/disability/how-to-file-claim/)

**VA Health Care — Apply for Coverage.** Comprehensive health care for eligible veterans through VA medical centers and clinics. Apply online, by phone at 1-877-222-8387, by mail, or in person. 1-877-222-8387 [VA health care enrollment](https://choose.va.gov/health/)

**FCC Lifeline — Phone and Internet Discount.** Monthly discount on phone or broadband service for qualifying low-income households — one benefit per household, with annual recertification. Apply online, by mail, or through your provider. 1-800-234-9473 [Lifeline consumer page](https://www.fcc.gov/lifeline-consumers)

**Medicare — How to Sign Up.** Federal health insurance for people 65 and older and some younger people with disabilities. Sign up through Social Security — online (needs a my Social Security account), by phone at 1-800-772-1213 (TTY 1-800-325-0778), or at a local office. People already getting Social Security benefits are enrolled automatically. 1-800-772-1213 1-800-325-0778 [How do I sign up for Medicare?](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare/sign-up/how-do-i-sign-up-for-medicare)

**FAFSA — Free Application for Federal Student Aid.** The free federal form for grants, work-study, and loans for college or career school. Every contributor (student and parents) needs their own StudentAid.gov account, and a new FAFSA is required every year. States use it for their own aid, so file before state deadlines. [Completing the FAFSA: steps for parents](https://studentaid.gov/articles/fafsa-for-parents/)

**SBA 7(a) Loans.** SBA's primary loan program: the SBA guarantees loans made by local lenders (up to $5 million) for real estate, working capital, equipment, refinancing, and ownership changes. Apply directly through a participating lender; SBA does not lend directly. [SBA 7(a) loans page](https://www.sba.gov/loans/7a-loans/)

**HUD Housing Counseling.** Free or low-cost counseling from HUD-approved agencies — buying a home, avoiding foreclosure, renting, reverse mortgages, and homelessness help. Foreclosure, eviction, and homeless counseling are always free. Call 800-569-4287 (TTY 202-708-1455) for help in many languages. 800-569-4287 202-708-1455 [About HUD housing counseling](http://www.hud.gov/hud-partners/single-family-about-housing-counseling)

**USAJOBS — Apply for Federal Jobs.** The federal government's job site. Create a USAJOBS account (not needed to search, required to apply), search announcements, and apply online — the application walks through resume, documents, review, personal info, then continues to the hiring agency. [OPM: How do I apply for federal jobs?](https://www.opm.gov/frequently-asked-questions/employment-faq/federal-hiring/how-do-i-apply-for-federal-jobs/)
