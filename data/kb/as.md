# American Samoa (AS) — GovServ Service Guide

Verified 2026-09-19. American Samoa has no conventional county governments; village councils and the territorial government administer services. Contacts marked "(unverified)" did not appear in the fetched official page text.

## Metro

### Pago Pago

**Pago Pago VA Clinic (Faleomavaega Eni Faauaa Hunkin VA Clinic)** (Veterans) — VA outpatient clinic: primary care, laboratory/pathology, geriatrics, telehealth, women's Veterans' care, advice nurse, beneficiary travel. Enrolled Veterans; call for appointments. Free. 800-214-1306; VA Health Connect 833-983-0487 (option 3). Fiatele Teo Army Reserve Building, Pago Pago, AS 96799. https://www.va.gov/pacific-islands-health-care/locations/faleomavaega-eni-faauaa-hunkin-va-clinic/

**American Samoa Vet Center — Readjustment Counseling** (Veterans) — Confidential readjustment counseling for Veterans, service members, and families. Free. 877-927-8387 (unverified — after-hours). Ottoville Road, Equator Building, Pago Pago. https://www.va.gov/american-samoa-vet-center/

### Tafuna

**LIHEAP — administered from Samoa Energy House, Tafuna** (Utilities) — The Territorial Energy Office runs home energy assistance from Samoa Energy House, Tafuna; no heating assistance (tropical), cooling/crisis assistance year-round. (684) 699-1101; malelega684@gmail.com. Samoa Energy House, Tafuna, AS 96799. https://acf.gov/ocs/map/liheap-map-state-and-territory-contact-listing

### Leone

No distinct village-level services verified. All programs are territorial (see State) or federal (see Federal).

### Nuʻuuli

No distinct village-level services verified. All programs are territorial (see State) or federal (see Federal).

## County

American Samoa has no counties and no county-administered services were verified. Villages (see Metro) and the territorial government (see State) are the local levels of service.

## State

**Nutrition Assistance Program (NAP)** (Food) — American Samoa's federally funded nutrition assistance program (separate from SNAP), run by the territorial DHSS for low-income residents who are elderly (60+), blind, or disabled. Rolling. https://fns-prod.azureedge.us/nap/american-samoa-summary

**Medicaid — American Samoa** (Healthcare) — Territorial Medicaid administered by ASMSA. No standard individual application/enrollment; federal funds support care through territorial providers. Contact ASMSA for how to access covered care. (684) 699-4777 (unverified). https://www.asmsa.gov/

**Territory Individual Income Tax Filing (Form 390)** (Money & Taxes) — American Samoa's own territory individual income-tax system; Tax Office provides forms and filing information. Filing-season dates vary — see the Tax Office page. (684) 633-4181. https://www.americansamoa.gov/tax-office

**Childcare Programs — DHSS** (Family & Childcare) — Territorial childcare programs; office at Fagatogo Square / Centennial Office Building, Utulei. (684) 633-1571 / 1572 / 1573; childcare@dhss.as. http://dhss.as/contact_childcare.html

**Small Business Development Center (AS-SBDC)** (Small Business) — Confidential, no-cost technical assistance and advising for existing and aspiring business owners. (684) 256-7232 / (684) 699-2929 (unverified); info@assbdc.net (unverified). https://as-sbdc.com/

**State Small Business Credit Initiative (SSBCI)** (Small Business) — Department of Commerce program supporting access to bank financing for small businesses; not a grant. 684-633-5255 ext. 1118 (unverified). Program info (PDF): https://www.doc.as.gov/_files/ugd/614e4b_dbbd715c568849548dbfe094dde7b1d6.pdf

**ASCC Financial Aid** (Education) — American Samoa Community College participates in federal grants and work-study; FAFSA school code 010010. (684) 699-2722 ext. 2208 (unverified); faid@amsamoa.edu (unverified). Financial Aid Handbook (PDF): https://www.amsamoa.edu/files/2024/10.October/2024-25%20ASCC%20FINANCIAL%20AID%20HANDBOOK%20as%20of%2009302024-1.pdf

## Federal

**VA Health Care** (Veterans) — Comprehensive health care for eligible Veterans; in American Samoa delivered through the Pago Pago VA Clinic. 877-222-8387. https://www.va.gov/health-care/how-to-apply/

**VA Disability Compensation** (Veterans) — Tax-free monthly payments for Veterans with a service-connected condition. 800-827-1000. https://www.va.gov/disability/how-to-file-claim/

**Social Security Retirement Benefits** (Seniors) — Monthly benefits typically from age 62 for people who worked and paid Social Security taxes 10+ years. 1-800-772-1213; TTY 1-800-325-0778. https://www.ssa.gov/retirement

**Supplemental Security Income (SSI)** (Disability) — Monthly payments for people 65+, blind, or disabled with limited income and resources. 1-800-772-1213. https://www.ssa.gov/apply/ssi

**Medicare Enrollment — Parts A & B** (Seniors) — Federal health insurance for 65+, qualifying disabilities, and ESRD. 1-800-772-1213. https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare/sign-up/ready-to-sign-up-for-part-a-part-b

**FAFSA — Free Application for Federal Student Aid** (Education) — Annual form for federal student aid (also used for ASCC aid). Free. Federal Student Aid Information Center 1-800-433-3243. https://studentaid.gov/articles/fafsa-for-parents/

**HUD Housing Counseling** (Housing) — Free or low-cost counseling from HUD-approved agencies (homebuying, foreclosure, renting). 800-569-4287; TTY 202-708-1455. http://www.hud.gov/stat/sfh/housing-counseling

**Lifeline** (Utilities) — Federal monthly phone/internet discount (up to $9.25; $34.25 on Tribal lands) for eligible low-income consumers; one per household. (800) 234-9473 (unverified). https://www.fcc.gov/general/lifeline-program-low-income-consumers
