## State

- [SNAP food assistance application assistance](https://www.dcf.ks.gov/services/ees/Pages/Food-Assistance-Application-Assistance-%28SNAP-Outreach%29.aspx) (DCF) — Free help with food assistance applications and eligibility assessments: Mirror Inc 316-283-6743 (Sedgwick/Johnson/Shawnee/Harvey); Catholic Charities NE KS 913-433-2100 (Johnson/Wyandotte/Shawnee +); Catholic Charities SW KS 620-272-0010; Harvesters 816-929-3276.
- [Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF)](https://cssp.kees.ks.gov/apspssp/sspNonMed.portal) (DCF) — Temporary cash aid (EBT) up to 24 months for families with children under 18 (up to 19 in high school) and eligible pregnant women; income under 30% of poverty; work participation via career navigator. Apply via DCF portal; 1-888-369-4777. (State plan on file covers through Sep 30, 2026.)
- [Kansas WIC nutrition program](https://www.kansaswic.org) (KDHE) — Supplemental foods, breastfeeding support, nutrition counseling for low-income pregnant/postpartum women, infants, and children under 5. Apply through your local WIC clinic (e.g., Wyandotte 913-573-6720).
- [Low Income Energy Assistance Program (LIEAP)](https://www.dcf.ks.gov/services/ees/Documents/Low%20Income%20Energy%20Assistance%20Program%20FAQ_final.pdf) (DCF) — **2026 SEASON CLOSED** (ran Jan 20–Mar 31, 2026). One-time annual heating benefit; adult responsible for heating costs, household income ≤150% poverty ($1,956/mo for one person, 2026). 1-888-369-4777.
- [Unemployment Insurance](https://www.dol.ks.gov/unemployment/overview) (KDOL) — Temporary partial wage replacement when unemployed through no fault of your own. Apply via Claimant Self Service Portal; weekly certifications. Contact Center 785-575-1460 / 1-800-292-6333 (no in-person service). Weekly benefit $165–$663 (benefit year Jul 1, 2026–Jun 30, 2027). Spanish handbook available.
- [Vocational Rehabilitation services](https://Www.Dcf.Ks.gov/services/RS/Documents/Policy/Policy%20Manual%20Section%202-A.pdf) (DCF) — Employment help for Kansans whose physical/mental impairment substantially blocks employment; eligibility decided within 60 days; apply in person, by phone, email, or mail at a local DCF office. Temporary conditions (broken limb, pregnancy) not eligible alone.
- [Senior Care Act](https://www.kdads.ks.gov/services-programs/aging/senior-care-act-sca) (KDADS) — In-home services (attendant, respite, homemaker, chore, adult day care) for Kansans 60+ with functional limitations who can stay in the community; sliding fee scale (donation to 100% of cost); functional assessment. Via Area Agency on Aging: 855-200-2372.
- [Aging Services (Older Americans Act programs)](https://www.kdads.ks.gov/about-us/about-our-commissions/aging-services) (KDADS) — Congregate and home-delivered meals, caregiver programs, in-home services, PACE; SHICK Medicare counseling; Senior Medicare Patrol fraud education; MIPPA outreach to low-income Medicare beneficiaries.
- [Tenant Based Rental Assistance (TBRA)](https://kshousingcorp.org/housing-partners/community-solutions/) (KHRC) — Help for income-eligible households with rent, security deposits, and/or utility deposits via local providers; find providers by county.
- [Weatherization Assistance Program](https://kshousingcorp.org/housing-partners/community-solutions/) (KHRC) — Free home energy audit and upgrades (certified crews) for eligible households to cut utility bills.
- [First Time Homebuyer Program (FTHB)](https://kshousingcorp.org/housing-partners/community-solutions/) (KHRC) — 0% interest loan of 15–20% of purchase price for down payment/closing costs via participating lenders; forgiven after 10 years in the home.
- [Kansas Legal Services](https://www.kansaslegalservices.org/contact-us-office-locations) — Civil legal help for eligible Kansans. Statewide intake 316-267-3975; application line 1-800-723-6953; online application. Offices: KCK 913-621-0200 (Johnson/Wyandotte), Topeka 785-354-8531 (Shawnee), Wichita 316-265-9681 (Sedgwick).
- [Board of Regents scholarships and grants](https://kansasregents.gov/scholarships_and_grants) — 2026–2027 application open: Promise Act (high-demand programs at community/technical colleges), Adult Learner Grant (25+), Comprehensive Grant ($200–$10,000 private / $100–$4,000 public via FAFSA), nursing, teacher service, Hero's, military service, National Guard (spring 2027 deadline Jan 31, 2027), state scholar.
- [Driver licenses and ID cards](https://ksrevenue.gov/dovdlcontact.html) (KDOR Division of Vehicles) — Licenses/IDs at exam stations statewide. 785-296-3671 (Mon–Fri 8a–4p); KDOR_DL@ks.gov; Zibell Building, 300 SW 29th St, Topeka.
