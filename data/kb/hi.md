### General Assistance (GA) — Cash Aid — Money & Taxes
*Hawaii Department of Human Services, Benefit, Employment & Support Services Division (BESSD)*

### Hawaii Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC) — Money & Taxes
*Hawaii Department of Taxation*

Refundable state tax credit for working filers who qualify for the federal Earned Income Tax Credit. For tax year 2025 the state credit was 40% of the federal credit; claim it by filing a Hawaii return with Form N-356 and Schedule CR. Later-year percentages are set by statute — check the Department of Taxation for the current year.

- Deadline — Tax filing (Ongoing): Claim the credit with your annual Hawaii tax return.
- Cost: Free to claim
- Apply: [Official EITC flyer — how to claim (Form N-356)](https://files.hawaii.gov/tax/geninfo/info/Flyer-EITC.pdf)
