### Lafayette Parish

**Lafayette Public Library — Get a Card** — Free Lafayette Public Library cards for people who live, work, own property, or attend school in Lafayette Parish. Apply in person with proof (a valid driver's license or work/school ID, plus address proof if the address is not on the ID). Adult cards expire after 5 years; replacement cards cost $1.00; non-resident cards are $20.00 per year. Eligibility: Lives, works, owns property, or attends school in Lafayette Parish; Non-residents may purchase a $20.00/year card. Rolling. Apply: https://lafayettepubliclibrary.org/faq
