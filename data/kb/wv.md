## Federal

- **Social Security Retirement Benefits** — Monthly retirement benefits typically starting at age 62 after 10+ years of covered work. Eligibility: age 62+, 10+ years of covered work (in most cases). Deadlines: rolling. Phone: 1-800-772-1213 (TTY 1-800-325-0778). Apply: https://www.ssa.gov/retirement
- **Supplemental Security Income (SSI)** — Monthly payments for people age 65+, blind, or with a qualifying disability who have limited income and resources ($2,000 individual / $3,000 couple). Eligibility: qualifying age/disability status, limited income/resources, U.S. citizen or qualifying noncitizen, resident of the 50 states, DC, or NMI. Deadlines: rolling. Phone: 1-800-772-1213. Apply: https://www.ssa.gov/apply/ssi
- **Medicare Enrollment — Parts A & B** — Federal health insurance for people 65+; automatic if already receiving Social Security; late Part B enrollment can cause coverage delays and lifelong penalties. Eligibility: age 65+ or qualifying disability. Deadlines: sign up 3 months before your 65th birthday if not receiving Social Security. Phone: 1-800-772-1213. Apply: https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare/sign-up/ready-to-sign-up-for-part-a-part-b
