## Federal

### HealthCare.gov Marketplace Coverage — Federal
- **Agency:** U.S. Department of Health and Human Services — Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services | **Category:** Healthcare | **Cost:** Varies — see program page
- Federal Health Insurance Marketplace for buying health coverage; Tennessee uses HealthCare.gov. Apply online or by phone. Open Enrollment runs November 1 through January 15 — enroll by December 15 for coverage starting January 1.
- **Eligibility:** Live in the United States; be a U.S. citizen or national, or a lawfully present noncitizen; not be incarcerated. People with Medicare cannot enroll in Marketplace plans
- **Deadlines:** Open Enrollment: November 1 – January 15
- **Prerequisites:** Apply during Open Enrollment or a Special Enrollment Period
- **What to bring:** Household and income information (see the get-ready-to-apply checklist)
- **Contacts:** Marketplace Call Center: 1-800-318-2596; Marketplace TTY: 1-855-889-4325
- **Apply/Info:** [HealthCare.gov eligibility](https://www.healthcare.gov/quick-guide/eligibility/)
- **Languages:** English | **Verified:** 2026-09-19

### FAFSA — Free Application for Federal Student Aid — Federal
- **Agency:** U.S. Department of Education — Federal Student Aid | **Category:** Education | **Cost:** Free
- The FAFSA is required to qualify for federal student loans, work-study, and grants. Submit it as early as possible — many scholarships and grants are first-come, first-served — and have your and your parents' tax information ready. Even students who think they may not qualify should file, since many schools use FAFSA data to award their own aid.
- **Eligibility:** Students seeking federal financial aid for college or career school
- **Deadlines:** Rolling
- **Prerequisites:** Create a StudentAid.gov (FSA ID) account; Have tax information ready
- **What to bring:** Your taxes and your parents' taxes
- **Contacts:** How to apply for financial aid (CFPB guide): https://www.ConsumerFinance.gov/consumer-tools/student-loans/applying-for-financial-aid/
- **Apply/Info:** [How to apply for financial aid (CFPB guide)](https://www.ConsumerFinance.gov/consumer-tools/student-loans/applying-for-financial-aid/)
- **Languages:** English | **Verified:** 2026-09-19

### Social Security Retirement Benefits — Federal
- **Agency:** U.S. Social Security Administration | **Category:** Seniors | **Cost:** Free
- Monthly retirement benefits from the Social Security Administration. Apply online, by phone, or at a local office — you can apply up to four months before you want benefits to start. You must be at least 62 for the entire month to be eligible.
- **Eligibility:** Workers age 62 or older with sufficient work credits; apply up to four months before benefits should start
- **Deadlines:** Rolling
- **Prerequisites:** Create or log in to a my Social Security account (for online applications)
- **What to bring:** Social Security number; Birth certificate; W-2 forms or self-employment tax return; Military discharge papers (if applicable); Bank routing and account numbers for direct deposit
- **Contacts:** Social Security: 1-800-772-1213; Social Security TTY: 1-800-325-0778
- **Apply/Info:** [SSA Online Services](https://www.ssa.gov/onlineservices/)
- **Languages:** English | **Verified:** 2026-09-19
