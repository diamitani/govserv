# Delaware GovServ — Verified Services (2026-09-19)

## Metro

### Wilmington
- **Wilmington 311 — City Service Requests** | Report and track non-emergency city service requests (potholes, trash, municipal services). Eligibility: Wilmington residents and visitors. Rolling. Phone: 311. Apply: https://wilmingtonde.gov/residents/311

### Dover
- **City of Dover — Utility Services & Rates** | Electric, water/wastewater, and trash services; start or change accounts, view rates. Eligibility: Dover residents and utility customers. Rolling. Phones: 302-736-7025 (Public Works/water), 302-736-7086 (after-hours water emergencies). Apply: https://www.cityofdover.gov/utility-services-rates-210210

### Newark
- **City of Newark — Bulk Refuse Collection** | Two no-cost bulk refuse collections per year for city residents, with item limits; excessive weight or special equipment may incur fees. Eligibility: City of Newark residents. Rolling (schedule ahead; two free per year). Phone: 302-366-7000. Apply: https://newarkde.gov/index.aspx?nid=502

### Middletown
- **Town of Middletown — Utility Billing & Payment Arrangements** | Electric, water, sewer, and trash billing; payment arrangements require an in-person request; assistance referrals listed on the billing page. Eligibility: Middletown utility customers. Rolling. Phone: 302-696-2958. Apply: https://www.middletown.delaware.gov/departments/Billing

### Smyrna
- **Town of Smyrna — Trash, Recycling & Utility Services** | Trash/recycling collection, utility services, yard-waste collection, meters. New utility applications carry a nonrefundable fee and deposit. Eligibility: Smyrna residents and utility customers. Rolling. Apply: https://smyrna.delaware.gov/281/Services

## County

### New Castle County
- **Down Payment & Settlement Program** | Deferred-payment loans for first-time homebuyers covering closing costs and up to half of the required down payment. Eligibility: first-time buyer (no principal-residence ownership in 3 years), income at or below 80% of county median, HUD-approved housing counseling. Rolling — confirm current income limits with the program office (published program sheet is dated 2015). Phones: 1-800-642-2227 x8905, 302-395-5600. Apply: https://www.newcastlede.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1488/Down-Payment-Settlement-Program-PDF
- **Senior & Disability Property Tax Exemptions** | Over-65 exemption, disability exemption, and disabled veteran exemption for county/school property taxes (disabled veterans: county + crossing-guard taxes reduced to zero). Eligibility: New Castle County homeowners; Over-65: 65 before July 1, 10 consecutive years DE domicile, property <= $676,000, household AGI <= $65,000 (excl. taxable Social Security/RR Tier 1); Disability: proof of disability, same income rule; Disabled veteran: 100% VA rating, primary residence, no residency wait. Deadlines: June 1 annually (exemptions, effective July 1); April 30 annually (state Senior School Tax Credit, up to $500, not retroactive). Phone: 302-395-5114. Apply: https://newcastlede.gov/232/Tax-Information-Forms

### Kent County
- **Kent County — Housing Repair Grants** | Grants for repair of owner-occupied houses and manufactured housing for low-to-moderate-income residents (CDBG/HOME/USDA funds); referral form puts homeowners on the waiting list. CDBG/HOME serve unincorporated Kent County and 19 municipalities excluding Dover. Eligibility: low-to-moderate-income Kent County homeowners. Rolling. Phone: 302-744-2480. Apply: https://www.kentcountyde.gov/My-Government/Departments/Planning/Community-Development
- **Elderly/Disability Property Tax Exemption** | Annual reduction in taxable assessment for homeowners 65+ or 100% disabled — can reduce or eliminate property tax liability; must reapply every year. Eligibility: Kent County homeowners; 65 by May 31 or 100% disabled (physician statement or SSA/VA award); 5 years DE residency + 1 year primary residence (waived for qualifying disabled veterans); AGI <= $18,000 single / $24,750 married (excludes Social Security, RR Tier 1, disability-related income); all county taxes paid in full. Deadline: April 30 annually. Phone: 302-744-2401. Apply: https://www.kentcountyde.gov/Residents/Tax-Credits-Exemptions-Assessment

### Sussex County
- **Sussex County — Owner-Occupied Home Rehabilitation** | Federal and county funding for home repairs for low-and-moderate-income households; call for an application by mail. Eligibility: low-and-moderate-income Sussex County homeowners. Rolling. Phone: 302-855-7777. Apply: https://sussexcountyde.gov/sites/default/files/PDFs/SCCDH_programs.pdf

## State

- **Delaware SNAP (Food Supplement Program)** | Monthly food benefits on EBT for low-income households. Eligibility: Delaware residents; income/household rules apply. Rolling. Phone: 1-866-843-7212. Apply: https://assist.dhss.delaware.gov/
- **TANF — Cash Assistance** | Temporary cash aid for families with dependent children until recipients get a job. Eligibility: Delaware families with dependent children; low income. Rolling. Phone: 1-866-843-7212. Apply: https://assist.dhss.delaware.gov/
- **General Assistance — State Cash Aid** | State-funded cash aid for low-income Delawareans who do not qualify for federal programs like TANF or Social Security. Rolling. Phone: 1-866-843-7212. Apply: https://assist.dhss.delaware.gov/
- **Purchase of Care — Child Care Assistance** | Help paying for child care so a caretaker can work, train, or meet special needs (listed as "Child Care Services" on ASSIST). Rolling. Phone: 1-866-843-7212. Apply: https://assist.dhss.delaware.gov/
- **Delaware Medicaid — Health Coverage** | Medical coverage for low-income individuals and families. STATUS: new federal non-citizen eligibility rules start 10/1/2026 — check de.gov/medicaidengage. Rolling. Phone: 1-866-843-7212. Apply: https://assist.dhss.delaware.gov/
- **Delaware Energy Assistance Program (DEAP / LIHEAP)** | Winter heating fuel help (Oct 1–Mar 31), year-round crisis assistance, summer cooling assistance. Eligibility: income at or below 60% of Delaware State Median Income. Rolling. Phones: 302-654-9295 (New Castle), 302-674-1782 (Kent), 302-856-6310 (Sussex), 302-255-9875 (DSSC). Apply: https://assist.dhss.delaware.gov/
- **Delaware WIC** | Nutrition program for pregnant women, new mothers, and children to age 5: foods, nutrition education, breastfeeding support, farmers' market benefits. Rolling. Phones: 302-605-4066 / 302-605-6653 / 302-605-4099 (New Castle), 302-605-1833 (Kent), 302-605-4055 (Sussex), 302-605-4077 (Spanish). Apply: https://dhss.delaware.gov/dph/homepage/about/sections/imimep/imhome/dhmic/wic/
- **Delaware SUN Bucks (Summer EBT)** | Summer grocery benefits for school-age children. STATUS: 2026 season CLOSED (deadline was 8/31/2026); 2027 applications now open via ASSIST. Automatic for SNAP/TANF/income-tested Medicaid/free-meals/foster/homeless/migrant/Head Start children. Phone: 1-866-843-7212. Apply: https://assist.dhss.delaware.gov/
- **Delaware Unemployment Insurance (UI)** | Temporary partial wage replacement; ID.me verification and JobLink registration required. Appeals due within 10 days of decision mailing. Rolling. Phone: 302-761-6637. Apply: https://labor.delaware.gov/divisions/unemployment-insurance/claimant-services/

- **Delaware ADRC (Aging & Disability Resource Center)** | Statewide guidance for older adults, people with disabilities, caregivers — regardless of income. Rolling. Phones: 1-800-223-9074, TDD 302-424-7141. Apply: https://dhss.delaware.gov/dsaapd/division-of-services-for-aging-and-adults-with-physical-disabilities/adrc/

- **Delaware Higher Education Office — Scholarships** | State scholarship portal: SEED (tuition-free DTCC or UD Associate in Arts for qualifying residents), INSPIRE, Academic Common Market, FAFSA guidance. - **Delaware Libraries — Get Connected** | Free Chromebook/Wi-Fi borrowing, private booths, digital-literacy help, Google Career Certificates. Limited offer: two free Talkspace sessions/month for up to three months for residents 18+ who register before 12/15/2026 (limited vouchers). Apply: https://lib.de.us/getconnected/
- **Delaware 211** | Free, confidential, multilingual non-emergency referrals (food, housing, health care, community resources). Hours: Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–7 p.m. Phones: dial 211, 1-800-560-3372 (landline), text ZIP to 898-211. Apply: https://delaware211.org/faq/

- **CLASI — Housing Legal Services** | Free legal help for qualifying low-income subsidized-housing tenants facing eviction, lockout, subsidy termination, unsafe conditions; also fair housing and manufactured housing. - **CLASI — Immigration Legal Services** | Free legal help for Delaware immigrants who are survivors of domestic violence, trafficking, or other violent crime, and for abused/abandoned/neglected immigrant children and youth: U Visa, T Visa, SIJS, VAWA self-petitions; Medical-Legal Partnership referrals may also get help with citizenship, green card/TPS renewals, DACA renewals. Eligibility: immigrant survivors of crime/violence and eligible children/youth living in Delaware; eligibility assessed at intake. Rolling. Phones: 302-575-0660 / 800-292-7980 (New Castle), 302-674-8500 / 800-537-8383 (Kent), 302-856-0038 / 800-462-7070 (Sussex). Apply: https://declasi.org/legal-services/immigration-legal-services/

Fully recognized by the Board of Immigration Appeals. Eligibility: any immigrant, regardless of status, documentation, or national origin. Rolling — nominal fees, reduced based on financial circumstances. Phones: 302-674-1600 (Kent), 302-856-9578 (Sussex). Apply: https://www.ccwilm.org/immigration/
