# Wyoming Government Services

34 verified services (verified 2026-09-19). Every URL below returned HTTP 200 when fetched.

## Metro

### Casper
- **Casper 311 — Request City Services** (City of Casper). Online portal for non-emergency service requests — potholes, streets, trash, code issues. Requests accepted any time; guest requests allowed. [Portal](https://cityofcasperwy.tylerportico.com/TIM/Portal/portal-home) · 307-235-8400.
- **Casper Area Transit (LINK) — Public Transit** (City of Casper). Fixed-route public transit; adults 18–61 ride $1.25 one way, qualified reduced-fare rides $0.50; scan passes sold at the Transit Office; Transit Center at 1715 East 4th Street. Bring exact fare — drivers carry no change. [LINK](https://www.casperwy.gov/services/casper_area_transit/link_public_transit.php) · [Reduced Fare](https://www.casperwy.gov/services/casper_area_transit/rider_subsidy_program.php).
- **Casper Housing Authority — Rental Assistance** (contracted by City of Casper). Rental vouchers and public housing for households at or below 50% of area median income; waiting lists likely — not immediate assistance. 145 N. Durbin Street, Casper, WY 82601. [Housing](https://www.casperwy.gov/community/housing/index.php) · (307) 266-1388.

### Laramie
- **UWYO Roundup Transit, SafeRide & Paratransit** (University of Wyoming Transportation Services). Roundup fixed routes for students, faculty, staff, visitors, and the local community; SafeRide on-call public transportation late nights/weekends; paratransit for riders unable to use fixed routes (eligibility through the Transportation Services office). [Transit](https://www.uwyo.edu/transportation/index.html) · [Paratransit](https://www.uwyo.edu/transportation/transit/paratransit.html) · (307) 766-7433.

## County

- **Property Tax Relief Programs — Natrona County** (Natrona County Assessor). Veterans exemption (~$400 off 2026 property tax or up to $180 off vehicle registration; deadline 4th Monday in May); long-term homeowner 50% exemption for 65+ residents with 25+ years of Wyoming property tax (apply in person with affidavit; sign-ups Oct 1, 2026–Mar 1, 2027); 25% owner-occupied single-family exemption (deadline Mar 1, 2027). [Programs](https://www.natronacounty-wy.gov/926/Property-Tax-Relief-Programs---Updated-M) · Assessor 307-235-9444, Treasurer 307-235-9470.
- **Campbell County Public Health — Gillette** (Campbell County Public Health). Walk-in clinics, immunizations, health screenings, STD/STI testing, maternal-child health, home visits, and WIC — many services free. [Public Health](https://www.ccgov.net/296/Public-Health) · 307-682-7275.
- **Veteran's Services — Sweetwater County** (Sweetwater County Veteran's Services). Help for veterans with VA benefit questions and claims; Director Garry McLean; offices in Rock Springs and Green River. 333 Broadway, Suite 231, Rock Springs, WY 82901. [Veteran's Services](https://www.sweetwatercountywy.gov/departments/veteran_s_services/index.php) · 307-362-6641.
- **District Board of Health — Sweetwater County** (Sweetwater County District Board of Health). Disease monitoring and prevention, food/water safety, waste disposal oversight, and public health education. 80 W. Flaming Gorge Way, Green River, WY 82935. [Board of Health](https://www.sweetwatercountywy.gov/boards/district_board_of_health/index.php) · 307-872-3970.

## State

- **SNAP — Wyoming** (Dept. of Family Services). Monthly food benefits on an EBT card for eligible low-income households; apply in person, by mail, or fax to the local DFS office, then complete a scheduled interview. [Program](https://dfs.wyo.gov/assistance-programs/food-assistance/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap/) · [How to apply](https://dfs.wyo.gov/assistance-programs/food-assistance/supplemental-nutrition-assistance-program-snap/snap-how-to-apply-and-frequently-used-forms/) · 307-473-3948 (program manager); 307-777-8550 (interview line — interviews only).
- **LIEAP — Low Income Energy Assistance** (Dept. of Family Services). Heating-season help with winter energy bills; new application required each year; applications run through Apr 30, 2027. [Program](https://dfs.wyo.gov/assistance-programs/home-utilities-energy-assistance/low-income-energy-assistance-program-lieap/) · [Online portal](https://dfsgovcloud.my.site.com/UtilityAssistanceOnlinePortal/s/) · 800-246-4221.
- **Weatherization Assistance Program (WAP)** (Dept. of Family Services). Free eligible home improvements (insulation, air sealing) for low-income households to cut energy bills. [Program](https://dfs.wyo.gov/assistance-programs/home-utilities-energy-assistance/weatherization-assistance-program-wap-2/) · [Online portal](https://dfsgovcloud.my.site.com/UtilityAssistanceOnlinePortal/s/).
- **POWER Cash Assistance** (Dept. of Family Services). Temporary cash assistance for families, including POWER Work and POWER Caretaker Relative programs, paired with employment services. [Cash assistance](https://dfs.wyo.gov/assistance-programs/cash-assistance/).
- **Child Care Subsidy** (Dept. of Family Services). Helps eligible low-income families pay for child care when parents are working, job searching, in school, or in training. [Child care](https://dfs.wyo.gov/services/family-services/child-care/) · [Resource & referral](https://dfs.wyo.gov/services/family-services/child-care/resource-and-referral-parents/).
- **Wyoming Medicaid — Apply for Health Coverage** (Dept. of Health). Health coverage for eligible low-income residents; apply online, by phone, by mail, or in person — processing can take up to 45 days. [How to apply](https://health.wyo.gov/healthcarefin/apply/) · [Program](https://health.wyo.gov/healthcarefin/medicaid/) · 1-855-294-2127; TTY 1-855-329-5204; fax 1-855-329-5205.
- **Kid Care CHIP** (Dept. of Health). Low-cost health coverage for Wyoming children whose families earn too much for Medicaid; apply through the Medicaid application process. [CHIP](https://health.wyo.gov/healthcarefin/chip/) · [Apply](https://health.wyo.gov/healthcarefin/apply/) · 1-855-294-2127.
- **WIC — Women, Infants, and Children** (Dept. of Health). Nutrition program for pregnant/postpartum women, infants, and children under 5 — food packages, nutrition education, breastfeeding support, referrals. [Apply for WIC](https://health.wyo.gov/publichealth/wic/apply-for-wic/) · [Participant portal](https://wic-portal.health.wyo.gov/ParticipantPortal/) · Helpdesk 307-777-6236.
- **Unemployment Insurance** (Dept. of Workforce Services). Weekly benefits if you lost your job through no fault of your own; register in WYUI and file continued claims within 14 days after the claim week ends. [Filing instructions](https://dws.wyo.gov/dws-division/unemployment-insurance/claimants/filing-for-ui/) · [FAQ](https://dws.wyo.gov/dws-division/unemployment-insurance/claimants/faq2/).
- **Vocational Rehabilitation** (Dept. of Workforce Services). Helps Wyoming job seekers with disabilities prepare for, find, and keep jobs. [Overview](https://dws.wyo.gov/dws-division/vocational-rehabilitation/) · [How to apply](https://dws.wyo.gov/dws-division/vocational-rehabilitation/job-seekers/how-do-i-apply/) · Cheyenne main office: 5221 Yellowstone Road — 307-777-7364.
- **Wyoming Veterans Commission — Benefits Assistance** (Wyoming Military Dept.). Help connecting veterans and families with benefits and military records. [Benefits resources](https://www.wyomilitary.wyo.gov/resources/veteran/benefits-resources-military-records/) · Hotline 800-833-5987; Commission 307-777-8152.
- **Start a Business** (Secretary of State, Business Division). Register LLCs, corporations, and more online or by paper. [Start a business](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/StartABusiness.aspx) · [Online filing](https://wyobiz.wyo.gov/Business/Default.aspx) · 307.777.7311; Business@wyo.gov.
- **Driver License and ID Services** (WYDOT). Driver licenses and ID cards; the oneWYO portal handles renewals, duplicates, address updates, driving records, replacement disabled placards, and uploads — first-time licensing requires a station visit. [Driver license](https://www.dot.state.wy.us/home/driver_license_records/driver-license.html) · [ID cards](https://www.dot.state.wy.us/home/driver_license_records/id_cards.html) · [oneWYO portal](https://onewyoprodweb.mvds.onewyo.gov/home) · 307-777-4800.
- **Hathaway Scholarship** (State of Wyoming). Merit scholarship for eligible Wyoming public-school, homeschool, and HSEC graduates to use at UW or Wyoming community colleges; apply through the selected college. [hathawayscholarship.org](https://hathawayscholarship.org).
- **Wyoming's Tomorrow Scholarship** (Community College Commission). For adult learners 24+ with financial need — requires citizenship/residency, DWS registration, FAFSA, and credential-seeking admission; apply through the selected college. [Program](https://communitycolleges.wy.edu/wyoming-tomorrow-scholarship).
- **Wyoming Works Program** (Community College Commission). Grants for adult students enrolling in approved programs at Wyoming community colleges to learn a skill and secure employment. [Program](https://communitycolleges.wy.edu/wyoming-works-program) · 307-777-7763.
- **Legal Aid of Wyoming** (Legal Aid of Wyoming). Free civil legal help for low-income Wyoming residents; hotline Mon–Fri 9 a.m.–4 p.m. [lawyoming.org](https://www.lawyoming.org/) · [Apply online](https://www.lawyoming.org/apply-online) · 1-877-432-9955.
- **Wyoming 211 — Community Resource Helpline** (Dept. of Health / Wyoming 211). Connections to food, job training, rent assistance, utilities, and more — call 211 or text your ZIP to 898211. [wyoming211.org](https://wyoming211.org/).
- **WCDA First-Time Homebuyer Programs** (Wyoming Community Development Authority). Wyoming's housing finance agency — low fixed-rate 30-year loans, Homestretch down payment assistance, Spruce Up renovation financing; purchase-price and income limits apply; homebuyer education required. [Homebuyers](https://www.wyomingcda.com/homebuyers/) · [WCDA](https://www.wyomingcda.com/).

## Federal

- **FAFSA — Federal Student Aid** (Federal Student Aid). The form Wyoming students use to apply for federal grants, loans, and work-study — needs an FSA ID; apply as early as possible (federal, state, and school deadlines vary). [studentaid.gov](https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa).
- **HUD-Approved Housing Counseling** (U.S. HUD). Free or low-cost counseling from HUD-approved agencies on buying, renting, mortgage defaults, foreclosure, and credit. [Talk to a housing counselor](https://www.hud.gov/counseling).
- **SBA Wyoming District Office** (U.S. Small Business Administration). Counseling, training, and guidance on SBA loan and contracting programs for Wyoming small businesses. [SBA Wyoming](https://www.sba.gov/district/wyoming).
- **Lifeline — Discounted Phone and Broadband** (USAC / FCC). Lowers monthly phone or broadband costs for qualifying households; tribal lands and survivor benefits available; recertify annually. [lifelinesupport.org](https://www.lifelinesupport.org/).
- **VA Health Care — Apply for Enrollment** (U.S. Dept. of Veterans Affairs). Enrollment in VA health care — checkups with a primary care provider and specialist appointments, including mental health. [va.gov/health-care](https://www.va.gov/health-care/).
- **VA Disability Compensation — File a Claim** (U.S. Dept. of Veterans Affairs). File for service-connected disability compensation (VA Form 21-526EZ) online, by mail, or in person. [File for benefits](https://www.va.gov/disability/file-disability-claim-form-21-526ez/introduction).
- **USAJOBS — Federal Employment** (U.S. Office of Personnel Management). Search federal job openings in Wyoming and nationwide; build a profile/resume and apply — never an application or testing fee. [usajobs.gov](https://www.usajobs.gov/).
