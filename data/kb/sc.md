## State

**Food & health**
- **SNAP / Food Assistance (SCDSS)** — nutrition benefits for low-income households; apply online, in person, by mail, or fax — 1-800-616-1309 — [dss.sc.gov](https://dss.sc.gov/media/ii1pdx24/dss-flyer-505-apr-25.pdf)
- **Healthy Connections Medicaid (SCDHHS)** — health coverage for eligible low-income residents; applications and annual reviews through the SCDHHS online system — 1-888-549-0820 — [scdhhs.gov](https://www.scdhhs.gov/communications/scdhhs-launches-series-how-videos-help-healthy-connections-medicaid-members-complete)
- **WIC Nutrition Program (SC DPH)** — food and nutrition support for pregnant, postpartum, breastfeeding mothers and children under 5; Medicaid/TANF/SNAP participants meet the income test — 1-855-472-3432 — [dph.sc.gov](https://dph.sc.gov/health-wellness/family-planning/women-infants-and-children-wic-nutrition-program/apply-wic-its-easy)
- **DPH Public Health Clinics** — family planning, immunizations, STD/HIV/Hep C testing, WIC, TB services; Richland 803-898-3630, Charleston 843-953-0032, Horry 843-915-8802 — appointment line 1-855-472-3432 — [dph.sc.gov](https://dph.sc.gov/public/public-health-clinics)

**Money & family**
- **TANF (SCDSS)** — temporary cash assistance for needy families with dependent children; most cases limited to 24 months within 10 years (hardship extensions possible) — [dss.sc.gov](https://dss.sc.gov/assistance-programs/tanf/frequently-asked-questions)
- **Child Care Scholarship Program (SCDSS)** — vouchers paid to ABC Quality providers; child under 13 (13–19 with verified special need); parent working 15+ hrs/week or in school; income under 300% FPL — (800) 476-0199 (unverified) — [scchildcare.org](https://www.scchildcare.org/)

**Jobs**
- **Unemployment Insurance (DEW)** — wage replacement; file online via MyBenefits; weekly claims within two weeks of claim-week end — 1-866-831-1724 — [dew.sc.gov](https://dew.sc.gov/node/116)
- **SC Works Online Services (SCDEW)** — job search, resumes, labor market data, training; UI claimants do their two weekly job searches here — [scworks.org](https://www.scworks.org/jobseeker/find-a-job/sc-works-online-services)

**Housing & utilities**
- **SC Housing Homebuyer Programs** — fixed-rate mortgages and down-payment assistance; Palmetto Heroes offers $10,000 for public servants — 2026 applications began Mar 16, first-come first-served — 803-896-2211 — [schousing.com](https://www.schousing.com/Home/HomeBuyers?sub_id=78d28a1f54b8406a997a46d11f2be30d)
- **LIHEAP (Carolina Community Actions)** — heating/cooling bill help for low-income households; priority for seniors, disabled, families with young children; program year Jan 1–Dec 31 — [carolinacommunityactions.org](https://www.carolinacommunityactions.org/liheap)

**Disability & veterans**
- **Vocational Rehabilitation (SCVRD)** — employment services for people with disabilities: counselor appointment, application, records authorization, individualized employment plan — [scvrd.net](https://www.scvrd.net/160/For-Individuals-Getting-Started-with-VR)
- **Disabilities & Special Needs Services (SCDDSN)** — services for residents with intellectual disability, autism, head/spinal cord injury, and similar disabilities; eligibility intake Mon–Fri 8:30–5 — 1-800-289-7012 — [ddsn.sc.gov](https://ddsn.sc.gov/ddsn-divisions/eligibility-division/7-categories-eligibility)
- **SC Department of Veterans' Affairs** — resource connection, peer mentoring, state veterans homes, cemetery program; veteran housing support 803-898-3568 — 803-734-0200 — [scdva.sc.gov](https://scdva.sc.gov/)

**Other statewide**
- **South Carolina Legal Services** — free civil legal help for eligible residents (no criminal cases) — intake Mon–Thu 9 a.m.–6 p.m. — 1-888-346-5592 — [sclegal.org](https://sclegal.org/services/)
- **SC 211** — free, confidential 24/7 referral for food, housing, utilities, health care, jobs, childcare, disaster relief — dial **2-1-1**, text 211-211 — [uwasc.org](https://www.uwasc.org/sc211)
- **GetCareSC (SC Department on Aging)** — statewide referral for seniors, caregivers, adults with disabilities: meals, transportation, in-home care, Medicare counseling — 1-800-868-9095 — [aging.sc.gov](http://aging.sc.gov)
- **LIFE & Palmetto Fellows Enhancements (CHE)** — state scholarship enhancements; full-time, degree-seeking at eligible SC institutions in approved majors — [che.sc.gov](https://che.sc.gov/node/72)
- **SC Driver's License & ID Services (SCDMV)** — licenses, permits, IDs, registration, titling; bring proof of identity, SSN, SC residency, and fees — [scdmvonline.com](https://www.scdmvonline.com/Locations)
- **Business Entity Registration (SC Secretary of State)** — file LLCs, corporations, nonprofits online; name search and agent lookup — [businessfilings.sc.gov](https://businessfilings.sc.gov/businessfiling)
