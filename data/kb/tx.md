## State

**Food & health**
- **Texas SNAP** — monthly food benefits on a Lone Star Card (purchase restrictions took effect 4/1/2026) — 877-541-7905 — [yourtexasbenefits.com](https://www.yourtexasbenefits.com)
- **Texas WIC** — nutrition benefits for pregnancy, breastfeeding, children under 5 — 800-942-3678 — [texaswic.org](https://texaswic.org/apply)
- **Texas Medicaid** — health coverage for eligible low-income Texans — [yourtexasbenefits.com](https://www.yourtexasbenefits.com)
- **Texas CHIP** — low-cost children's health coverage (FirstCare Lubbock exit 8/31/2026; members moved to Superior HealthPlan 9/1/2026) — 800-964-2777 — [yourtexasbenefits.com](https://www.yourtexasbenefits.com)

**Money, jobs & housing**
- **Texas TANF** — monthly cash help for families with children 18 or younger — [yourtexasbenefits.com](https://www.yourtexasbenefits.com)
- **ClaimItTexas** — search and claim unclaimed property; no time limit — 1-800-321-2274 — [claimittexas.gov](https://claimittexas.gov/)
- **Texas Unemployment Benefits** — temporary income after job loss — 800-558-8321 — [twc.texas.gov](https://www.twc.texas.gov/jobseekers/unemployment-benefits)
- **TDI Consumer Help** — insurance questions and complaints, weekdays 8–5 Central — 800-252-3439 — [tdi.texas.gov](http://www.tdi.texas.gov/CONSUMER/get-help-with-an-insurance-complaint.html)
- **TDHCA Help for Texans** — rental assistance, homelessness, homebuyer help — [tdhca.texas.gov](https://www.tdhca.texas.gov/help-for-texans)
- **Texas CEAP** — help with heating/cooling bills; funding limited, availability not guaranteed — 877-541-7905 — [tdhca.texas.gov](https://www.tdhca.texas.gov/help-for-texans)

**Veterans, seniors, disability**
- **Texas Veterans Commission** — VA claims, employment, education, legal clinics — 1-800-252-8387 — [tvc.texas.gov](https://tvc.texas.gov/)
- **Hazlewood Act** — up to 150 credit hours tuition exemption at Texas public colleges for veterans, spouses, dependent children — 512-463-3168 — [hazlewood.tvc.texas.gov](https://hazlewood.tvc.texas.gov)
- **Texas Area Agencies on Aging** — meals, transportation, caregiver support for 60+ — 800-252-9240 — [hhs.texas.gov](https://hhs.texas.gov/services/aging)
- **Disability Rights Texas** — free legal advocacy for Texans with disabilities — [disabilityrightstx.org](https://disabilityrightstx.org/)

**Education, legal, transportation, navigation**
- **ApplyTexas** — one application for Texas public universities, community colleges, many private schools — [applytexas.org](https://www.applytexas.org/)
- **TexasLawHelp.org** — free legal information and referrals — [texaslawhelp.org](https://texaslawhelp.org/)
- **Texas Driver License & ID** — apply, renew, replace; appointments recommended — 512-424-2600 — [dps.texas.gov](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license)
- **2-1-1 Texas** — free statewide helpline for food, health, housing, jobs, and more — dial **2-1-1** — [211texas.org](https://www.211texas.org/)
