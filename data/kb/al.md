40 verified services. Verified 2026-09-19.

## Metro

### Birmingham

**Birmingham 311 — Customer Service Center** — Report potholes, storm drains, traffic signals, illegal dumping, missed trash pickup, and other non-emergency city issues. Eligibility: anyone reporting a Birmingham city issue. Rolling — submit any time online as guest or account holder. Phone: 311 / 205-254-2489. Apply: http://www.birminghamal.gov/government/city-council/residents/311-birminghams-customer-service-center

**MAX Transit — Birmingham-Jefferson County Transit** — Fixed-route bus service across Birmingham and Jefferson County; routes, fares, trip planning, mobile app. Eligibility: open to public; reduced fares for qualifying riders. Rolling. Apply: https://maxtransit.org/

**Birmingham Public Library** — Free library system: central library + 18 branches, books, public computers/Wi-Fi, research databases, programs. Eligibility: open to public; card needed to borrow. Rolling. Phone: 205-226-3600 (unverified). Apply: https://www.cobpl.org

**Housing Authority of the Birmingham District (HABD)** — Public housing and Housing Choice Vouchers for low-income Birmingham residents. Eligibility: low-income household; income limits and waitlist status vary. Rolling — waitlists open/close periodically. Apply: https://www.habd.org

### Huntsville

**Orbit — Huntsville Public Transit** — Fixed-route buses, Mon–Fri 6am–9pm, Sat 8am–5pm; tickets at Huntsville Transit Center (521 Pratt Ave) or Token Transit app. Eligibility: open to public; $0.50 reduced fare for 65+, disabled riders, Medicare/Medicaid cardholders, students with ID. Rolling. Apply: https://www.huntsvilleal.gov/development/streets/public-transportation/orbit/

**Access Huntsville — ADA Paratransit** — Shared-ride curb-to-curb paratransit for people with disabilities in the ADA area, plus seniors 65+ in the city paratransit area away from Orbit routes; $2.00/trip. Eligibility: ADA-certified disability, or 65+ in service area. Rolling — schedule ahead; cancel 2+ hrs ahead. Phone: 256-427-6857. Apply: https://www.huntsvilleal.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/CityofHSV_PublicTransit_Rebrand_Access_Pamphlet_R5-v1-digital.pdf

## State

**Alabama LIHEAP** — Heating/cooling bill help via county community action agencies (ADECA does not take applications directly). Eligibility: Alabama household at or below 150% FPL; apply through county agency. Rolling — seasonal; call county agency. Phone: 1-800-392-8098 (regional: Huntsville 256-907-1550, Jefferson 855-287-1730, Tuscaloosa 833-836-7817). Apply: https://adeca.alabama.gov/liheap/

**ALL Kids — Alabama CHIP** — Low-cost health coverage for children; joint ALL Kids/child-Medicaid application, online or mail/fax. Eligibility: Alabama child; income/age rules vary. Rolling. Phone: 1-888-373-5437 (Medicaid 1-800-362-1504). Apply: https://www.alabamapublichealth.gov/allkids/how-to-apply.html

**Alabama Medicaid** — Health coverage for eligible low-income adults, children, pregnant women, people with disabilities. Eligibility: Alabama resident; varies by group. Rolling. Phone: 1-800-362-1504. Apply: https://medicaid.alabama.gov/

**Alabama WIC Program** — Nutrition assistance, health assessments, breastfeeding support for pregnant/postpartum women, infants, children under 5 via county WIC clinics. Eligibility: Alabama resident; income guidelines + nutritional-risk screening. Rolling — schedule at county clinic. Phone: 888-942-4673. Apply: https://www.alabamapublichealth.gov/wic/

**Food Assistance Program (SNAP) — Alabama** — Monthly food benefits; apply online, by mail, or at county DHR office. Eligibility: Alabama household; rules vary. Rolling. Phone: 1-833-822-2202. Apply: http://dhr.alabama.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Form-2116-1942-Combined-Rev-10-2025-PDF-English.pdf

**Family Assistance (TANF) — Alabama** — Temporary cash assistance for low-income families with children; county DHR office application + interview. Eligibility: Alabama family with dependent children; rules vary. Rolling. Apply: https://dhr.alabama.gov/

**Alabama Unemployment Compensation** — Weekly benefits for workers who lost jobs through no fault of their own; file claims and weekly certifications online. Eligibility: Alabama work history; wage/work-search rules vary. Rolling — file new claim ASAP; certify weekly. Phone: 1-866-234-5382 (claims), 800-361-4524 (questions). Apply: https://uiclaimantportal.labor.alabama.gov

**AlabamaWorks — Career Centers** — Statewide career centers: job-search help, resume assistance, training referrals, employer connections. Eligibility: varies. Rolling. Apply: https://alabamaworks.com

**Alabama Vocational Rehabilitation Service** — Employment services for Alabamians with disabilities: counseling, placement, assistive tech, training, school-to-work transition. Eligibility: Alabama resident; impairment substantially impeding employment; able to benefit. Rolling. Phone: 334-293-7500 / 800-441-7607. Apply: https://rehab.alabama.gov/services/vr/vr

**Alabama Aging & Disability Resource Centers (ADRC)** — Connects older adults and people with disabilities to meals, transportation, in-home help, Medicare counseling, caregiver support. Eligibility: older adult or person with disability; varies by program. Rolling. Phone: 1-800-243-5463. Apply: https://alabamaageline.gov/helpservices/

**Alabama Department of Veterans Affairs** — Help with VA benefits claims, health-care enrollment, education and burial benefits via county veterans offices. Eligibility: veteran/service member/eligible family member. Rolling. Phone: 334-242-5077. Apply: https://va.alabama.gov/

**Legal Services Alabama** — Free civil legal help (housing, family, consumer, benefits); income screening for most programs; some elder-law (60+) grants uncapped. Eligibility: Alabama resident with civil legal issue. Rolling — online or phone intake. Phone: 866-456-4995 (English), 888-835-3505 (Spanish). Apply: https://legalservicesalabama.org/apply-for-services/

**My Alabama Taxes (MAT)** — File Alabama income tax returns, make payments, check refunds, manage tax accounts online. Eligibility: anyone with an Alabama tax requirement. Rolling — file by published state deadlines. Apply: https://www.myalabamataxes.alabama.gov

**Alabama Secretary of State — Business Services** — Register LLCs/corporations, file reports, search business records online. Eligibility: anyone forming/operating an Alabama business entity. Rolling. Fees vary by filing. Apply: https://www.sos.alabama.gov/

**Alabama Housing Finance Authority — Step Up Homebuyer Program** — Down-payment assistance and mortgage options for Alabama homebuyers; income/purchase-price limits apply; apply via participating lender. Eligibility: primary-residence buyer in Alabama; limits vary. Rolling — funds subject to availability. Apply: https://www.ahfa.com

**U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA)** — Loans, disaster assistance, free SCORE/SBDC counseling, federal contracting help; Alabama District Office serves the state. Eligibility: small business owners/entrepreneurs; varies. Rolling. Apply: https://www.sba.gov

**USAJOBS — Federal Jobs** — Official federal job board incl. Alabama federal facilities, military installations, VA centers. Eligibility: varies by posting. Rolling — each posting has its own closing date. Apply: https://www.usajobs.gov
