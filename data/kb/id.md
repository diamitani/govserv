# Idaho Services Guide

39 verified services covering Boise, Meridian, Nampa, Caldwell, Idaho Falls / Ada, Canyon, Bonneville counties, statewide Idaho, and federal programs. All entries verified 2026-09-19.

## Metro

### Boise

**Boise Public Library — Free Library Card** — Boise Public Library. Free library card for Boise residents and property owners within city limits. Borrow from all Boise Public Library locations and Lynx Consortium libraries, including Ada Community Library. Boise Public Library is fine-free. Education. Free. https://www.boisepubliclibrary.org/using-the-library/library-cards/

**Boise Title VI Discrimination Complaint** — City of Boise — Human Resources. File a Title VI discrimination complaint if you believe a City of Boise program, service, or activity excluded you or treated you differently because of race, color, or national origin. Complaints must be written and signed; the Title VI Coordinator can help you put one in writing. 208-972-8500. Legal Aid. Free. https://www.cityofboise.org/departments/human-resources/title-vi/title-vi-complaint-form/

**Boise Renters — Substandard Housing Inspection** — City of Boise — Planning & Development Services. Boise renters can request a city inspection when their rental has substandard conditions the landlord has not fixed. An inspector documents the conditions and can require the landlord to make repairs. 208-608-7070. Housing. Free. https://www.cityofboise.org/media/21357/renterssubstandardhousinginspection-internactiveversion-2026-3.pdf

**Valley Regional Transit — Reduced Fares** — Valley Regional Transit (VRT). Reduced fares on Valley Regional Transit buses serving the Treasure Valley, including Boise, Meridian, Nampa, and Caldwell. Available to youth 6–18, adults 65+, people with disabilities, and Medicare cardholders with valid proof. (208) 345-7433. Transportation. Reduced fares — amount varies by pass type. https://www.valleyregionaltransit.org/fares-passes/

### Meridian

**Meridian Library District — Free Library Card** — Meridian Library District. Free library card for Meridian Library District residents. Bring photo ID and proof of residence to any location; online registration through the catalog is temporary and must be completed in person within 30 days. Education. Free. https://www.mld.org/cards

### Nampa

**Nampa Housing Authority — Public Housing & Vouchers** — Nampa Housing Authority. Nampa Housing Authority provides affordable rental housing and administers Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) rental assistance for low-income households in Nampa. (208) 466-2601. Housing. Free. http://nampahousing.com/

### Caldwell

**Community Council of Idaho — Farmworker & Family Services** — Community Council of Idaho. Nonprofit serving farmworkers and low-income families from its Caldwell headquarters and sites statewide: Migrant & Seasonal Head Start, employment and training, Community Family Clinic on a sliding fee scale, affordable housing, and DOJ-recognized immigration legal services (Familias Unidas) covering family petitions, citizenship, DACA, and TPS. (208) 454-1652. Immigration. Fees apply — vary by service; sliding fee scale available. https://communitycouncilofidaho.org/services/

### Idaho Falls

**Greater Idaho Falls Transit (GIFT) — On-Demand Rides** — Greater Idaho Falls Transit. Greater Idaho Falls Transit provides on-demand shared-ride bus service within Idaho Falls city limits. Book a curb-to-curb ride by phone or through the app. 208-999-4438. Transportation. Fees apply — vary by service. https://www.greateriftransit.com/

## County

### Ada County

**Ada County — Veterans' Employment Preference** — Ada County — Human Resources. Ada County gives veterans' hiring preference for initial county hires. Complete the Veterans' Preference claim form and submit it by the position's closing date; a DD-214 showing an honorable discharge is required if you receive a conditional job offer. 208-287-7123. Employment. Free. https://adacounty.id.gov/human-resources/veterans-preference/

**Ada County Veterans Treatment Court** — Ada County — Fourth Judicial District. Ada County's Veterans Treatment Court offers an alternative to incarceration for veterans charged with substance-abuse-related crimes, connecting them with treatment, counseling, case management, and housing. Veterans are screened for drug/alcohol dependency and mental health issues; the judge makes the final determination on entry. 208-287-7670. Legal Aid. Free. https://adacounty.id.gov/judicial-court/district-court/veterans-treatment-court/

**Central District Health — Public Health Services** — Central District Health. Central District Health is the public health district for Ada, Boise, Elmore, and Valley counties, with offices including a Boise location. Services include immunizations, WIC, family planning, environmental health, and communicable disease control. 208-375-5211. Healthcare. Free. https://cdh.idaho.gov/

### Canyon County

**Southwest District Health — Primary Care** — Southwest District Health. Southwest District Health offers primary care for ages 5 and older at its clinics serving Canyon County and the Nampa/Caldwell area. Call to schedule an appointment. 208-455-5300. Healthcare. Fees apply — vary by service. https://swdh.id.gov/clinic-services/primary-care/

### Bonneville County

**Eastern Idaho Public Health — Bonneville County** — Eastern Idaho Public Health. Eastern Idaho Public Health serves Bonneville County from its Idaho Falls office with public health clinics, health education, referrals, environmental health, and epidemiological investigation. (208) 522-0310. Healthcare. Free. https://www.bonnevillecountyidaho.gov/page/eastern-idaho-public-health

## State

**SNAP — Food Benefits** — Idaho Department of Health and Welfare (DHW). Monthly food benefits on an EBT card for eligible low-income households. Apply online, by phone, in person, or by mail/email/fax; an interview is required after DHW receives the application. 877-456-1233. Food. Free. https://healthandwelfare.idaho.gov/services-programs/food-assistance/apply-snap

**Idaho Medicaid — Health Coverage** — Idaho Department of Health and Welfare (DHW). Free or low-cost health coverage for eligible Idahoans, including children, pregnant women, parents, adults under Medicaid expansion, and elderly, blind, or disabled adults. Apply online through Idalink, at Your Health Idaho, or at a DHW field office. Healthcare. Free. https://healthandwelfare.idaho.gov/services-programs/medicaid-health

**Temporary Assistance for Families in Idaho (TAFI) — Cash Aid** — Idaho Department of Health and Welfare (DHW). Idaho's TANF cash assistance program: temporary monthly cash benefits to help eligible low-income families pay for food, clothing, shelter, and other essentials. Money & Taxes. Free. https://healthandwelfare.idaho.gov/services-programs/financial-assistance/about-tafi

**WIC — Women, Infants, and Children Nutrition Program** — Idaho Department of Health and Welfare (DHW). Nutrition assistance for pregnant and breastfeeding women, postpartum mothers, and children under 5: monthly food benefits on a WIC EBT card, nutrition education, and breastfeeding support. Services are delivered through Idaho's public health districts at 50+ clinics. Food. Free. https://healthandwelfare.idaho.gov/services-programs/food-assistance/apply-wic

**LIHEAP — Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program** — Idaho Department of Health and Welfare (DHW). Helps Idaho families pay utility bills and includes crisis assistance to prevent disconnection or restore service. Administered by Community Action Agencies in every Idaho county; funding is limited and first-come, first-served. Utilities. Free. https://healthandwelfare.idaho.gov/services-programs/financial-assistance/home-heating-and-utility-assistance/apply-heating-assistance

**Weatherization Assistance Program (WAP)** — Idaho Department of Health and Welfare (DHW). Free home energy-efficiency upgrades for low-income households — insulation, air sealing, and heating system improvements that cut utility bills and increase comfort. Apply through your local Community Action Agency. Utilities. Free. https://healthandwelfare.idaho.gov/services-programs/financial-assistance/home-heating-and-utility-assistance/apply-weatherization

**Idaho Child Care Program (ICCP) — Child Care Assistance** — Idaho Department of Health and Welfare (DHW). Helps working families pay for child care for children under 13 (older if the child has a disability). Benefits are managed through Idalink; call 2-1-1 or 800-926-2588 for help. 800-926-2588. Family & Childcare. Free. https://healthandwelfare.idaho.gov/services-programs/child-care

**Unemployment Insurance (UI)** — Idaho Department of Labor. Temporary wage replacement for workers who lost jobs through no fault of their own. A federal-state partnership administered by Idaho Department of Labor staff; file claims and weekly certifications online, with appeals and FAQ resources available. Employment. Free. https://www.labor.idaho.gov/dnn/Unemployment-Insurance

**Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) — Rental Assistance** — Idaho Housing and Finance Association (IHFA). Federal rental vouchers for low-income households: participants pay about 30% of adjusted gross monthly income toward rent and choose their own housing. IHFA administers the program for 34 of Idaho's 44 counties; waiting lists run from several months to more than two years. 855.505.4700. Housing. Free. https://www.idahohousing.com/renters/rental-assistance/

**Office of Veterans Advocacy — Claims Assistance** — Idaho Division of Veterans Services (IDVS). Free, nationally accredited state service officers help Idaho veterans and families file VA disability compensation claims, pensions, appeals, and rating increases, plus VA health care enrollment. No state residency requirement for assistance. (208) 780-1300. Veterans. Free. https://veterans.idaho.gov/

**Idaho State Veterans Homes — Long-Term Care** — Idaho Division of Veterans Services (IDVS). State-run veterans homes providing skilled nursing care, rehabilitation, and quality-of-life services for eligible Idaho veterans and their families. (208) 780-1300. Veterans. Fees apply — vary by level of care; see program page. https://veterans.idaho.gov/

**Vocational Rehabilitation — Disability Employment Services** — Idaho Division of Vocational Rehabilitation (IDVR). A state-federal program helping people with disabilities prepare for, secure, retain, or regain employment through counseling, training, job placement, and assistive technology. (208) 334-3390. Disability. Free. https://vr.idaho.gov/

**Driver's Licenses & ID Cards (Star Card)** — Idaho Transportation Department — Division of Motor Vehicles. Issues Idaho driver's licenses, ID cards, and the Star Card (Idaho's REAL ID-compliant credential), plus vehicle registrations, plates, and titles. DMV office locations statewide. (208) 584-4343. Transportation. Fees apply — vary by credential. https://itd.idaho.gov/itddmv/

**State Tax Filing & Property Tax Programs** — Idaho State Tax Commission. File Idaho individual income taxes (free e-filing available), and access property tax programs including homeowner's exemptions and property tax reduction for qualifying seniors, disabled, and veteran homeowners. (208) 334-7633. Money & Taxes. Free. https://tax.idaho.gov/

**Business Entity Registration (SOSBiz)** — Idaho Secretary of State. Register a business entity in Idaho — LLCs, corporations, assumed business names, and annual reports — through the Secretary of State's online SOSBiz portal. Small Business. Fees apply — vary by filing type. https://sosbiz.idaho.gov/

**Child Support Services — Establish, Modify, Enforce** — Idaho Department of Health and Welfare (DHW). Idaho Child Support Services helps families locate the other parent, establish or modify child support orders for financial and medical support, establish paternity, and request genetic testing. Apply online, by phone, or by mail. 800-356-9868. Family & Childcare. Free. https://healthandwelfare.idaho.gov/services-programs/children-families/apply-services

**Property Tax Reduction (Circuit Breaker) — Homeowner Relief** — Idaho State Tax Commission. Idaho's Property Tax Reduction program, also called Circuit Breaker, reduces property taxes on a qualifying homeowner's primary residence and up to one acre of land by as much as $1,500. The State Tax Commission administers the program; applications are filed with your county assessor, which will help you complete the application. (208) 334-7633. Money & Taxes. Free. https://tax.idaho.gov/property

**Idaho Legal Aid Services — Free Civil Legal Help** — Idaho Legal Aid Services, Inc.. Statewide nonprofit civil law firm providing free legal help to low-income Idahoans: family law, civil protection orders, housing, domestic and sexual violence, and senior and public-benefits advice lines. Seven offices across Idaho (Boise, Coeur d'Alene, Idaho Falls, Lewiston, Nampa, Pocatello, Twin Falls). 208-746-7541. Legal Aid. Free. https://idaholegalaid.org/apply-for-help

**Idaho Commission on Aging — Senior Services** — Idaho Commission on Aging. State office that plans, coordinates, and funds services helping older Idahoans live independently: Area Agencies on Aging provide congregate and home-delivered meals, transportation, caregiver support, and community services, plus a long-term care ombudsman program. (208) 334-3833. Seniors. Free. https://aging.idaho.gov/

**Idaho Opportunity Scholarship — Recent Graduates** — Idaho State Board of Education (NextSteps Idaho). Idaho's largest state scholarship for recent Idaho high school graduates attending eligible Idaho colleges and universities. The 2026–27 application opens October 1, 2026; both the scholarship application and the FAFSA must be completed by March 1, 2027. Education. Free. https://scholarships.idaho.gov/

**Idaho Opportunity Scholarship — Adult Learners** — Idaho State Board of Education (NextSteps Idaho). The Idaho Opportunity Scholarship for adult learners returning to school or starting college later in life, attending eligible Idaho postsecondary institutions. File the FAFSA by the semester deadline for the term you plan to attend. Education. Free. https://nextsteps.idaho.gov/scholarship-idaho/idaho-opportunity-scholarship-for-adult-learners

**Idaho Office for Refugees — Resettlement Coordination** — Idaho Office for Refugees. Administers Idaho's refugee resettlement program and coordinates services for newcomers statewide — connecting refugees, asylees, and other humanitarian arrivals with resettlement agencies in Boise and Twin Falls, employment support, English classes, and citizenship services. 208-342-2254. Immigration. Free. https://www.idahorefugees.org/

## Federal

**Medicare (Federal)** — Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services. Federal health insurance for people 65 or older meeting citizenship or residency requirements, and for some people under 65 with a disability, End-Stage Renal Disease, or ALS. Some people are enrolled automatically; others must actively sign up depending on when they start Social Security benefits. Seniors. Varies by part; Part A premium-free for most. https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare

**VA Disability Compensation (Federal)** — U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. Monthly tax-free payments to veterans who got sick or injured while serving, or whose service made an existing condition worse — covering physical conditions and mental health conditions such as PTSD. File a claim online, by phone, mail, or fax; an accredited attorney, claims agent, or Veterans Service Organization representative can help. Veterans. Free to apply. https://www.va.gov/disability/

**IRS Free File (Federal)** — Internal Revenue Service. Prepare and e-file a federal tax return for free through IRS Free File: guided tax software for qualifying filers, and Free File Fillable Forms at any income level. Always start at IRS.gov/freefile — not at a partner's commercial site. Money & Taxes. Free for qualifying filers. https://www.irs.gov/filing/free-file-do-your-federal-taxes-for-free

**Boise VA Medical Center (Federal)** — U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. VA medical center serving veterans in the Boise area with primary and specialty care, mental health services, and more. Enroll in VA health care online, by phone, or in person. Healthcare. Free for enrolled veterans — some copays may apply. https://www.va.gov/boise-health-care/
