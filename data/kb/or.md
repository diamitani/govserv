# Oregon Government Services (OR)

36 verified services. Capital: Salem. Grouped Metro / County / State / Federal. Verified 2026-09-19.

## Metro

### Portland
- **TriMet Honored Citizen Reduced Fare** — Reduced Portland-metro transit fares for 65+, Medicare recipients, people with disabilities, qualifying-income riders, veterans, and military. Apply any time; proof required for most categories. [Program page](http://trimet.org/fares/honoredcitizen.htm)
- **PDX 311 / Multnomah County non-emergency** — Portland and Multnomah County government navigation. Dial 311, 7 days 7 a.m.-6 p.m. (except federal holidays); 711 relay. Email: 311@portlandoregon.gov. [Announcement](https://www.portland.gov/hello/news/2026/8/31/pdx-311-your-connection-local-government-one-call-can-help-you-find-answers)

### Salem
- **Cherriots Reduced Fare ID** — Reduced Salem-area transit fares. 60+ show age ID; people with qualifying disabilities apply in person at 220 High Street NE, Salem (503-588-2877). Original card free; $5 replacement. [Program page](https://ww.cherriots.org/reducedfare/)
- **Marion County Veteran Service Office** — Free VA-claim help in Salem: 780 Commercial Street SE, Suite 302; 971-707-4400; info.vso@mwvcaa.org. Walk-ins Mon-Wed 9-11 a.m. and 1-4 p.m.; closed noon-1 p.m. and last day of month. [ODVA page](https://www.oregon.gov/odva/services/pages/counties/marion.aspx)

## State

- **Oregon SNAP** — Monthly food benefits on an EBT card. As of June 1, 2026, every application and renewal requires an interview. 800-699-9075 (Mon-Fri, 7 a.m.-6 p.m. Pacific); TTY 711. [SNAP help](https://www.oregon.gov/odhs/food/Pages/snap-help.aspx) · [Interview update](https://apps.oregon.gov/oregon-newsroom/OR/ODHS/Posts/Post/snap-interviews-required-for-all-households-starting-june-1-2026)
- **Oregon Health Plan (OHP)** — Free or low-cost Medicaid coverage: medical, dental, mental health. Applications 800-699-9075; member line 1-800-273-0557; PO Box 14015, Salem, OR 97309. [Contact/apply](https://www.oregon.gov/oha/hsd/ohp/pages/contact-us.aspx)
- **Oregon WIC** — Nutrition support for pregnant/postpartum people and young children; clinics in all 36 counties. Dial 211 or call 1-800-735-2900 (TTY 711). [County clinic list](https://www.oregon.gov/oha/ph/healthypeoplefamilies/wic/pages/countyinfo.aspx)
- **Oregon Unemployment Insurance** — Wage replacement for workers who lost jobs through no fault of their own. File through Frances Online; weekly claims required. 1-877-345-3484. [File a claim](https://unemployment.oregon.gov/file-a-claim)
- **Oregon TANF** — Monthly cash benefits for low-income families with children, plus job support. Oregon residency and low income/resources required; apply online, by phone, or in person - an interview follows. 800-699-9075. [Apply](https://www.oregon.gov/odhs/cash/Pages/tanf.aspx)
- **Temporary Assistance for Domestic Violence Survivors (TA-DVS)** — Up to $3,200 over 90 days for safety-related costs, for Oregon residents meeting TANF income limits who are pregnant or caring for a minor child and at risk of domestic violence. Apply through a local ODHS office. [Program page](https://www.oregon.gov/odhs/dv/Pages/tadvs.aspx)
- **Oregon LIHEAP** — Help paying home heating bills through local Community Action Agencies. 1-800-453-5511 (option 2). Seasonal program - check current availability. [Fact sheet](https://www.oregon.gov:443/ohcs/energy-weatherization/Documents/factsheets/Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program Fact Sheet 2023.pdf)
- **Employment Related Day Care (ERDC)** — Child-care subsidy for working, job-searching, schooling, medical-leave, and approved family-wellbeing activities. Copay may apply; report changes within 10 days. 1-800-699-9075; Direct Pay Unit 1-800-699-9074. [Family quick tips (PDF)](https://www.oregon.gov/delc/providers/Documents/Quick%20Tips%20for%20Families%20EN.pdf)
- **Paid Leave Oregon** — Paid family, medical, and safe leave for Oregon employees earning at least $1,000 in a base or alternate base year. Up to 12 weeks (14 for pregnancy-related medical leave); job protection after 90+ days with an employer. 833-854-0166. [Brochure (PDF)](https://paidleave.oregon.gov/Documents/PaidLeave-Brochure-EN.pdf)
- **Oregon Tax Credits** — Refundable state credits: Oregon EIC (ITIN filers included), Oregon Kids Credit, and Working Family Household and Dependent Care Credit. Claimed on the Oregon return. Questions: eic.4me@dor.oregon.gov. [Credits page](http://www.oregon.gov/dor/programs/individuals/Pages/credits.aspx)
- **Oregon Driver License and ID Services** — Licenses, permits, and ID cards including REAL ID, by appointment or standby. Knowledge tests available online in multiple languages (English, Spanish, Arabic, Simplified Mandarin Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Vietnamese). DMV 503-945-5000. [Get a license](https://www.oregon.gov/odot/DMV/Pages/DriverID/licenseget.aspx) · [Appointments](https://www.oregon.gov/odot/dmv/pages/appointments.aspx)
- **Oregon Vocational Rehabilitation (VR)** — Employment counseling and services for Oregonians with disabilities. Free; apply through a local VR office. [Program page](https://www.oregon.gov/dhs/EMPLOYMENT/VR/Pages/index.aspx)
- **Oregon VR Youth Services (Ages 14-24)** — Pre-employment and employment services for youth 14-24 with a documented disability. STATUS: eligible applicants are placed on a waitlist. 503-945-5880. [Program page](https://www.oregon.gov/odhs/vr-youth-services/pages/default.aspx)
- **ODVA Veterans Emergency Financial Assistance** — STATUS: Open. One-time emergency help for Oregon veterans, paid to creditors. Apply through a VSO or Veteran Advocate by the 10th of each month. 503-373-2085. [Program page](https://www.oregon.gov/odva/agency-programs/grants/pages/emergency-assistance-grant.aspx)
- **ADRC of Oregon** — Free statewide options counseling for older adults and people with disabilities of any age or income. 1-855-673-2372. [About ADRC](https://adrcoforegon.org/consumersite/connect/about_adrc/)
- **211info** — Statewide helpline for housing, rent, utilities, food, and other local help. Dial 211 or call 866-698-6155; interpreters available. [211info.org](https://www.211info.org/)
- **OHCS Housing Assistance** — STATUS: The Oregon Emergency Rental Assistance Program (OERAP) is closed to new applications. OHCS routes residents to 211 and Community Action Agencies for rent, utility, shelter, and weatherization help, plus homeownership and wildfire recovery. [Housing assistance](https://www.oregon.gov/ohcs/housing-assistance/Pages/housing-stability.aspx)
- **Oregon Opportunity Grant and Oregon Promise** — State college grants. Oregon Promise is last-dollar tuition help (up to 12 credits/term, 90 credits total; Class of 2026 SAI limit 27,000; renewal by June 1). File the FAFSA or the ORSAA (confidential alternative for undocumented, DACA, TPS, U-visa students). ORSAA help: (541) 687-7400 option 3, ORSAAHelp@hecc.oregon.gov; Promise: OregonPromise@hecc.oregon.gov. [Promise fact sheet (PDF)](https://www.oregon.gov/highered/access/Documents/OSAC/2026-27%20OPG%20Fact%20Sheet%20for%20New%20Students.pdf) · [ORSAA guide (PDF)](https://www.oregonstudentaid.gov/media/3wlplisl/guide-to-the-orsaa-2025.pdf)
- **ODHS Refugee Services** — Cash assistance and other services for refugees when they first arrive in Oregon. 800-699-9075. [ODHS benefits eligibility](https://www.oregon.gov/odhs/providers-partners/benefits-eligibility/pages/default.aspx)
- **Oregon Business Registration (Secretary of State)** — Register an assumed name, LLC, corporation, or nonprofit. Online review typically begins within one business day; renewals on the filing anniversary (assumed names every two years). obr.noreply@sos.oregon.gov. [FAQ and e-filing](https://sos.oregon.gov/business/Pages/faq.aspx)

## Federal

- **Social Security - Retirement, Disability (SSDI), and SSI** — Retirement from 62; disability benefits for adults unable to work due to a condition expected to last 12+ months; SSI for 65+ or people with disabilities who have limited income and resources. Apply online and save progress. 1-800-772-1213. [Apply](https://www.ssa.gov/blog/en/posts/2023-08-31.html)
- **Medicare** — Federal health insurance for 65+, some younger people with disabilities, and ESRD/ALS. Sign up through Social Security; some enrolled automatically. Late Part B enrollment can mean a monthly penalty. 1-800-772-1213. [How to sign up](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare/sign-up/how-do-i-sign-up-for-medicare)
- **VA Portland Health Care System - Enrollment** — Enroll with VA Form 10-10EZ; walk-in enrollment in Portland, Hillsboro, and Salem (Mon-Fri 8 a.m.-4 p.m., federal holidays excluded). 503-273-5069 or 503-220-8262 ext. 55069. [Register for care](https://www.va.gov/portland-health-care/register-for-care-replaced)
- **VA Disability Compensation** — Monthly tax-free payments for service-connected disabilities. File online, by mail (VA Form 21-526EZ), in person, or with an accredited representative; save and finish within 365 days. [How to file](https://www.va.gov/disability/how-to-file-claim/)
- **FAFSA - Federal Student Aid** — Required application for federal grants, work-study, and student loans; the 2026-27 form covers July 1, 2026-June 30, 2027. [Federal Student Aid loans page](https://studentaid.gov/understand-aid/types/loans/)
- **HUD-Approved Housing Counseling** — Free or low-cost counseling to obtain, sustain, or keep housing. 800-569-4287; TTY 202-708-1455. [Find counseling](http://www.hud.gov/stat/sfh/housing-counseling)
- **FCC Lifeline** — Up to $9.25/month off phone or internet service ($34.25 on Tribal lands); one benefit per household. Oregon residents use the state's application process. 1-800-234-9473; LifelineSupport@usac.org. Recertify yearly. [Lifeline consumers](https://www.fcc.gov/lifeline-consumers)
- **SBA Portland District Office** — Funding programs, counseling, federal-contracting certifications, and disaster recovery for small businesses in 30 Oregon counties. Appointment only: 419 SW 11th Avenue, Suite 310, Portland; 503-326-2682. [SBA Oregon](https://sba.gov/or/)
