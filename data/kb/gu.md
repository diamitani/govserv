# Guam (GU) — GovServ Service Guide

Verified 2026-09-19. Guam has no counties; its 19 municipalities (villages) are the local level of government.

## Metro

### Dededo

**Dededo Mayor's Office — Village Services and Verification of Residence** (Legal Aid) — Verification-of-residence letters for registered households (ready next business day, emailable), GRTA bus pass sales, flea-market reservations/transactions (Wed-Thu 9am-4pm), smoke-alarm requests, used-cooking-oil collection. Dededo residents; VOR requires registered household. Rolling. (671) 632-5203 / (671) 632-5019; flea market (671) 637-9014; dededo.astumbo@gmail.com. Directory: https://mcog.guam.gov/directory (VOR details per June 2021 village news — call to confirm current availability)

**Northern Regional Community Health Center (NRCHC) — Dededo** (Healthcare) — DPHSS northern public clinic: doctor visits, children's and women's care, immunizations, pharmacy; WIC Dededo clinic on-site (Mon-Sat 8am-5pm). Uninsured not turned away. Call ahead — hours disrupted by 2026 typhoons. Open to Guam residents. Rolling. (671) 635-7412; WIC (671) 635-7471/72. 520 W Santa Monica Ave, Dededo. http://dphss.guam.gov/

**GPLS Dededo Branch Library** (Education) — Free cards for Guam residents 6+ with ID; print/scan, computers, programming. Branch hours limited — call ahead (was Tuesdays 9am-12pm, 1pm-5:30pm in the 2024 schedule). (671) 632-5503. http://gpls.guam.gov/

### Yigo

**Yigo Mayor's Office — Village Services** (Legal Aid) — Community services and GRTA bus pass sales for Yigo residents. Rolling. (671) 653-5248 / 653-9446 / 653-9119. https://mcog.guam.gov/directory

### Tamuning

**Tamuning-Tumon-Harmon Mayor's Office — Village Services** (Legal Aid) — Community services for Tamuning residents. Note: this office does NOT sell GRTA bus passes. Rolling. (671) 646-5211 / 646-8646. https://mcog.guam.gov/directory

**DPHSS Bureau of Economic Security — Central Office (Tamuning)** (Money & Taxes) — In-person applications for SNAP, Medicaid, cash, MIP for the central region. Rolling. (671) 300-8853/8863; centralbes@dphss.guam.gov. 761 S Marine Corps Dr, RAN-Care Bldg, Tamuning. Application: https://dphss.guam.gov/sites/default/files/Application%20for%20Public%20Benefits%2010.2023.pdf

### Mangilao

**Mangilao Mayor's Office — Village Services** (Legal Aid) — Community services and GRTA bus pass sales for Mangilao residents. Rolling. (671) 734-2163. https://mcog.guam.gov/directory

### Barrigada

**Barrigada Mayor's Office — Village Services** (Legal Aid) — Community services and GRTA bus pass sales for Barrigada residents. Rolling. (671) 734-3737/3859. https://mcog.guam.gov/directory

**GPLS Barrigada Branch Library** (Education) — Free cards for Guam residents 6+ with ID. Branch hours limited — call main library (was Thursdays 9am-12pm, 1pm-5:30pm in the 2024 schedule). (671) 475-4751. http://gpls.guam.gov/

**DRT Business Licensing — New Licenses and Online Renewals (Barrigada/Tamuning)** (Small Business) — Form BL-01 (new license), one per activity/location; drop box at DRT main office Barrigada, BLPC by appointment at DWP Compound Tamuning; online renewal with EIN/SSN + GRT account + email. Businesses operating in Guam. Rolling. (671) 646-3102; maelinda.bayona@revtax.guam.gov. Renew: https://www.guamtax.com/efile/biz.html

## County

Guam has no counties — villages (municipalities) are the local level of government; all local services are listed under Metro above.

## State

**SNAP — Guam** (Food, DPHSS Bureau of Economic Security) — Monthly food assistance; apply with the Application for Public Benefits at Dededo, Tamuning, or Inarajan BES offices. Low-income Guam households. Rolling. (671) 635-7432/7485 (Dededo), (671) 300-8853/8863 (Tamuning), (671) 828-7542 (Inarajan). Apply: https://dphss.guam.gov/sites/default/files/Application%20for%20Public%20Benefits%2010.2023.pdf

**Medicaid — Guam (DPHSS)** (Healthcare) — Health coverage for eligible low-income residents; temporary post-typhoon flexibilities active as of 2026-09-15. Rolling. Same BES offices/phones. Apply: https://dphss.guam.gov/sites/default/files/Application%20for%20Public%20Benefits%2010.2023.pdf

**TANF — Guam Cash Assistance** (Money & Taxes, DPHSS BES) — Temporary cash aid for families in need. Rolling. Same BES offices/phones. Apply: https://dphss.guam.gov/sites/default/files/Application%20for%20Public%20Benefits%2010.2023.pdf

**Medically Indigent Program (MIP) — Guam** (Healthcare, DPHSS) — Coverage for medically indigent residents ineligible for Medicaid. Rolling. Same BES offices/phones. Apply: https://dphss.guam.gov/sites/default/files/Application%20for%20Public%20Benefits%2010.2023.pdf

**Guam WIC — Women, Infants, and Children Nutrition Program** (Food, DPHSS) — Nutrition aid for pregnant/postpartum women, infants, children under 5. Clinics: Dededo (671) 635-7471/72 Mon-Sat 8-5; Tiyan (671) 475-0295/96 (adjusted hours effective 2026-09-03 — call ahead); Santa Rita (671) 565-3536; Southern (671) 828-7550. Rolling. Clinic info: https://dphss.guam.gov/sites/default/files/news_1781573159_PR26-110%20DPHSS%20WIC%20Clinics%20to%20Close%20Temporarily%20for%20Emergency%20Preparedness%20Training.pdf

**Child Care and Development Fund (CCDF) — Guam Child Care Subsidy** (Family & Childcare, DPHSS Bureau of Child Care Services) — Child care subsidies. STATUS: new applicants waitlisted as of 2026-09-03; updated income guidelines effective 2026-10-01. https://dphss.guam.gov/bureau-of-child-care-services/

**DPHSS Public Health Clinics — Northern and Southern Regional Community Health Centers** (Healthcare) — Doctor visits, children's/women's care, immunizations, TB, dental, mental health, labs, pharmacy. Uninsured not turned away; call ahead (typhoon disruptions). Rolling. NRCHC (671) 635-7412 (520 W Santa Monica Ave, Dededo); SRCHC (671) 828-7511. http://dphss.guam.gov/

**DPHSS Division of Senior Citizens — Senior Services** (Seniors) — Senior centers, adult day care, meal services. STATUS: April 2026 update — senior centers closed until power/water restored, adult day care fully operational; call ahead for current schedules. (671) 735-7415/21; seniorcenters@dphss.guam.gov; adult day care (671) 475-6266. http://dphss.guam.gov/

**LIHEAP — Low Income Home Energy Assistance (Guam Energy Office)** (Utilities) — STATUS: both 2026 cycles CLOSED (Crisis Assistance 2026-03-16–2026-08-14; Cooling Credit 2026-03-16–2026-03-27); future cycles funding-dependent. (671) 646-4361. https://energy.guam.gov/liheap/

**GHURA Public Housing** (Housing) — Affordable units at AMP sites islandwide; apply in person at site offices; wait lists open/close by site (recent: AMP4 opened 2026-03-20–03-31; AMP3 closed 2026-07-15). Low-income families, seniors, disabled. Free to apply. (671) 477-9851; TTY (671) 472-3701. https://ghura.org/

**GHURA Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher (HCV)** (Housing) — Rental vouchers; apply online only during announced wait-list openings — no general open wait list. Free to apply. (671) 477-9851. https://ghura.org/section-8

**GHURA Family Self-Sufficiency (FSS) Program** (Employment) — Voluntary employment/savings program for active HCV/public-housing participants in good standing; 5-year contract, escrow earnings; applications of interest waitlisted. Free. (671) 475-1333; (671) 475-1339; scepeda@ghura.org. https://ghura.org/about-fss-program

**Guam Housing Corporation — First-Time Homeowners Assistance Program (FTHAP)** (Housing) — Down-payment/closing-cost help: first-time 5 yrs, U.S. citizen/PR, Guam resident 5 yrs, workshop + participating lender, transaction ≤ $420,000. Confirm availability. Free to apply. (671) 647-4143/46; 590 S Marine Corps Dr Ste 514, ITC Bldg, Tamuning. https://guamhousing.org/

**Guam American Job Center (HIREGUAM)** (Employment, Guam DOL) — Job listings, counseling, training, resume help, free computer/phone/fax. Register with HIREGUAM. Free. (671) 300-4555. http://dol.guam.gov/employment-and-training/job-seekers/

**Disaster Unemployment Assistance (DUA) — Guam** (Employment, Guam DOL) — Temporary benefits for workers jobless due to a declared disaster, not eligible for regular UI. Declaration-specific — wait for official filing announcement (normally 30-day window once announced). http://dol.guam.gov/dua/

**Guam REAL ID Driver's License / ID Card** (Transportation, DRT DMV) — REAL ID licenses/IDs; bring originals: identity, DOB, lawful status, SSN, residency (2 docs or mayor's letter within 30 days addressed to DRT, per conditions). Rolling. Fees apply. https://www.guamtax.com/help/realIDpolicy.html

**GBHWC — Outpatient Services** (Healthcare, Guam Behavioral Health and Wellness Center) — Mental health/substance-use outpatient care; first-time clients submit the Request and Appointment form, then intake at the main facility. Rolling. https://gbhwc.guam.gov/

**GBHWC Crisis Hotline and 988 — Guam** (Healthcare) — 24/7 crisis support. Always available. (671) 647-8833/8834; 988. Free. https://governor.guam.gov/press_release/leon-guerrero-tenorio-administration-addressing-mental-health-crisis/

**Guam Legal Services — Protection and Advocacy (P&A)** (Disability, GLSC-DLC) — Free disability-related legal advocacy; must be physically on Guam, no citizenship requirement. Phone/email/office intake, Mon-Fri 8-5; interpreters on request. (671) 477-9811/9812; 434 W O'Brien Dr Ste 102, Hagatna. https://www.guamlegalservices.org/node/34

**Guam Legal Services — Legal Assistance for Victims (LAV)** (Legal Aid, GLSC-DLC) — No-cost civil legal help (protective orders, divorce, custody) for survivors of sexual assault, DV, stalking, dating violence; income requirements. Free. (671) 477-9811; information@guamlsc.org. https://guamlegalservices.org/sites/default/files/press_release_lav.pdf

**Micronesian Legal Services Corporation — Guam Office** (Legal Aid) — Free civil legal help (no criminal); income below 125% (some cases 200%) of Hawaii poverty guidelines; appointment only Mon-Fri 8-5; staff languages English/Chamorro/Chuukese. Free. (671) 969-5504/5604/5704; guam@mlscnet.org; 238 Archbishop Flores St Ste 902, DNA Bldg, Hagatna. https://micronesianlegal.org/?page_id=340

**GRTA Fixed-Route Bus — Islandwide Public Transit** (Transportation) — Islandwide buses. Fares: discounted (students 6-18, seniors 55+, ADA) $0.50 ride / $1.50 day / $7.50 week / $25 month; regular $1.50 / $4 / $20 / $65. Passes at Barrigada, Dededo, Mangilao, Yigo mayor offices (cash or business check; not Tamuning). https://grta.guam.gov/how-do-i/bus-fares

**GRTA ADA Paratransit** (Transportation) — Island-wide complementary paratransit for ADA-certified riders, same days/hours as fixed route; reserve by phone (not email), generally day before; no service waitlist. Eligibility determination required. https://grta.guam.gov/services/paratransit-riders-guide/american-disabilities-ada-directives

**Guam Public Library System (GPLS) — Library Cards and Branch Services** (Education) — Free cards for residents 6+ with ID; main library Hagatna (254 Martyr St, (671) 475-4751), branches Agat/Barrigada/Dededo/Merizo/Yona + Bookmobile; print/scan, computers, programs. Branch hours limited (2024 staffing schedule) — call ahead. http://gpls.guam.gov/

**Guam Election Commission — Voter Registration and 2026 General Election** (Legal Aid) — General Election 2026-11-03; registration deadline 2026-10-19; early voting 2026-10-08–10-28; homebound voting from 2026-10-05; absentee request deadline 2026-10-31. Dates subject to change. Free. https://gec.guam.gov/2026-important-dates/

**Guam Small Business Development Center (SBDC)** (Small Business, UOG) — Free confidential 1-on-1 business counseling, low/no-cost training. (671) 735-2590; UOG School of Business & Public Administration, Mangilao. https://www.uog.edu/sbdc/

## Federal

**Guam VA Clinic (Pacific Islands Health Care System)** (Veterans, VA) — Primary care and veteran health services; free for eligible veterans. (800) 214-1306; 498 Chalan Palasyo, Agana Heights. https://www.va.gov/pacific-islands-health-care/locations/guam-va-clinic/

**Guam Vet Center — Readjustment Counseling** (Veterans, VA) — Free confidential non-medical counseling for eligible veterans, service members, families; no VA enrollment/rating required; walk-ins for many services; 24/7 call center. (877) 927-8387; crisis 988 press 1; 400 Route 8 Ste 301, Maite. https://www.va.gov/guam-vet-center/
