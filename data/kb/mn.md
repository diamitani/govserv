# Minnesota Government Services

Verified September 19, 2026. Phones appear on official pages unless marked (unverified). Apply links are official program pages (HTTP 200 verified).

## Metro

### Minneapolis metro

- **Minneapolis Public Housing Authority (MPHA)** (Housing) — Nearly 6,000 public and deeply affordable rental units across 42 high-rises, family homes, and townhomes in Minneapolis for households at or below 80% of Area Median Income. Demand is high and applicants join a waitlist. 612-342-1400. [mphaonline.org](https://mphaonline.org/mpha-housing/)
- **Metro Transit Transit Assistance Program (TAP)** (Transportation) — Reduced $1-per-ride transit fares on a Go-To Card for eligible lower-income riders across the seven-county Twin Cities metro (Minneapolis–St. Paul). Reduced $1 fares are also available for seniors 65+, youth 6–12, and Medicare card holders; service-connected disabled veterans ride free with a VA ID. 612-373-3333. [metrotransit.org](https://www.metrotransit.org/fares?intent=info&agency_id=0)

### St. Paul metro

- **Saint Paul Public Housing Agency** (Housing) — Public housing and Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) rental assistance in Saint Paul. STATUS: the Housing Choice Voucher waiting list is currently closed; public housing applications for scattered-site 5-bedroom homes opened July 21, 2025 until further notice (online only). 651-371-6135. [stpha.org](https://stpha.org/applicant-resources-and-information/how-to-apply)
- **Saint Paul Public Library Card** (Education) — Free library cards for Saint Paul residents — eCards available online for first-time cardholders — with books, eBooks, computers, homework help, and language-learning resources across 13 locations. Photo ID, proof of date of birth, and proof of current address required for full cards. [sppl.org](https://sppl.org/library-cards/)

### Rochester metro

- **Olmsted County Housing and Redevelopment Authority (HRA)** (Housing) — Administers rental assistance, homeowner and rental rehabilitation funds, and county-owned rental properties to expand affordable housing opportunities for Olmsted County residents, including Rochester. [olmstedcounty.gov](https://www.olmstedcounty.gov/government/county-boards-commissions/housing-redevelopment-authority-hra/olmsted-county-hra-mission)
- **Rochester Public Library Card** (Education) — Free library cards for Rochester and Olmsted County residents, with books, eBooks, computers, printing, and bookmobile service. Main library: 101 2nd Street SE, Rochester, MN 55904. 507-328-2300. [rplmn.org](https://rplmn.org/borrow/apply-for-a-library-card/)

### Duluth metro

- **Duluth Transit Authority (DTA)** (Transportation) — Fixed-route bus service in the Duluth area. Fares: peak $1.50, off-peak $0.75, day pass $3.00, 31-day adult pass $40.00. Reduced fares available for eligible riders with DTA approval. [duluthtransit.com](https://www.duluthtransit.com/home/getting-there/fares-and-passes/)
- **Duluth Public Library Card** (Education) — Free library cards for Duluth and Arrowhead Library System region residents. An Easy Access Card is available with just a government-issued photo ID (no proof of address required), giving access to digital resources and up to 4 physical items. [duluthlibrary.org](https://duluthlibrary.org/library-cards/get-a-library-card/)

### Bloomington metro

- **Bloomington Housing and Redevelopment Authority (HRA)** (Housing) — Affordable housing programs in Bloomington: Housing Choice Vouchers for households at or below 50% of Area Median Income, down payment assistance, home improvement loans, and senior home-maintenance services. [bloomingtonmn.gov](https://bloomingtonmn.gov/hr/hra-resources)


## County

### Hennepin County

- **Hennepin County Library Card** (Education) — Free library cards for Hennepin County residents and property owners, covering Minneapolis and Bloomington. Apply online for an immediate digital card, or in person at any of 41 locations; includes eBooks, audiobooks, computers, and 100+ online resources. [hclib.org](https://www.hclib.org/about/library-cards/get-a-card)


## State

- **Minnesota SNAP — Food Benefits** (Food) — Monthly food benefits for low-income Minnesota households to buy groceries, issued on an EBT card. Apply through your county human services agency. [dcyf.mn.gov](https://dcyf.mn.gov/how-snap-works-1)
- **Minnesota Family Investment Program (MFIP)** (Money & Taxes) — Minnesota's cash assistance program for low-income families with children, combining monthly cash and food benefits with employment services. Apply through your county human services agency. [edocs.dhs.state.mn.us](https://edocs.dhs.state.mn.us/lfserver/Public/DHS-5830-ENG)
- **Minnesota Unemployment Insurance** (Employment) — Temporary partial wage replacement for workers who lost a job through no fault of their own. Apply online through the Unemployment Insurance Minnesota system. [uimn.org](https://uimn.org/applicants/howapply/application-process/self-service.jsp)
- **Medical Assistance and MinnesotaCare** (Healthcare) — Free or low-cost health coverage: Medical Assistance for low-income Minnesotans, and MinnesotaCare for people who earn too much for MA but cannot afford other coverage. Apply through MNsure or your county human services agency. [mn.gov](https://mn.gov/dhs/health-care/apply/)
- **Emergency Medical Assistance (EMA)** (Healthcare) — Covers emergency medical services for people who do not qualify for Medical Assistance because of immigration status. [mn.gov](https://mn.gov/dhs/assets/emergency-medical-assistance_tcm1053-652616.pdf)
- **Medical Assistance for Employed Persons with Disabilities (MA-EPD)** (Disability) — Allows employed people with disabilities to pay a premium and keep Medical Assistance health coverage while working. [mn.gov](https://mn.gov/dhs/health-care/apply/over-65-blind-disabled/employed-with-disabilities/)
- **Child Care Assistance Program (CCAP)** (Family & Childcare) — Helps low-income families pay for child care while parents work, look for work, or attend school. Apply through your county human services agency. [dhs.state.mn.us](https://www.dhs.state.mn.us/main/idcplg?IdcService=GET_FILE&dID=167173&dDocName=MNDHS-075662&allowInterrupt=1)
- **Minnesota Energy Assistance Program (EAP)** (Utilities) — Helps low-income households pay heating and electric bills and repair or replace heating equipment. Administered by local community action agencies statewide. [mn.gov](https://mn.gov/commerce/energy/consumer-assistance/energy-assistance-program/media-toolkit.jsp)
- **Minnesota Working Family Credit** (Money & Taxes) — Refundable state tax credit for working individuals and families with low to moderate income. Claim it on your Minnesota income tax return. [revenue.state.mn.us](https://revenue.state.mn.us/working-family-credit)
- **Minnesota Homeowner Property Tax Refund** (Money & Taxes) — Annual state refund for homeowners whose property taxes are high relative to household income. File with the Minnesota Department of Revenue. [revenue.state.mn.us](https://www.revenue.state.mn.us/filing-property-tax-refund)
- **Minnesota Renter's Property Tax Refund** (Money & Taxes) — Annual state refund for renters who pay property taxes indirectly through rent. File with the Minnesota Department of Revenue. [revenue.state.mn.us](https://www.revenue.state.mn.us/filing-property-tax-refund)
- **Minnesota Driver and Vehicle Services (DVS)** (Transportation) — Driver's licenses, permits, and state ID cards, including REAL ID. Renew and manage many services through the DVS online portal; apply in person at DVS exam stations and deputy registrar offices. [dps.mn.gov](https://dps.mn.gov/news/online-portal-lets-you-manage-dvs-services-your-schedule)
- **Register a Business in Minnesota** (Small Business) — Register a business entity — LLC, corporation, assumed name, or nonprofit — with the Minnesota Secretary of State, and find startup guidance for new businesses. [sos.mn.gov:443](https://sos.mn.gov:443/business-liens/business-help/how-to-register-your-business/)
- **Minnesota Small Business Development Centers (SBDC)** (Small Business) — No-cost, confidential business consulting and low- or no-cost training through nine regional centers statewide, helping entrepreneurs start, grow, and finance businesses. DEED is the lead center with the U.S. Small Business Administration. [mn.gov](https://mn.gov/mmb-stat/documents/budget/research-and-data/summary-of-agencies-programs-activities/employment-and-economic-development.pdf)
- **Minnesota Free School Meals for Kids** (Food) — Free school breakfast and lunch for students at participating Minnesota public and charter schools. [education.mn.gov](https://education.mn.gov/MDE/dse/FNS/SNP/free/)
- **Minnesota WIC — Women, Infants, and Children** (Food) — Nutrition education, breastfeeding support, health and social service referrals, and monthly food benefits for pregnant and postpartum women, infants, and children under age 5. [health.state.mn.us](https://www.health.state.mn.us/people/wic/index.html)
- **Minnesota Aging Pathways** (Seniors) — Information and assistance connecting older adults, caregivers, and families to aging services across Minnesota. [mn.gov](https://mn.gov/board-on-aging/direct-services/aging-pathways/)
- **Minnesota Department of Veterans Affairs (MDVA)** (Veterans) — State veterans benefits and services, including help filing VA claims, state veterans homes, state veterans cemeteries, and outreach programs. [mn.gov](https://mn.gov/mdva/about/what-we-do.jsp)
- **Minnesota Veteran's Relief Grant** (Veterans) — Grants to help Minnesota veterans facing unexpected financial hardship with essential needs. Check the current overview for eligibility and application steps. [mn.gov](https://mn.gov/mdva/assets/2024-07-22-vrg-overview_tcm1066-638308.pdf)
- **Minnesota Vocational Rehabilitation Services (VRS)** (Disability) — Employment services for Minnesotans with disabilities, including career counseling, training, and job placement support. [mn.gov](https://mn.gov/deed/job-seekers/find-vrs/)
- **Minnesota Housing Start Up and Step Up** (Housing) — First-time and repeat homebuyer loan programs with down payment and closing cost assistance, offered through participating Minnesota lenders. [mnhousing.gov](https://www.mnhousing.gov/get/mhfa_1043060)
- **LawHelpMN — Free Legal Help** (Legal Aid) — Statewide directory and self-help resources for free civil legal help in Minnesota, including housing, family, consumer, and immigration issues. 1-877-696-6529. [lawhelpmn.org](https://lawhelpmn.org/sites/default/files/2025-10/2025%20Tenants%20Rights%20Booklet.pdf)
- **MNsure — Minnesota Health Insurance Marketplace** (Healthcare) — Minnesota's official health insurance marketplace to compare and enroll in qualified health plans, and to apply for Medical Assistance and MinnesotaCare. [mnsure.org](https://www.mnsure.org)
- **Minnesota Office of New Americans (ONA)** (Immigration) — State office established in 2023 (Minn. Stat. § 116J.4231) to support immigrant and refugee integration — reducing employment barriers and connecting New Americans with state services, employers, and community organizations. [mn.gov](https://mn.gov/deed/assets/ona-report-2025-acc_tcm1045-701555.pdf)
- **CareerForce Minnesota** (Employment) — Minnesota's workforce development system: job search tools, career planning, training resources, and in-person help at CareerForce locations statewide. [careerforcemn.com](https://www.careerforcemn.com)

## Federal

- **Medicare Enrollment** (Healthcare) — Federal health insurance for people 65 and older, certain younger people with disabilities, and people with End-Stage Renal Disease. Compare plans and enroll at medicare.gov. [medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov/)
- **VA Minneapolis Health Care** (Veterans) — The VA Minneapolis Health Care System provides health care services to eligible Minnesota veterans. Apply for VA health care enrollment online. [va.gov](https://www.va.gov/minneapolis-health-care/)
- **SBA Local Assistance — Minnesota** (Small Business) — Find SBA district offices, Small Business Development Centers, SCORE mentors, and Women's Business Centers in Minnesota, plus federal loan and contracting programs. [sba.gov](https://www.sba.gov/local-assistance/find)
- **FCC Lifeline Program** (Utilities) — Federal discount on monthly phone or broadband service for qualifying low-income households. Apply through the National Verifier. [lifelinesupport.org](https://www.lifelinesupport.org/)
- **FAFSA — Federal Student Aid** (Education) — Free Application for Federal Student Aid: the single application for federal grants, work-study, and student loans for Minnesota students attending college or career school. [studentaid.gov](https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa)
- **USAJOBS — Federal Jobs** (Employment) — Official job board for federal government positions, including openings in Minnesota. Create an account to search and apply. [usajobs.gov](https://www.usajobs.gov/)
- **HUD Minneapolis Field Office** (Housing) — HUD's Minneapolis field office serving Minnesota — a contact point for fair housing complaints and HUD-approved housing counseling resources. (612) 370-3000. [lawhelpmn.org](https://lawhelpmn.org/sites/default/files/2025-10/2025%20Tenants%20Rights%20Booklet.pdf)
