# Nebraska Services Guide

39 verified services covering Omaha / Douglas County, Lincoln / Lancaster County, Bellevue / Sarpy County, Grand Island / Hall County, Kearney / Buffalo County, statewide Nebraska, and federal programs. All entries verified 2026-09-19.

## Metro

### Omaha

**Metro Transit bus and MOBY paratransit** — Metro Transit (Omaha). Fixed-route local buses, ORBT rapid transit along Dodge Street, MyRide OMA trip planner, and MOBY ADA door-to-door paratransit for ADA-eligible residents and visitors. K-12 students ride fare-free on any route (K-12 Rides Free pilot). Reduced fares may be available for qualifying seniors and riders with disabilities — confirm current fare programs with Metro before traveling. (402) 341-0800 (unverified). Transportation. https://www.ometro.com/

**Omaha Housing Authority public housing and Housing Choice Vouchers** — STATUS: mixed. Since 1/1/2025, public housing waitlist open only for 1-bedroom Senior Towers apartments to applicants 62+. Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) waitlist currently closed. (402) 444-6900; 1823 Harney St, Omaha, NE 68102. Housing. Free to apply. https://ohauthority.org/contact/

**Omaha Public Library and Do Space technology labs** — Public library service with borrowing and programs, plus Do Space labs at Central Library (7205 Dodge St, 2nd floor) and Abrahams Branch (5111 N 90th St): 3D printing, laser cutting/engraving, podcast and media studios, memory lab for digitizing old media, sewing, specialty printing. All ages welcome; library cards available at OPL locations (confirm residency requirements with OPL). (402) 444-4800 (unverified). Education. Free. https://omahalibrary.org/do-space/

### Lincoln

**StarTran bus service, reduced fares, and paratransit** — City of Lincoln. Wheelchair-accessible fixed routes; 60-cent reduced fare for seniors 62+ and people with disabilities (Senior Saver/Go For Less/Medicare ID); $8 low-income 31-day pass at or below 200% FPL; door-to-door paratransit $2.50 each way via the League of Human Dignity; UNL students/faculty/staff ride free with UNL pass. Six days a week, no Sundays. StarTran: (402) 476-1234; paratransit registration: (402) 441-7871; 710 J St, Lincoln. Transportation. Cash fare $1.25; day pass $2.50. https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/City/Departments/LTU/StarTran/How-to-Ride/FAQ

**Lincoln Housing Authority affordable housing** — Public housing and Housing Choice Voucher programs; apply online via LHA's Apply for Housing process. Waiting lists open and close periodically — check current status before applying. Income-eligible households under HUD limits. (402) 434-5500 (unverified). Housing. Free to apply. https://l-housing.com/

### Bellevue

**Bellevue Specialized Transportation bus service** — City of Bellevue Human Services. Door-to-door bus for elderly or disabled Bellevue residents (doctor appointments, groceries, Senior Activity Center). Pre-register and reserve rides in advance; trips to Bellevue and Omaha destinations Monday–Friday. (402) 293-3138. Transportation. $2 each way in town; $4 out of town. https://www.bellevue.net/299/Programs

**Bellevue Human Services emergency assistance and food pantry referral** — Short-term help with utilities, rent, or groceries for Bellevue residents facing unexpected income interruption, funded by donations. Also food pantry referrals (food bags + grocery vouchers), holiday toy assistance for Sarpy County families with children 12 and under (applications each November), and referrals to other programs. Appointments: (402) 293-3138, Mon–Fri 8 AM–4:30 PM; bring ID + proof of residency. Money & Taxes. Free. https://www.bellevue.net/299/Programs

### Grand Island

**CRANE Public Transit** — City of Grand Island. Portal-to-portal, demand-response public transit for the Grand Island urbanized area. All buses ADA accessible; a personal attendant may ride free. Book at least 24 hours ahead; same-day trips when space allows. (308) 646-0069 (unverified). Transportation. Per-ride fare. https://www.crane-transit.com/

### Kearney (Buffalo County)

Kearney-area services are covered at the county level — see **Buffalo County Veteran Services** under County below.

## County

### Douglas County

**Douglas County Health Department immunization clinic** — Vaccines at no cost (donations accepted) at 1111 S 41st St, Ste 210, Omaha. Childhood/adolescent clinics every Tuesday 8:30–11 AM by appointment; adult clinic Mon–Sat by appointment (schedule varies). No travel immunizations. Children birth–18 who are uninsured/underinsured/Medicaid-eligible/AI-AN; adults 19+ who are uninsured. (402) 444-6163. Healthcare. Free. https://www.douglascountyhealth.com/clinics-services/immunization-clinic

### Lancaster County

**Lancaster County Veterans Service Office** — Help applying for state and federal veterans benefits, veteran advocacy, and emergency financial assistance through the County Veterans' Aid Fund (food, shelter, clothing, medical, funeral) for qualifying veterans, spouses, and dependents. Victory Park Campus, 600 S 70th St, Bldg #2, Ste #200, Lincoln. Call to schedule: (402) 441-7361 (unverified). Veterans. Free. https://www.lancaster.ne.gov/484

### Sarpy County

**Sarpy/Cass Health Department immunizations** — Immunization clinics and referrals for Sarpy and Cass County residents, including partner clinics, VNA home-bound immunizations, and the Vaccines for Children program. Office: 701 Olson Dr, Ste 101, Papillion, Mon–Fri 8 AM–4:30 PM. (402) 339-4334. Healthcare. Free or reduced cost for eligible children. https://www.schd.ne.gov/what-we-do/programs-services/access-to-services/immunizations.html

### Hall County

**CRANE Public Transit** — See Grand Island above (serves the Hall County urbanized area). https://www.crane-transit.com/

### Buffalo County

**Buffalo County Veteran Services** — County veterans' service office created by state statute to help veterans and their dependents in times of need and to obtain earned benefits. Claims assistance and referrals for federal, state, and county veterans benefits. Officer: Matthew Starkey. PO Box 1270, Kearney, NE 68848. (308) 236-1253; veterans@buffalocounty.ne.gov. Office hours Mon–Fri 8 AM–5 PM; appointments preferred, walk-ins welcome. Veterans. Free. https://buffalocounty.ne.gov/county-offices/veteran-services

## State

**Nebraska SNAP food assistance** — DHHS. Monthly food benefits on an EBT card for low-income households. Apply via iServe Nebraska. STATUS: on 6/22/2026 a federal court vacated USDA approval of Nebraska's food-restriction waivers; prior soda/energy-drink restrictions were being removed. (800) 383-4278; Lincoln (402) 323-3900; Omaha (402) 595-1258. Food. Free to apply. https://iserve.nebraska.gov/

**Nebraska Medicaid and CHIP** — DHHS. Health coverage for children, adults 19–64, pregnant people, parents/caretakers, former foster youth, adults 65+, and people meeting SSA disability guidelines; CHIP and 599 CHIP for qualifying children. Apply any time via iServe; newly eligible children get one year of continuous coverage. NOTE: iServe states that starting May 1, able-bodied adults may need to complete work requirements to keep or qualify for Medicaid; DHHS will contact affected members. (855) 632-7633; Lincoln (402) 473-7000; Omaha (402) 595-1178. Healthcare. Free to apply. https://iserve.nebraska.gov/

**Nebraska Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP)** — DHHS. Heating/cooling bill help for households at or below 150% FPL. 2025–2026 program year: 10/1/2025–9/30/2026 (DEADLINE 2026-09-30). Heating: Oct–Mar; cooling: Jun–Aug; crisis help year-round. Apply via iServe. (800) 383-4278; Lincoln (402) 323-3900; Omaha (402) 595-1258. Utilities. Free. https://iserve.nebraska.gov/

**Nebraska WIC** — DHHS. Nutritious foods, nutrition education, screenings/referrals, breastfeeding support for pregnant/postpartum/breastfeeding women, infants, and children up to age five. New federal income guidelines published 7/1/2026 increased eligibility; current Medicaid/SNAP/ADC recipients are income-eligible. Apply at local WIC clinics. Food. Free. https://dhhs.ne.gov/Pages/WIC-About.aspx

**Nebraska Child Care Subsidy** — DHHS. Help paying for DHHS-approved child care while parents work, attend school/training, participate in Employment First, attend medical visits, or are unable to provide care due to illness/injury. Generally children 12 and younger (up to 19 for special need). Apply via iServe + phone interview; provider approval can take up to six weeks, payments not backdated. (800) 383-4278 (unverified). Family & Childcare. Free to apply. https://iserve.nebraska.gov/

**Nebraska Aid to Dependent Children (TANF)** — DHHS. Monthly cash assistance for low-income families with minor children (18 and younger) for rent, utilities, food, clothing, and necessities. Employment First participation may be required. Apply via iServe. (800) 383-4278 (unverified). Money & Taxes. Free. https://iserve.nebraska.gov/

**Nebraska Unemployment Insurance** — Nebraska Department of Labor. Weekly benefits for workers who lost jobs through no fault of their own. File through NEworks (account + searchable résumé required); file promptly — claims begin in the filing week and earlier weeks can't be paid. Processing up to 21 days; weekly certifications required; first eligible week is an unpaid waiting week. (402) 458-2500; NDOL.UnemploymentHelp@nebraska.gov. Employment. Free. https://neworks.nebraska.gov/

**NEworks job search and training services** — Nebraska Department of Labor. Free job search across thousands of Nebraska openings, résumé builder, unemployment filing, training services, and labor-market/wage data. Registration required for full features. Employment. Free. https://neworks.nebraska.gov/

**Nebraska Vocational Rehabilitation employment services** — Nebraska VR. Help for people with disabilities to prepare for, find, and keep jobs: counseling, training, placement, and work supports. Apply via the VR site; eligibility determined after application. Disability. Free. https://vr.nebraska.gov/

**Legal Aid of Nebraska** — Free civil legal help for low-income Nebraskans: housing, debt/finances, family matters, public benefits. Apply online via LawHelpNE or by phone. Applying does not guarantee representation. (402) 348-1069. Legal Aid. Free. https://lawhelpne.legalaidofnebraska.org/

**Disability Rights Nebraska protection and advocacy** — Nebraska's designated Protection & Advocacy system: legal representation, information, and referrals for people with disabilities and families. (402) 413-2016; toll-free (800) 422-6691. Disability. Free. https://www.disabilityrightsnebraska.org/

**Nebraska Telephone Assistance Program / Lifeline** — Nebraska Public Service Commission. Federal Lifeline + Nebraska discount lowering monthly phone bills for qualifying households (Medicaid, SNAP, SSI, federal public housing, veterans/survivors pension, or income at/below 135% FPL). One benefit per household; bill must include applicant's name. Apply through the National Verifier + Nebraska Citizenship Attestation. psc.ntap@nebraska.gov; Lifeline Support Center (800) 234-9473. Utilities. Free to apply. https://www.lifelinesupport.org/

**Nebraska Opportunity Grant** — Nebraska Coordinating Commission for Postsecondary Education. Need-based aid for Nebraska residents attending Nebraska colleges, based on FAFSA/Student Aid Index, awarded through participating schools. No separate state application — complete the FAFSA and school's application. Education. Free; grants not repaid. https://ccpe.nebraska.gov/nebraska-opportunity-grant-nog

**EducationQuest college planning and scholarships** — Free college planning, FAFSA help, scholarship searches, and need-based scholarships/school grants. Offices in Omaha, Lincoln, Kearney, Scottsbluff. Omaha (402) 391-4033 (unverified); Lincoln (402) 475-5222 (unverified); Kearney (308) 234-6310 (unverified). Education. Free. https://educationquest.org/about-us/

**Nebraska Veterans Aid Fund** — Nebraska Department of Veterans' Affairs. Temporary emergency aid for qualifying veterans, spouses, dependents when normal living is disrupted and other resources aren't immediately available: food, fuel, shelter, transportation, daycare, clothing, funeral, medical/surgical. Since 9/3/2025, apply through a State Service Officer as well as county or recognized veterans-organization officers. Veterans. Free. https://veterans.nebraska.gov/nva

**Nebraska Aging and Disability Resource Center** — DHHS. Information/referral, options counseling, transition counseling, benefits assistance, mobility training for Nebraskans 60+, people with disabilities of any age, caregivers, and families. (844) 843-6364. Seniors. Free. https://dhhs.ne.gov/pages/aging-and-disability-resource-center.aspx

**NebFile free state tax filing** — Nebraska Department of Revenue. Free online filing for most 2025 Nebraska resident individual returns. STATUS: NOW OPEN as of 9/19/2026. Full-year 2025 NE residents with valid email; no amended returns. Money & Taxes. Free. https://revenue.nebraska.gov/individuals/nebfile-individuals

**Nebraska DMV online services** — License/ID renewals and replacements, some new licenses/permits, driver records, registration renewal, reinstatement checks for eligible transactions. Some people must visit an office (noncitizens needing record checks, name changes). Transaction/portal fees apply. Transportation. https://www.nebraska.gov/dmv/dlr/restrictions.html

**Nebraska Secretary of State business filings** — Business entity formation, registration, and related filings; eDelivery requires a signed PDF upload. Filing fees vary. (402) 471-4079. Small Business. https://sos.nebraska.gov/business-services/forms-and-fee-information-old?quot=

**NIFA affordable homeownership programs** — Nebraska Investment Finance Authority. Homebuyer programs through participating lenders plus an online loan eligibility tool. Program names, rates, and requirements change — confirm current details with NIFA or a participating lender. Housing. https://nifa.org/

**Nebraska 211 community resource referrals** — Free, confidential referrals to food, shelter, utility assistance, transportation, legal/disaster services, and other community resources. Dial 2-1-1 (unverified) or search online. Food. Free. https://ne211.org/

**Nebraska Homestead Exemption** — Department of Revenue, Property Assessment Division. Property tax relief for owners 65+, qualified disabled individuals, and qualified disabled veterans/surviving spouses (income and valuation limits by category). STATUS: closed. Tax year 2026 applications (Form 458) were due to the county assessor by 6/30/2026. (888) 475-5101; (402) 471-5962. Money & Taxes. Free to apply. https://revenue.nebraska.gov/sites/default/files/doc/news-release/pad/2026/NewsRelease_HomesteadExemption_2026.pdf

## Federal

**988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** — Free, confidential 24/7/365 support from trained counselors by call, text, or chat for mental health struggles, emotional distress, substance use concerns, or anyone who needs to talk; also supports friends and loved ones. Call or text 988. Healthcare. Free. https://988lifeline.org/

**Social Security retirement, disability, and SSI** — Social Security Administration. Retirement benefits, SSDI, and SSI applications filed online. (800) 772-1213 (unverified). Money & Taxes. Free. https://www.ssa.gov/apply

**Medicare health insurance** — Federal health insurance for people 65+ meeting citizenship/residency requirements, and some under 65 with disability, ESRD, or ALS. Some enroll automatically via Social Security; others must sign up. (800) 633-4227 (unverified). Healthcare. https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare

**VA disability compensation** — U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. Monthly tax-free payments for service-connected disabilities. File online, by mail (VA Form 21-526EZ), in person, by fax, or with an accredited representative; evidence due within a year of filing. (800) 827-1000 (unverified). Veterans. Free. https://www.va.gov/disability/how-to-file-claim/

**HUD-approved housing counseling** — U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development. Find a HUD-approved counselor for buying, renting, avoiding foreclosure, or resolving housing problems. (800) 569-4287 (unverified). Housing. Free or low-cost. https://www.hud.gov/counseling
