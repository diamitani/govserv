## State

- **211 NH — Community Resource Helpline** — Statewide connection to resources from trained Information and Referral Specialists, available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year: food, housing, utility assistance, substance use resources, legal services, mental health support, and more — 211 — [211nh.org](https://211nh.org/)
- **NH Works — Career Centers & Job Services** — Career centers and workforce services across New Hampshire: job matching through the state's Job Match System, unemployment services, Trade Adjustment Assistance, veterans services, job fairs, and labor market information for job seekers and employers. — [www.nhworks.org](https://www.nhworks.org/job-seekers/nh-works-centers/)
- **New Hampshire Housing — Homeownership & Rental Assistance** — Statewide programs making homeownership more affordable — mortgages, refinancing, and downpayment/closing-cost assistance (cash assistance amounts advertised include $5,000, $10,000, or $15,000 with an NH Housing mortgage) — plus rental assistance through HUD's Housing Choice Voucher (HCV) program for very low-income households, and emergency housing resources. — [www.nhhfa.org](https://www.nhhfa.org/)
- **New Hampshire Legal Assistance — Free Civil Legal Aid** — Free civil legal aid for people in New Hampshire, primarily in housing (eviction, foreclosure, property tax deeding); domestic violence and family law (protective orders, divorce, parenting rights); and public benefits (disability, unemployment) — 1 (800) 639-5290 — [www.nhla.org](https://www.nhla.org/)
