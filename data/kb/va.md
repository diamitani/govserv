## Metro

### Virginia Beach / Norfolk / Chesapeake
- **HRT Student Freedom Pass** — free unlimited HRT bus, light rail, and ferry rides for ages 13–17 across Hampton Roads, including Chesapeake, Norfolk, and Virginia Beach — enrollment via program page; questions: **757-222-6100** — [gohrt.com](https://gohrt.com/programs/student-freedom-pass/?gad_source=1)
- **VB311 (City of Virginia Beach)** — city service requests and inquiries — dial 311 or **757-385-3111**; public-safety non-emergency is **757-385-5000** — [virginiabeach.gov](https://virginiabeach.gov/connect/blog/make-the-right-call-learn-the-three-ways-to-reach-help-in-virginia-beach)
- **MyNorfolk / Norfolk Cares Center** — City of Norfolk online system for accepting and tracking non-emergency city service requests; no phone verified on the city page — [Norfolk.gov](https://www.Norfolk.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=4914&ARC=9699)
- **Legal Aid Society of Eastern Virginia (LASEV)** — free civil legal help for low-income residents of Chesapeake, Norfolk, Virginia Beach, and other eastern jurisdictions — **757-627-5423** or statewide **1-866-534-5243** — [vsb.org access guide](https://vsb.org/BU/Site/legal-help/access-guide.aspx)
- **NRHA Housing Waitlists (Norfolk)** — Housing Choice Voucher and public housing waitlists are NOT open; a listed PBV/LIHTC opening ended Aug 3 — **757-314-2058**, TDD **800-545-1833** — [nrha.us](https://nrha.us/housing/apply/waitlists/)

## State

**Food**
- **SNAP (food assistance)** — monthly food benefits on an EBT card for low-income households; apply via CommonHelp — **833-522-5582** — note: VaCAP ended 2026-06-01; sweetened carbonated beverages ineligible from 2026-11-01 — [commonhelp.virginia.gov](https://commonhelp.virginia.gov/)
- **WIC Nutrition Program** — nutrition education, breastfeeding support, and food benefits for pregnant women, new mothers, infants, and kids under 5; apply through your local health department/WIC office — [vdh.virginia.gov](https://www.vdh.virginia.gov/central-virginia/wic/)

**Health coverage**
- **Cardinal Care (Virginia Medicaid)** — free or low-cost health coverage; five managed care plans as of July 2025 — apply via CommonHelp or **1-855-242-8282** (TDD **1-888-221-1590**); call center closed Saturdays since 2026-04-04 — [coverva.dmas.virginia.gov](https://coverva.dmas.virginia.gov/apply/how-to-apply/)

**Money & family**
- **TANF Cash Assistance and VIEW** — temporary cash aid for families with children, plus VIEW/FEP employment services, Diversionary and Emergency Assistance — apply via CommonHelp — **855-635-4370** — [dss.virginia.gov](https://www.dss.virginia.gov/relief/tanf/apply/)
- **Child Care Subsidy Program** — helps families pay for child care while parents work, job-hunt, or train; income up to 85% of state median income for families with young children; 30-day processing, minimum 12 months of eligibility — apply online/by mail/in person at your local DSS — [childcare.virginia.gov](https://www.childcare.virginia.gov/home/showdocument?id=57736&t=638633886494375242)

**Work**
- **Unemployment Insurance (VEC)** — temporary benefits after job loss through no fault of your own; file online — ID.me verification, photo ID and SSN needed; file within the same week — **866-832-2363**; status line **800-897-5630** — [uidirect.vec.virginia.gov](https://uidirect.vec.virginia.gov/CSS/CSSLogon.htm)
- **Vocational Rehabilitation Services (DARS)** — job counseling, training, and employment supports for Virginians with disabilities; self-referral accepted — **1-800-552-5019** (TTY **1-800-464-9950**) — [dars.virginia.gov](https://dars.virginia.gov/employment-services/for-individuals/)

**Veterans**
- **DVS Benefits Services** — free help with benefit claims for veterans, service members, and families at 38 offices statewide — [dvs.virginia.gov](https://www.dvs.virginia.gov/benefits-services)
- **VMSDEP** — education benefits (up to eight semesters) for qualifying spouses/dependents of service members — **844-838-7838**, vmsdep@dvs.virginia.gov — [dvs.virginia.gov](https://www.dvs.virginia.gov/benefits-services/education/virginia-military-survivors-dependents-education-program-vmsdep)

**Housing & utilities**
- **Down Payment Assistance Grant (Virginia Housing)** — grant (no repayment) for first-time buyers with a qualifying Virginia Housing loan — [virginiahousing.com](https://virginiahousing.com/homebuyers/down-payment-assistance-grant)
- **Energy Assistance Program** — fuel, crisis, cooling, and weatherization help for low-income households; seasonal windows — **855-635-4370** — [dss.virginia.gov](https://dss.virginia.gov/relief/ea/)

**Help finding help**
- **211 Virginia** — free, confidential statewide helpline for food, housing, utilities, health care, and more — 24/7/365 — dial 211 or **1-800-230-6977**; English/Spanish, interpretation in 150+ languages — [211virginia.org](https://211virginia.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/211_FAQs.pdf)
- **Legal Aid Access Guide (Virginia State Bar)** — statewide legal aid routing — **1-866-534-5243**; online guide last updated 2023 — [vsb.org](https://vsb.org/BU/Site/legal-help/access-guide.aspx)
- **Driver's License and ID Renewal (DMV)** — renew online/in person/by mail; REAL ID (in-person visit required) has been needed for domestic flights and secure federal facilities since 2025-05-07 — [dmv.virginia.gov](https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/e-renew-online)

## Federal

- **Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI) and SSI** — monthly benefits for people whose disability prevents work: SSDI for insured workers (Medicare after 24 months), SSI for aged/blind/disabled people with limited income and resources (Medicaid included) — apply any time — [ssa.gov Red Book](https://www.ssa.gov/redbook/eng/overview-disability.htm?tl=2)
- **Lifeline Phone and Internet Discount (FCC)** — up to $9.25/month off phone/internet/bundled service for low-income households (≤135% FPL or SNAP/Medicaid/SSI/FPHA/veterans pension); one per household — **1-800-234-9473** — [fcc.gov](https://www.fcc.gov/lifeline-consumers)
- **VA Hampton Health Care Enrollment** — enroll in VA health care to get care across Hampton Roads; apply online/by phone/by mail/in person — bring DD214, disability award letters, prior-year income info, other coverage IDs — **757-722-9961** (unverified) — [va.gov](https://www.va.gov/hampton-health-care/register-for-care/)
- **HUD-Approved Housing Counseling** — free/low-cost advice on buying, renting, default, foreclosure, and credit — call **800-569-4287** or search by zip code — [hud.gov](http://www.hud.gov/hud-partners/housing-national-agencies)
