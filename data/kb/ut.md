## Metro

### Provo

**Home Purchase Plus — Down Payment Assistance (Provo)** — A 0% interest, deferred-payment loan for first-time homebuyers purchasing in Provo City. Qualified applicants may receive the mortgage-required down payment (5% for conventional or 3.5% for FHA) plus estimated closing costs, minus $1,000 of their own money, plus an additional 5%/3.5% — capped at $40,000. No payments are due while the home is owned and occupied as the primary residence; a $5,000 prepayment penalty applies if sold or vacated within two years. Currently accepting applications — applicants must have a property under contract before applying, and apply through neighborly.provo.org under Down Payment Assistance. Eligibility: First-time homebuyer (no ownership in a principal residence in the last 3 years); Income-eligible per the program's HUD income chart; Minimum mid-credit score of 650; On current job for at least 6 months; Liquid assets of $15,000 or less at closing; Completed pre-homeownership counseling; Home price within HUD purchase-price limits (check current limit). Rolling — Currently accepting applications (verified 2026-09-19) — applicants must have a property under contract before applying. Apply: https://www.provo.org/departments/development/cdbg-home/home-purchase-plus-program. Cost: Free to apply.

**Provo 311 — Report An Issue** — Report non-emergency city issues in Provo — potholes, missed trash, streetlights, and more — through the city's online Report An Issue portal with a map-based service request, or dial 3-1-1. For emergencies, dial 911; for police or animal control response, call 801-852-6210. Eligibility: Anyone reporting a non-emergency city issue in Provo. Rolling — Reports accepted year-round through the online portal. Phone: 801-852-6210. Apply: https://www.provo.gov/215/Report-An-Issue. Cost: Free.

### Salt Lake City

**mySLC — Salt Lake City Service Requests** — Salt Lake City's mySLC portal is the city's 311-equivalent: request services, report non-emergency issues (graffiti removal, illegal dumping, abandoned belongings, homeless encampments, needles or biowaste), share comments with city officials, and track submissions with city staff directly. Graffiti cleanup is typically within 24 business hours. Eligibility: Anyone in Salt Lake City needing city services or reporting a non-emergency issue. Rolling — Reports accepted year-round through mySLC. Apply: https://www.slc.gov/cleancity/report-or-request-service/. Cost: Free.

### West Jordan

**West Jordan Senior Housing** — A 65-unit one-bedroom development for seniors 62 and older, developed under HUD's Section 202 program. Rent with utilities included at 30% of monthly income. Amenities include laundry rooms, a craft/exercise room, library, meeting room with kitchen, covered parking, and a walking trail; the project is near the West Jordan Senior Center, Gene Fullmer Recreation Center, and a TRAX station. Contact the Utah Nonprofit Housing Corporation at 801-364-6117 for details and an application. Eligibility: Age 62 or older. Rolling — Contact the Utah Nonprofit Housing Corporation for current availability. Phone: 801-364-6117. Apply: https://www.westjordan.utah.gov/community-development/affordable-housing/. Cost: Rent set at 30% of monthly income, utilities included.

Also handles communicable disease response, food establishment inspections, and vital records. Eligibility: Open to all residents of Salt Lake County. **UTA Reduced Fare FAREPAY Card** — UTA's Reduced Fare FAREPAY Card gives qualifying riders a flat $1.00 one-way fare (regular $2.50) with daily and weekly fare caps, on UTA buses, TRAX, FrontRunner, S-Line streetcar, and UTA On Demand across Salt Lake, Utah, and other Wasatch Front counties. Apply online, verify eligibility, and receive the card after approval. Eligibility: Youth ages 6–18; Seniors ages 65+; Persons with disabilities (Medicare or healthcare professional verification); Income-qualified riders: member of a participating program or household income at 200% or less of federal poverty guidelines (e.g., $31,920/year for a household of one). Rolling — Apply any time; income-based eligibility must be renewed annually. Phone: 801-743-3882. Apply: https://rideuta.com/Fares-And-Passes/Individual-Fares/Reduced-Fare. Cost: $1.00 per one-way trip (regular fare $2.50); capped at $2/day and $8/week for local service.

## State

**SNAP (Food Assistance) — Utah** — The Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program helps Utah families buy food. If you qualify, you receive an Electronic Benefit Transfer (EBT/Horizon) card for groceries at authorized SNAP stores and farmers markets. Eligibility is based on household size, monthly income, expenses, assets, and citizenship status. Eligibility: Utah resident; Low-income household; eligibility varies by household size, monthly income, expenses, assets, and citizenship status; Example on the official page: a family of four with monthly income under $2,500 after deductions could qualify. Rolling — Apply any time; benefits start the day of application. SNAP interviews can be done by phone. Phone: 1-866-435-7414, 800-997-4444. Apply: https://mydoorway.utah.gov/food-assistance-snap/. Cost: Free.

**Utah Medicaid — Medical Assistance** — Medicaid is a state and federal program that covers medical expenses for low-income individuals and families, including family Medicaid, Adult Expansion (ages 19–64), pregnant women, children, and CHIP for kids who do not qualify for Medicaid. Eligibility varies by household size and monthly income. Eligibility: Utah resident; Income limits vary by program; examples on the official page: Adult Expansion — family of four at $3,458/month or less; pregnant women — $3,614/month or less; CHIP — $5,200/month or less; U.S. citizen or qualified non-citizen (Medicaid programs). Rolling — Apply any time; no open enrollment for Medicaid. Phone: 1-866-435-7414. Apply: https://mydoorway.utah.gov/medical-medicaid/. Cost: Free to apply; CHIP may have quarterly premiums and co-pays.

**Utah WIC — Women, Infants and Children** — WIC provides nutrition education, breastfeeding support, and supplemental foods to pregnant women, mothers, infants, and children up to their 5th birthday. Families get a debit-like WIC card for healthy groceries at authorized stores. Services are delivered through local health departments statewide. Eligibility: Utah resident (citizenship not required); Pregnant woman, postpartum/breastfeeding mother, infant, or child under 5; Income guidelines apply; nutritional risk determined at the clinic appointment. Rolling — Apply any time; schedule an appointment at your local WIC clinic. Phone: 1-877-WIC-KIDS, 1-855-784-5584. Apply: https://wic.utah.gov/. Cost: Free.

**HEAT — Home Energy Assistance Target (LIHEAP)** — HEAT is Utah's Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Program — it helps pay monthly power and gas bills for eligible low-income households year-round, while federal funds last. Priority goes to households with elderly (60+), disabled, or young children (under 6) members; crisis help is available if you are in danger of shut-off. Eligibility: Utah resident; Total household income at or below 150% of the federal poverty level (official example: family of four at $4,125/month or less); Household responsible for paying home energy costs; At least one adult (18+) or emancipated member in the household; At least one U.S. citizen or qualified non-citizen in the household. Priority application window opens — Program year begins Oct 1 each year and runs through Sep 30 (or until federal LIHEAP funds are exhausted). Applications from households with disabled, elderly (60+), or children under 6 accepted Oct 1; general public Nov 1. Check your local HEAT office for current-year dates. Phone: 866-205-4357, 801-526-9240. Apply: https://jobs.utah.gov/community/scso/seal/heat.html; https://mydoorway.utah.gov/utility-assistance/. Cost: Free.

**Weatherization Assistance Program — Utah** — Free energy-efficient home improvements for low-income Utahns — tuning or repairing heating and cooling systems, adding insulation, and other upgrades. The program reports average savings of about 12% (roughly $283/year). Priority goes to elderly, disabled, and families with young children. Apply through the local agency serving your county. Eligibility: Utah resident; Low-income household; priority for elderly, disabled, and families with young children. Rolling — Apply any time through your county's local weatherization agency. Apply: https://mydoorway.utah.gov/utility-assistance/. Cost: Free — improvements are installed at no cost.

**Unemployment Insurance — Utah** — Temporary wage replacement for qualified Utah workers who are unemployed through no fault of their own and are able, available, and seeking full-time work. Benefits are based on previous earnings, not economic need. File online at jobs.utah.gov or call the Claims Assistance and Re-Employment Team. Eligibility: Utah worker with wages in covered employment; Unemployed through no fault of your own; Able, available, and seeking full-time work. Rolling — File immediately after becoming unemployed or when hours drop below full time — no benefits are paid for weeks before the week you file. Phone: 801-526-4400, 801-612-0877, 801-375-4067, 888-848-0688. Apply: https://mydoorway.utah.gov/unemployment-insurance/. Cost: Free.

**Family Employment Program (FEP) — TANF Cash Assistance** — The Family Employment Program is Utah's TANF-funded cash assistance program for families. It offers temporary cash support while connecting parents to employment, training, child support, and other services aimed at financial independence. Cash payment amounts vary by household size. Eligibility: Utah resident; Family with a dependent child under 18 (or 18 and a full-time student expected to finish before 19), or a pregnant woman in her third trimester; Countable assets of $2,000 or less (motorized vehicles exempt); U.S. citizen or qualifying non-citizen status; Income limits published by DWS (vary by household size). Rolling — Apply any time. Utah has a 36-month state lifetime limit on TANF cash assistance (established Jan 1, 1997). Phone: 1-866-435-7414. Apply: https://jobs.utah.gov/Infosource/EOMOA/61APP_09.12.pdf. Cost: Free.

**Child Care Assistance — Utah** — Helps eligible Utah families pay part of the cost of child care while parents work or attend approved school or training. Income limits are higher than most other assistance programs and are updated annually. Monthly payments go directly to the provider. Eligibility: Utah resident; Parent, foster parent, approved relative, or legal guardian of the child; Household income within published limits (updated annually) — limits are higher than most other assistance programs; Work at least 15 hours/week or need care for approved school/training activities; Use a DWS-approved provider (licensed or approved family, friend, or neighbor). Rolling — Apply any time. Income eligibility limits are updated annually — apply even if you were denied in the past. Phone: 1-866-435-7414. Apply: https://mydoorway.utah.gov/child-care-assistance/. Cost: Free to apply; families pay any cost above the subsidy amount directly to the provider.

**File Utah State Taxes — Taxpayer Access Point (TAP)** — The Utah State Tax Commission's official way to file individual income tax returns and business taxes online. Individuals can file the current year's Individual Income Tax return through the Taxpayer Access Point (TAP) with or without a login profile. Businesses create a TAP profile to file, pay, and amend tax returns. Eligibility: Anyone with a Utah individual income tax obligation or an active Utah business tax account. Rolling / seasonal — Individual income tax returns can be filed in TAP from the date the IRS begins accepting e-filed returns (typically late January) through November 30 of the same year for non-logged-in filers; logged-in filers can file the current year's return up to two years from the original due date. Phone: 801-297-3996. Apply: https://tax.utah.gov/business/file-and-pay/. Cost: Free to file via TAP (Taxpayer Access Point).

Rolling — Offices generally open Monday–Friday, 8:00 am–5:00 pm (some extended hours); appointments are scheduled through the DLD homepage by service type. Phone: 801.965.4437, 888.353.4224. Apply: https://dld.utah.gov/office-locations/. Cost: Varies by service — see DLD fee schedule on the DLD site.

Eligibility: Anyone forming or registering a business entity to operate in Utah. Rolling — File formation documents before starting operations. Renewal filing timing — see program page. **Services for People with Disabilities (DSPD)** — The Division of Services for People with Disabilities promotes self-determined lives by overseeing home and community-based services (Medicaid waivers) for Utahns with disabilities. Eligibility: Utah residents with qualifying disabilities — DSPD determines eligibility through intake; Service type depends on which Medicaid waiver the person qualifies for. **Utah Legal Services — Free Civil Legal Aid** — Utah Legal Services is a nonprofit law office providing free legal help in non-criminal (civil) cases to low-income Utahns, in areas including family law, protective orders, landlord/tenant, public benefits, consumer cases, and criminal record expungement. Offices in Salt Lake City, Ogden, Provo, and St. George serve the whole state. Eligibility: Low-income Utah resident — in most cases household income at or below 200% of the federal poverty level; Citizen or legal permanent resident (exceptions for domestic violence cases, e.g., protective orders); Case must be a priority case type; ULS screens every applicant. Rolling — Apply online or call any time; call as soon as you recognize you have a legal problem. Phone: 801-328-8891. Apply: https://www.utahlegalservices.org/page/16/who-qualifies-get-help-utah-legal-services. Cost: Free.
