# Indiana — GovServ Guide (verified 2026-09-19)

## Metro

### Indianapolis / Marion County

**IndyGo Reduced Fares and Veterans Pass.** Half fare ($1.35 two-hour fare, $3 daily cap, $12.15 weekly cap) for 65+ (Medicare card or Half Fare ID), youth 18 and under (K-12 student ID or Half Fare ID), and people with disabilities (Half Fare ID; disability form needs professional verification). Veterans ride fixed-route/rapid transit free with a $2 veterans pass ($5 replacement) — bring photo ID plus DD214, VA ID, or veteran-designated state license/ID. (317) 635-3344. [Fares and passes](https://www.indygo.net/fares-and-passes/?intent=info&agency_id=0)

**Indianapolis Public Library Card.** Free cards for Marion County residents (excluding Speedway), county property owners, qualifying teachers/students, and business owners. Online: 18+, county resident, email required. In person: photo ID with current address (or mail postmarked within 30 days, current lease, or checkbook). [Get a library card](http://INDYPL.ORG/get-a-library-card)

**Marion County Public Health Immunization and Nursing Clinics.** Appointment-only infant, child, and adult immunizations plus TB testing, blood-pressure screening, counseling, and referrals. Bring immunization record, Medicaid/insurance card, and ID (parent/guardian required for under 18). Phones: Northeast (317) 221-7300; South (317) 221-5700; Northwest (317) 221-7500; Eagledale (317) 221-3441. [Clinic schedule (PDF)](https://marionhealth.org/media/3swfskek/nursing-service-imm-clinic-schedule-eng-and-span-2-effective-12-6-24.pdf)

**Marion County Veteran Service Office.** Help identifying and applying for military-service-related benefits. Mon-Fri 12-8 PM; call for appointment, email preferred: veteran.services@indy.gov. (317) 327-8387; 200 E. Washington St., Suite 2141, Indianapolis. [Find your CVSO](https://www.in.gov/dva/locate-your-cvso/clinton-county/)

### Fort Wayne / Allen County

**Citilink Reduced Fares.** $0.75 cash / $0.70 mobile single ride, $1.85 all-day, $27 31-day for youth 5-18, seniors 60+, people with disabilities, and Medicare card holders with valid ID. Mon-Sat service; no Sundays. Free travel training: (260) 265-1753. (260) 432-4546. [Fares and map (PDF)](https://fwcitilink.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Citilink-Map_18x24_June-2026.pdf)

**Allen County Department of Health.** Confidential, low-cost HIV/STD testing, treatment, and counseling by appointment; vaccinations; certified birth/death certificates. Medical Annex (HIV/STD, TB, immunizations): 4813 New Haven Ave., Fort Wayne. HIV/STD/TB (260) 449-7504; immunizations (260) 449-7514. [allencountyhealth.com](http://www.allencountyhealth.com/)

**Allen County Veteran Service Office.** Mon-Fri 8 AM-12 PM, 1-4:30 PM. (260) 449-7861; 1 E. Main St., Room 106, Fort Wayne. [Find your CVSO](https://www.in.gov/dva/locate-your-cvso/clinton-county/)

### Evansville / Vanderburgh County

**METS Reduced Fares.** $0.35 reduced fare (base $0.75) for seniors, disabled riders, and Medicare cardholders; children 5 and under ride free with a paying adult (max 3). Proof: state photo ID showing 65+, or paper Medicare card plus state photo ID, or recent SSA disability letter plus state photo ID. Monthly passes: regular $60, student $45, elderly/handicapped $30. METS office (812) 435-6166; Mobility paratransit (approval required) (812) 435-6188; SWIRCA T.R.I.P. for 60+ (812) 464-7800. [METS fares and passes](https://www.evansvillegov.org/city/department/division.php?structureid=240)

**Vanderburgh County Health Department — Communicable Disease Services.** Diagnostic, treatment, immunization, foreign-travel/flu, and TB services. 420 Mulberry St., Evansville; 8 AM-4:30 PM. (812) 435-2400. [Services](https://vanderburghhealth.org/communicable-disease/)

**Vanderburgh County Veteran Service Office.** Mon-Fri 8 AM-4:30 PM Central. (812) 435-5239; 201 NW 4th St., Suite 303, Evansville. [Find your CVSO](https://www.in.gov/dva/locate-your-cvso/clinton-county/)

### South Bend / St. Joseph County

**Transpo Reduced Fare.** $0.50 discounted fare (regular $1) for disabled riders, Medicare cardholders, and 65+ with Medicare card, age-bearing photo ID, or Transpo Reduced Fare Card. Children 4 and under ride free (max 2 per fare-paying adult). No Sunday service. (574) 233-2131. [Fares and schedule (PDF)](https://www.sbtranspo.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Transpo_Route16_2025.pdf)

**St. Joseph County Health Department Immunizations.** Infant-through-adult immunizations; serves private insurance, Medicaid, Medicare, and uninsured patients. Availability is EXTREMELY LIMITED — appointments strongly recommended. COVID-19 vaccine eligibility is currently restricted to age 6 months+ meeting listed age, disability, or health-condition criteria. County-City Building, 9th floor, 227 W. Jefferson Blvd., South Bend; Mon-Fri 8 AM-noon, 1-4:30 PM. (574) 235-9750 (option 2 to schedule). [Immunizations](https://www.in.gov/localhealth/stjosephcounty/immunizations/)

**St. Joseph County Veteran Service Office.** Mon/Tue/Thu 8 AM-4 PM; Wednesdays the CVSO is at the VA clinic; Fridays by appointment only. (574) 235-9978; 227 W. Jefferson Blvd., Room 309, South Bend. [Find your CVSO](https://www.in.gov/dva/locate-your-cvso/clinton-county/)

### Carmel / Hamilton County

**Carmel Clay Public Library Card.** Free resident cards for Carmel/Clay Township residents; pre-register online or apply in person. Grades 5 and below need a parent/guardian present; grades 6+ can use a student ID or driver's license alone. School-year cards available for Carmel K-12 students and school employees. Online cards must be picked up within one month. Forms in English, Spanish, Arabic, Telugu, Simplified Chinese. (317) 814-3936. [Get a library card](https://carmelclaylibrary.org/get-a-library-card)

**Hamilton County Veteran Service Office.** Mon-Fri 9 AM-4 PM. (317) 776-9671 / (317) 776-9610; 1 Hamilton County Square, Suite 179, Noblesville. [Find your CVSO](https://www.in.gov/dva/locate-your-cvso/clinton-county/)

## State

**SNAP — Food Assistance (FSSA/DFR).** Monthly food benefits for eligible low-income households. Apply via the FSSA Benefits Portal, by phone, or at a DFR office: (800) 403-0864. 2025 federal changes tightened work requirements (ABAWD) and noncitizen eligibility; lost/stolen benefits need a replacement affidavit within 10 days; sugary drinks and candy are not SNAP-eligible in Indiana as of January 1, 2026. Free language assistance on request. [Apply online](https://fssabenefits.in.gov/bp/)

**Healthy Indiana Plan (HIP).** Health coverage for adults 19-64 who do not qualify for Medicare or Medicaid; POWER contributions $1-$20 by tier. Some members 19-64 must meet work/volunteer/training/education requirements starting January 1, 2027. [Apply for health coverage](https://fssabenefits.in.gov/bp/)

**Hoosier Healthwise.** Health coverage for children 0-19 and pregnant people. (800) 403-0864 (press 1). [Apply for health coverage](https://fssabenefits.in.gov/bp/)

**WIC (Indiana Department of Health).** Nutrition program for pregnant/postpartum/breastfeeding women, infants, and children under 5: healthy foods, nutrition education, breastfeeding support, health screening, referrals. Indiana residents meeting income guidelines and nutritional-risk criteria; contact a local WIC clinic for a free certification visit. [Eligibility information](https://faqs.in.gov/hc/en-us/articles/15925602730388-How-do-I-know-if-I-qualify-for-WIC)

**Energy Assistance Program / LIHEAP (IHCDA).** Heating and cooling bill help via local providers. The 2026 program year closed April 20, 2026 at 5:00 PM Eastern; reopening expected fall 2026 (no exact date). Appeals within 30 days to your local provider. (317) 232-7777; eap@ihcda.in.gov. [Program information](https://www.in.gov/ihcda/homeowners-and-renters/low-income-home-energy-assistance-program-liheap/)

**Unemployment Insurance (DWD).** Temporary benefits for workers unemployed through no fault of their own; weekly vouchers required, first week unpaid. Max weekly benefit $390; typical max 26 weeks. File through Uplink Claimant Self Service; help line (800) 891-6499. [File a claim](https://uplink.in.gov/CSS/CSSLogon.htm) | [Program information](https://www.IN.gov/dwd/indiana-unemployment/individuals)

**IMPACT — Employment and Training (FSSA).** No-cost job and training services for current SNAP or TANF recipients. (800) 403-0864, option 3. [FAQs](https://www.in.gov/fssa/dfr/impact-job-training/frequently-asked-questions/)

**Indiana BMV Identification Card.** State photo ID: $9 standard fee; free for qualifying voting-eligible U.S. citizens 18+. Bring proof of identity, lawful status, SSN, and two Indiana residency documents. [BMV ID cards](https://www.in.gov/bmv/licenses-permits-ids/learners-permits-and-drivers-licenses-overview/identification-cards)

**INBiz — Business Registration and Filings (Secretary of State).** Register a business and manage state filings online. Business Services (317) 234-9768, Mon-Fri 8 AM-4:30 PM. [About INBiz](https://inbiz.in.gov/about)

**Indiana 211 (FSSA).** Free, confidential referral to food, housing, utility, health, and employment resources statewide — no eligibility requirements. Dial 2-1-1 or (866) 211-9966 (Mon-Fri 8 AM-6 PM), text your ZIP code to 898-211, or search online. [Contact options](https://www.in.gov/fssa/indiana-211/indiana-211-contacts)

**Indiana Legal Services — Free Civil Legal Help.** Free noncriminal legal help for eligible low-income Hoosiers; project eligibility varies — apply if unsure. Online intake 24/7; phone (844) 243-8570, Mon-Fri 10 AM-2 PM Eastern. [Apply online](https://www.indianalegalservices.org/applyonline-ar-sa/)

**Indiana Vocational Rehabilitation (DDRS).** Employment services for people with disabilities; not based on income or resources. (800) 545-7763. [Program information](https://www.in.gov/fssa/ddars/brs/vocational-rehabilitation-employment/)

**Indiana Disability Rights.** Rights information, application/appeal guidance, referrals, self-advocacy support, possible advocate/attorney help. (317) 722-5555; (800) 622-4845. [Request help](https://www.in.gov/idr/for-help-fill-this-out)

**WorkOne Career Centers (DWD).** Free resume help, career planning, training/scholarship assistance, job search support, interview coaching, computer access. [WorkOne](https://www.in.gov/dwd/workone/)

**INfreefile (Department of Revenue).** File federal and state returns free through participating vendors; each vendor sets its own eligibility rules. [Free filing options](https://www.in.gov/dor/i-am-a/individual/infreefile/)

**Area Agencies on Aging / ADRCs (DDRS).** Help navigating aging and disability services. AAA/ADRC (800) 713-9023; Better Aging (800) 673-0002; Long-Term Care Ombudsman (800) 622-4484. [DDRS contacts](https://www.in.gov/fssa/ddars/contact-ddars/)

## Federal

**SSI and Social Security Disability (SSA).** Monthly SSI payments for adults and children with disabilities and people 65+ with little or no income and resources. Online SSI filing is limited (ages 18 to 64 and 10 months, never married, alleging a disability, no prior SSI application); others apply by phone. (800) 772-1213, Mon-Fri 8 AM-7 PM; interpreter services in 120+ languages; TTY (800) 325-0778. [ssa.gov](https://www.ssa.gov/)

**Medicare.** Federal health coverage for 65+, certain younger people with disabilities, and people with End-Stage Renal Disease. Compare plans and enroll at the official site. [medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov/)

**VA Indiana Health Care — Enrollment.** Enroll before scheduling VA appointments: VA Form 10-10EZ plus discharge records, relevant VA award letters, income information when requested, and insurance cards. Indiana Health Benefits Unit (317) 988-4310. [Register for VA care](https://www.va.gov/indiana-health-care/register-for-care-replaced/)

**U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA).** Free counseling and mentoring, SBA-backed loans, federal contracting help, and disaster assistance for small businesses. [sba.gov](https://www.sba.gov/)

**FCC Lifeline — Phone and Internet Discount.** Up to $9.25/month off phone or broadband (up to $34.25 on Tribal lands); one per household; income at/below 135% FPG or qualifying-program participation; annual recertification required. (800) 234-9473. [FCC Lifeline](https://www.fcc.gov/lifeline-consumers)

**HUD-Approved Housing Counseling (Indiana).** Free homebuying, foreclosure-avoidance, rental, and reverse-mortgage counseling. Counselor line (800) 569-4287; HUD Indianapolis office (317) 226-6303. HUD does not take local PHA applications or track vacancies/waitlists. [HUD Indiana](http://www.hud.gov/states/indiana)

**USCIS — Immigration and Citizenship Services.** File forms online, manage your case, and track status with a USCIS online account. [uscis.gov](https://www.uscis.gov/)
