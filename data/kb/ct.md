# Connecticut Government Services — GovServ Knowledge Base

40 verified services. Verified 2026-09-19.

## Metro

### Bridgeport

**Bridgeport Renters' Rebate** — Annual one-time rebate for elderly or totally disabled Bridgeport renters; one-year Connecticut residency required. 2026 income limits: $46,300 single / $56,500 married. Applications April 1–September 30 yearly, walk-in only, Mon–Thu 10:00 a.m.–3:30 p.m., 999 Broad Street. Phone: 203-576-7147. Apply: https://www.bridgeportct.gov/rentersrebate

**Bridgeport Relocation & Housing Hardship Program** — City help for residents with an eviction notice or a hardship likely to lead to eviction. Eligibility: Bridgeport resident facing eviction or housing hardship. Rolling. Phone: 203-576-7680. Apply: https://bridgeportct.gov/government/departments/housing-code/housing-resources

### New Haven

**New Haven Fair Rent Commission** — Investigates tenant complaints; can stop or delay unconscionable rent increases and limit rent where health/safety violations exist (per the City's 2024 resource guide). Eligibility: New Haven renter with a rent dispute. Rolling. Phone: 203-946-4930.

**New Haven Livable City Initiative (LCI)** — Housing-code complaints and inspections for New Haven rental housing (per the City's 2024 resource guide). Eligibility: New Haven resident with a housing-code complaint. Rolling. Phone: 203-946-7090.

**New Haven Health Department Health & Wellness Center** — Affordable vaccinations, testing, school physicals, STI testing and treatment, 424 Chapel Street (per the City's 2024 resource guide). Eligibility: open to public; call for services and hours. Rolling. Phone: 203-946-8181.

### Hartford

**HouseHartford Homebuyer Assistance** — Down-payment help for low/moderate-income buyers of 1–4 family homes or condos in Hartford: up to 20% of price, max $40,000, subject to restrictions. Household income within HUD 80% area-median limits; apply through a participating lender. Rolling. Apply: https://www.hartfordct.gov/Government/Departments/DDS/DDS-Services/Homebuyer-Assistance

**Hartford 311** — Toll-free non-emergency line to city services and information, Mon–Fri 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m. Report apartment issues (pests, mold, no heat/hot water/gas/electricity), trash, potholes, and other requests. Eligibility: anyone needing City of Hartford services. Rolling. Phone: 860-757-9311. Apply: https://www.hartfordct.gov/Government/Departments/Public-Works/Hartford-311

### Stamford

**Stamford Rent Rebate Program (2026)** — One-time annual payment to eligible elderly/disabled renters based on prior-year income, rent, and utilities. 2026: age 65+ as of 12/31/2025 or 18+ on Social Security Disability; one-year CT residency; $46,300 single / $56,500 married. 2026 window ran April 1–September 18, 2026 (recurs annually). Phone: 203-977-4747 / 203-977-4872. Apply: https://www.stamfordct.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/50654/639094415109070000

**Stamford Social Services Benefits Counseling** — Help applying for Medicare, Husky C, and the Medicare Savings Program, plus MedAssist CHOICES-trained counseling; 888 Washington Blvd., 6th Floor. Eligibility: Stamford residents; Medicare counseling for eligible seniors/people with disabilities. Rolling — call for appointment. Languages: English, Spanish, Haitian Creole. Phone: 203-977-4112; Medicare/Husky help: 203-977-4016 / 203-977-4874. Apply: https://www.stamfordct.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/31895/638373902596570000

### Waterbury

**Waterbury Renters Rebate** — Annual rebate for elderly or disabled Waterbury renters with one-year CT residency; proof of prior-year rent, utilities, and income required. 2026 limits: $46,300 single / $56,500 married. Applications April 1–September 30 yearly, by appointment. Phone: 203-346-3810. Apply: https://waterburyct.org/content/103357/104221/153626/170707/default.aspx

**Waterbury Senior Shuttle** — Free transportation for Waterbury residents 60+; Senior Shuttle ID card required. Nonmedical rides up to 3 days ahead, medical rides up to 1 month ahead; schedule 9:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m. Rolling. Phone: 1-800-757-7782 (rides); 203-346-3810 ext. 7274 (ID cards). Apply: https://www.WaterburyCT.org/filestorage/103431/107671/107728/May_2025_Newsletter-ENG.pdf

## County

### Fairfield County

**GBT Access ADA Paratransit** — Paratransit for people whose physical or cognitive impairment prevents regular GBT bus use; service generally within ¾ mile of GBT routes, including Bridgeport and nearby municipalities. Complete the application, identify a health professional, and attend an in-person assessment; determination generally within 21 days. Multilingual assistance available. Rolling. Phone: 203-366-7070 ext. 131 (customer service); 203-579-7777 (reservations); 203-333-3031 (languages). Apply: https://gogbt.com/wp-content/uploads/GBT-Access-Riders-Guide-FEB-2026-v2.pdf

## State

**Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP)** — Monthly food benefits for eligible low-income CT residents; apply online, by mail, or at a DSS Resource Center. Paper applications in English and Spanish. Rolling. Phone: 1-855-626-6632. Apply: https://portal.ct.gov/DSS/SNAP/Supplemental-Nutrition-Assistance-Program---SNAP/Apply

**HUSKY Health (Medicaid/CHIP)** — Connecticut's Medicaid and Children's Health Insurance Program via DSS. Eligibility: CT resident; income-based. Rolling. Phone: 1-855-805-4325 (enrollment/renewal); 1-855-626-6632 (DSS VoiceCT); 1-888-786-2345 (counselors). Apply: https://www.huskyhealthct.org/

**Connecticut WIC Program** — Nutrition support for pregnant/postpartum people, infants, and children under 5; nutrition assessment required; CT residency required, no citizenship proof needed; SNAP/TFA/HUSKY recipients automatically income-eligible. English and Spanish. Rolling — online interest form. Phone: 1-800-741-2142. Apply: https://Portal.ct.gov/dph/wic/how-to-apply

**Connecticut Energy Assistance Program (CEAP)** — Help paying winter heating costs for eligible CT households. 2026–2027 season applications were open as of September 2026. Seasonal — check the portal. Apply: https://portal.ct.gov/heatinghelp

**Connecticut Unemployment Insurance** — Temporary wage replacement for workers who lost a job through no fault of their own; file and manage claims via ReEmployCT. Note: the program page warns federal cuts are affecting CTDOL operations and wait times. Rolling. Apply: https://portal.ct.gov/DOLUI

**Temporary Family Assistance (TFA)** — Time-limited monthly cash assistance for low-income CT families with children. Rolling. Apply: https://portal.ct.gov/dss/find-benefits-and-support/cash-assistance

**State-Administered General Assistance (SAGA)** — Cash assistance for single adults who don't qualify for other cash programs. Rolling. Apply: https://portal.ct.gov/dss/find-benefits-and-support/cash-assistance

**State Supplement Program** — State cash assistance for aged, blind, or disabled CT residents meeting income/asset rules. Rolling. Apply: https://portal.ct.gov/dss/find-benefits-and-support/cash-assistance

**Refugee Cash Assistance** — Short-term cash assistance for refugees and other eligible humanitarian arrivals during their initial months in the U.S. Time-limited. Apply: https://portal.ct.gov/dss/find-benefits-and-support/cash-assistance

**Connecticut Department of Veterans Affairs — Programs and Services** — Advocacy and benefits help, residential programs, skilled nursing care, cemetery services, and eligible-family-member services. Eligibility: CT veterans; family members for some services. Rolling. Apply: https://portal.ct.gov/dva

**DVA Advocacy and Assistance** — Claims and benefits help for CT veterans and families; 287 West Street, Rocky Hill. Rolling. Phone: 860-616-3685; email oaa.dva@ct.gov. Apply: https://portal.ct.gov/dva/advocacy-and-assistance

**CHFA Government-Insured Mortgage Program** — State homebuyer mortgages for first-time buyers (or no home ownership in prior 3 years, targeted-area exceptions); primary residence, income and sales-price limits, homebuyer education, participating lender required. Rolling. Apply: https://www.chfa.org/homebuyers-homeowners/homebuyers/homebuyer-mortgage-program/

**Statewide Legal Services of Connecticut** — Free civil legal advice for low-income CT residents; most services limited to households at or below 125% FPL; no criminal, plaintiff-side, or immigration cases. Rolling. Phone: 1-800-453-3320. Apply: https://www.slsct.org/get-help

**Bureau of Rehabilitation Services (BRS)** — Employment preparation, placement, and retention for CT residents with disabilities (legally blind applicants go to BESB). Provide disability, education, and work/volunteer history; parent/guardian involvement under 18. Rolling. Apply: https://portal.ct.gov/ads/knowledge-base/articles/employment-services/for-job-seekers/how-to-start-your-journey-with-the-bureau-of-rehabilitation-services

**CT Paid Leave** — Paid family and medical leave: up to 12 weeks of wage replacement for most qualifying reasons. Apply 30 days before foreseeable leave or as soon as practicable otherwise; identity, employer verification, and leave-reason documents required. Rolling. Phone: 877-499-8606. Apply: https://www.ctpaidleave.org/claims?language=en_US

**CHOICES — Connecticut's Health Insurance Counseling Program** — Free, unbiased Medicare/Medicaid and long-term-care counseling for Medicare-eligible CT residents and families. Rolling. Phone: 1-800-994-9422. Apply: https://preview.ct.egov.com/ads/-/media/ads-beta/choices/ads_ship_choices_brochure.pdf

**Roberta B. Willis Need-Merit Scholarship** — Need- and merit-based scholarship for CT students at eligible in-state colleges; the high school counselor applies — students cannot apply directly. Annual — confirm the current February date with OHE (AY 2025–2026 deadline was Feb 15). Email: ohe.sfa@ct.gov. Apply: https://preview.ct.egov.com/-/media/sde/performance/fafsa/frequently-asked-questions--roberta-b-willis-need-merit-scholarship.pdf

**Consumer Law Project for Elders** — Free legal help for CT residents 60+ with consumer problems such as unfair loan terms; Spanish-speaking staff. Rolling. Phone: 1-800-296-1467. Apply: https://housedems.ct.gov/Issues/seniors

**Connecticut Home Care Program for Elders** — Home-care services for low/moderate-income families caring at home for someone 65+ at risk of nursing-home placement; eligibility levels vary by income/assets. Rolling. Phone: 1-800-445-5394 (DSS Alternate Care Unit). Apply: https://housedems.ct.gov/Issues/seniors

**Connecticut Business Services (Concord)** — Official business portal: entity/name filings, licensing guidance, UCC, tax links, contracting opportunities, hiring/training resources, closure/dissolution. Rolling. Apply: https://www.concord-sots.ct.gov/

**Way to Go CT Mobility Management Program** — Helps seniors, veterans, and people with disabilities understand transportation options. Rolling. Phone: 860-667-6207 ext. 17. Apply: https://housedems.ct.gov/Issues/seniors

**2-1-1 Coordinated Access Networks** — 2-1-1 is CT's front door to the Coordinated Access Networks for housing and homelessness services statewide; dial 211, Option 3. Rolling. Phone: 211. Apply: https://bridgeportct.gov/government/departments/housing-code/housing-resources

## Federal

**Social Security Benefits** — Retirement (62+ with 10 years of covered work), disability, survivor/family benefits, and SSI for low-income disabled, blind, or 65+. Rolling. Apply: https://www.ssa.gov/benefits

**Medicare** — Federal health insurance for 65+, end-stage renal disease, and disability beneficiaries; enroll and compare plans at medicare.gov. Enrollment windows apply. Apply: https://www.medicare.gov/

**VA Disability Compensation** — Monthly tax-free payments for service-connected physical and mental-health conditions (including PTSD); file online, by phone, mail, or fax; accredited representatives can help. Rolling. Apply: https://www.va.gov/disability/

**U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA) Resources** — Loans and funding, low-interest disaster loans for homeowners/renters/businesses, federal-contracting certifications, counseling and training. Rolling. Apply: https://www.sba.gov/

**Lifeline Phone and Internet Discount** — Up to $9.25/month off phone, internet, or bundled service (up to $34.25 on Tribal lands); qualify by income or programs like SNAP/Medicaid; recertify yearly. Rolling. Phone: 800-234-9473 (7 days, 9 a.m.–9 p.m. ET). Apply: https://www.lifelinesupport.org/

**USCIS Online Immigration Services** — File immigration forms online and track your case with a USCIS account. Form-specific deadlines and fees. Apply: https://www.uscis.gov/
