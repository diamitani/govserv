# Ohio — GovServ Guide (verified 2026-09-19)

## Metro

### Columbus / Franklin County

**Columbus 311 — City Service Requests.** One-stop non-emergency city services: potholes, missed trash, streetlights, code issues. Online/app 24/7; phone Mon-Fri 7 AM-7 PM at (614) 645-3111; 240+ languages. [Submit a request](https://www.columbus.gov/311-Customer-Service-Center?lang_update=638591699996606225)

**COTA Income Assistance / Reduced Fare.** 50% transit discount for SNAP, childcare assistance, Ohio Works First, PRC, Medicaid, TANF, or WIC recipients; seniors, children, veterans, riders with disabilities also half fare. Capped $2.25/day, $31/month via Transit app or smartcard. Apply in person at 33 N. High St., Columbus, with benefit verification + photo ID. (614) 228-1776. [Program details](https://cota.com/blog/cota-to-launch-income-assistance-pilot-program-on-march-1-to-improve-transit-fare-equity/)

**OhioMeansJobs Columbus-Franklin County.** Free job search help, resume/interview coaching, training funds. 1111 E. Broad St., Columbus; Mon-Fri 8 AM-5 PM; (614) 559-5052. [omjcfc.org](https://www.omjcfc.org/)

**Homeless Hotline / Coordinated Access.** Single front door to emergency shelter in Columbus and Franklin County. 24-hour hotline (614) 274-7000; families with children, pregnant women, and veterans prioritized. [Community Shelter Board](https://www.csb.org/)

### Cleveland / Cuyahoga County

**Cleveland 311 — City Service Requests.** Non-emergency city services 24/7: potholes, waste collection, vacant properties, parks. Call 311 or (216) 664-2000. [clevelandohio.gov/311](https://www.clevelandohio.gov/311)

**GCRTA Senior and Disability Discount Fare.** Discounted fixed-route fares for 65+, Medicare holders, people with disabilities (professional certification), and service-connected veterans. ID $3-5. Bring application + photo ID to 1240 W. 6th St. [Application (PDF)](https://riderta.com/sites/default/files/FixedRouteDisabilityFareApp.pdf)

**OhioMeansJobs Cleveland-Cuyahoga County.** Free employment services — resume help, job leads, training, youth programs. Walk-ins welcome, Mon-Fri 8 AM-4:30 PM. (216) 777-8200. [Find job support](https://greaterclevelandworks.org/find-a-job/)

**Legal Aid Society of Cleveland.** Free civil legal advice and representation for low-income residents of Ashtabula, Cuyahoga, Geauga, Lake, and Lorain counties. Intake: (888) 817-3777; online application in English/Spanish. [Apply](https://lasclev.org/apply-for-free-legal-aid/)

### Cincinnati / Hamilton County

**311Cincy — City Service Requests.** Non-emergency city services 24/7 online/app: potholes, trash, graffiti, abandoned vehicles. Call 311 or (513) 765-1212. [311Cincy](https://www.cincinnati-oh.gov/311/)

**Metro Fare Deal Program.** Half-price Metro fares for 65+, Medicare holders, riders with disabilities, and Access card holders; monthly sticker $44. Apply in person at the Fare Deal office, 7000 Montgomery Rd. (Mon/Wed/Fri), (513) 632-7540. [Program details](https://www.go-metro.com/fares-passes/fare-deal-program/)

**Legal Aid Society of Greater Cincinnati.** Free civil legal help for low-income residents. (513) 241-9400; toll-free (800) 582-2682. [lascinti.org](https://lascinti.org/)

### Toledo / Lucas County

**Engage Toledo — City Service Requests.** 24/7 city services: potholes, tall grass, water emergencies, parking. Phone (419) 936-2020; email engagetoledo@toledo.oh.gov; app and online portal. [Engage Toledo](https://toledo.oh.gov/engage-toledo)

**TARTA Reduced Fare Program.** $0.75 fixed-route fares for 65+, veterans/active military, SSI/SSDI recipients, and people with disabilities. Apply at the Transit Hub, 612 N. Huron St., Toledo. (419) 243-7433. [Application process](https://tarta.com/ride-tarta/how-to-ride/fares-passes/reduced-fare-application-process/)

### Akron / Summit County

**Akron 3-1-1 — City Service Requests.** One call to City Hall: tall grass, snow plowing, potholes, trash, streetlights, housing complaints. Mon-Fri 7 AM-6 PM at 3-1-1 or (330) 375-2311. [Akron 3-1-1](https://www.akronohio.gov/departments/neighborhood_assistance/311.php)

**METRO RTA Reduced Fare Program.** Discounted fixed-route fares for seniors and people with disabilities (medical professional must certify disability applications). (330) 376-5353. [Application (PDF)](https://www.yourmetrobus.org/Data/Sites/2/pdf/reduced-fare-app-final-11.10.16.pdf)

## County

Franklin, Cuyahoga, Hamilton, Lucas, and Summit County services are listed under their metro areas above. County-level workforce, transit, veterans, and legal-aid programs apply countywide.

## State

**SNAP — Food Assistance (ODJFS).** Monthly food benefits on an Ohio Direction Card. Apply via Ohio Benefits; helpline (844) 640-6446. [Program page](https://benefits.ohio.gov/home/resources/assistance-programs/01-snap)

**Ohio Medicaid.** Free/low-cost health coverage; most members join a managed care plan. Consumer hotline (800) 324-8680; interpreters available. [Program page](https://benefits.ohio.gov/home/resources/assistance-programs/03-medicaid) | [medicaid.ohio.gov](https://medicaid.ohio.gov)

**WIC (Ohio Department of Health).** Nutrition support for pregnant/postpartum women, infants, and children under 5; free language assistance. (614) 644-8006. [Program page](https://odh.ohio.gov/know-our-programs/women-infant-children-wic)

**HEAP — Home Energy Assistance.** Seasonal heating help via Community Action Agencies. 2026-27 program year: July 1, 2026-May 31, 2027. (800) 282-0880. [Find your provider](https://www.energyhelp.ohio.gov) | [OCC fact sheet](https://occ.ohio.gov/factsheet/home-energy-assistance-program-heap)

**Ohio Unemployment Insurance (ODJFS).** Temporary benefits for workers unemployed through no fault of their own. Claimant line (877) 644-6562; TTY (800) 750-0750. [File online](https://unemployment.ohio.gov)

**Ohio BMV Online Services.** Renew/replace licenses and IDs, renew registration, change address, schedule testing. Fees vary. [BMV Online](https://bmvonline.dps.ohio.gov/bmvonline/home)

**Register a Business (Secretary of State).** File LLCs/corporations and follow the starting-a-business roadmap. (877) SOS-FILE. [Roadmap](https://www.ohiosos.gov/business/ohio-business-roadmap/starting-a-business)

**Ohio College Opportunity Grant.** Need-based grants for Ohio residents at eligible schools; file the FAFSA by **Oct 1, 2026** for 2026-27. [OCOG](https://highered.ohio.gov/educators/financial-aid/sgs/ocog) | [FAFSA](https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa)

**OHFA Homebuyer Program.** 30-year fixed-rate options + down-payment help through approved lenders. (888) 362-6432. [My Ohio Home](https://myohiohome.org/homebuyerprogram.aspx)

**OOD Vocational Rehabilitation.** Employment services for Ohioans with disabilities: counseling, training, placement. [Start an application](https://ood.ohio.gov/information-for-individuals/oodworks/03-start-application/start-an-application)

**PASSPORT Waiver.** Home-based care for Medicaid-eligible Ohioans 60+ needing nursing-facility-level care. (844) 644-6582. [Program rule](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-administrative-code/rule-5160-31-03)

**Ohio Department of Veterans Services.** State veterans programs + County Veterans Service Offices in all 88 counties. (614) 644-0898. [dvs.ohio.gov](https://dvs.ohio.gov/home)

**Ohio Legal Help.** Free civil legal information, forms, and referrals statewide. [ohiolegalhelp.org](https://www.ohiolegalhelp.org)

**Ohio 211.** Free referrals to food, housing, utility, and crisis help statewide; dial 2-1-1 (unverified) or search online. [ohio211.org](https://ohio211.org)

**Ohio Crime Victim Compensation (Attorney General).** Help with medical/counseling bills, lost wages, funeral costs for violent-crime victims; file within 3 years. (800) 582-2877. [Apply](https://ohioattorneygeneral.gov/Individuals-and-Families/Victims/Apply-for-Victims-Compensation)

## Federal

**Medicare.** Health insurance for 65+ and qualifying disabilities. [medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov)

**FAFSA — Federal Student Aid.** Free application for federal grants, work-study, loans. Ohio priority: Oct 1, 2026. [File](https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa)

**Lifeline.** Discounted phone/broadband for low-income households. (800) 234-9473. [lifelinesupport.org](https://www.lifelinesupport.org)

**SBA Programs.** Small business loans, free counseling (SCORE/SBDCs), contracting help. [sba.gov](https://www.sba.gov)

**SSI.** Monthly payments for disabled/older adults with little income or resources. [ssa.gov/ssi](https://www.ssa.gov/ssi)

**SSDI.** Monthly payments for workers whose disability limits work; may include Medicare. [ssa.gov/disability](https://www.ssa.gov/disability)

**VA Health Care.** Comprehensive care for eligible veterans at Ohio VA facilities. (877) 327-0022. [va.gov/health-care](https://www.va.gov/health-care/)

**VA Disability Compensation.** Tax-free monthly payments for service-connected disabilities. (800) 827-1000. [va.gov/disability](https://www.va.gov/disability/)

**USCIS.** Green cards, citizenship, work authorization, case status. [uscis.gov](https://www.uscis.gov)

**HUD Housing Counseling.** Free/low-cost help from HUD-approved Ohio counselors: buying, foreclosure prevention, renting. [hud.gov/counseling](https://www.hud.gov/counseling)
