# Missouri Government Services

Verified September 19, 2026. Phones appear on official pages unless marked (unverified). Apply links are official program pages (HTTP 200 verified).

## Metro

### Kansas City metro

- **KCMO 311 — City Services** (Transportation) — Report/tracking for potholes, dumping, missed trash. 311 / 816-513-1313; interpretation available. [kcmo.gov](https://www.kcmo.gov/city-hall/311)
- **RideKC Reduced Fare** (Transportation) — Reduced fares for ages 6–18, 65+, Medicare holders, qualifying low-income-program recipients, people with disabilities. Full fares returned 2026-06-01. [ridekc.org](https://ridekc.org/fares)
- **Kansas City Public Library Card** (Education) — Free card; instant online signup; full access for metro KC residents (ID/address verification in person). 816.701.3400. [kclibrary.org](https://kclibrary.org/services/library-cards)
- **Legal Aid of Western Missouri** (Legal Aid) — Free civil legal help for low-income/elderly residents in 40 western MO counties. 816-474-6750. [lawmo.org](https://lawmo.org/get-legal-help/)
- **USCIS Kansas City Application Support Center** (Immigration, Federal) — Scheduled biometrics (fingerprints, photo, e-signature). Wait for appointment notice; bring notice + unexpired photo ID. Reschedule ≥12h ahead via USCIS account or 800-375-5283. [uscis.gov](https://www.uscis.gov/about-us/MO/Kansas-City)

### St. Louis metro

- **St. Louis 311 — Citizens' Service Bureau** (Transportation) — City-service issue reporting. 311 inside city / 314-622-4800 outside. [stlouis-mo.gov](https://www.stlouis-mo.gov/government/departments/public-safety/news/city-celebrates-311.cfm)
- **Metro Transit Reduced Fares (St. Louis)** (Transportation) — Reduced fares for 65+, people with disabilities, Medicare ID holders. 314.982.1510. STATUS: red ADA paratransit cards stop being accepted 2026-09-30. [metrostlouis.org](https://www.metrostlouis.org/reduced-fare-program/)
- **Legal Services of Eastern Missouri** (Legal Aid) — Free civil legal help for low-income residents. 800.444.0514 / 314.534.4200; phone intake Mon/Wed/Thu 9am–1pm. No PI/traffic/bankruptcy/criminal. [lsem.org](https://lsem.org/get-help-now/)

### Springfield metro

- **Legal Services of Southern Missouri** (Legal Aid) — Free civil representation for eligible low-income/elderly in 43 southern MO counties; clients may owe court costs. (417) 881-1397 / (800) 444-4863. [lsosm.org](https://www.lsosm.org/applicants/)
- **OACAC LIHEAP — Ozarks Energy Assistance** (Utilities) — Local LIHEAP for the Ozarks. Opens Oct 1 (60+/disability), Nov 1 (all others); ECIP needs a disconnect notice. (417) 864-3460 (unverified). [oac.ac](https://oac.ac/liheap-energy-programs/)

### Columbia metro

- **Columbia Contact Center** (Transportation) — General city services/info. 573.874.2489 (311 is non-emergency police, not city services). [como.gov](https://www.como.gov/departments/city-managers-office/)
- **Go COMO Transit (Fare-Free)** (Transportation) — Fare-free bus + ADA paratransit; free since March 2020, ordinance extends through 2026-09-30. Assistance in Spanish, French, Chinese, Korean, Japanese. [gocomotransit.com](https://www.gocomotransit.com/riderguide/)
- **Columbia Housing Authority** (Housing) — Affordable housing for low-income families/individuals in Columbia and Boone County. STATUS: Section 8 (HCV) waitlist closed since late 2024; no HCV applications during 2026; no plans to open until 2027. (573) 443-2556. [columbiaha.com](https://columbiaha.com/)

## County

### Boone County

- **Mid-Missouri Legal Services** (Legal Aid) — Free civil legal help for low-income mid-Missouri residents, including Columbia. Phone applications Mon–Fri 9am–2:30pm; interpreter services on request. (573) 442-0116 / (573) 634-4545 / 800-568-4931. [mmls.org](https://www.mmls.org/our-services/apply/)

## State

- **Missouri SNAP — Food Benefits** (Food) — Monthly food benefits for low-income households; online/paper/phone application + interview. 855-823-4908. [dss.mo.gov](https://dss.mo.gov/food-assistance/apply-for-snap)
- **MO HealthNet — Medicaid** (Healthcare) — Health coverage for eligible low-income children, parents, seniors, people with disabilities. STATUS (2026-07-01): specified acupuncture, chiropractic, and physical-therapy coverage stopped under the chronic-pain program; chiropractic statewide. 1-855-373-4636 (translation) / 800-392-2161 (constituent services). [dss.mo.gov](https://dss.mo.gov/healthcare/apply)
- **Missouri Unemployment Insurance** (Employment) — Weekly benefits for workers who lost jobs through no fault of their own; file ASAP after job loss. 573-751-9040 (Jefferson City), 816-889-3101 (KC), 314-340-4950 (St. Louis), 417-895-6851 (Springfield), 800-320-2519 (outside local areas). [labor.mo.gov](http://labor.mo.gov/unemployed-workers)
- **Missouri LIHEAP — Energy Assistance** (Utilities) — Heating/cooling cost help. Energy Assistance Oct–May; ECIP Nov–May (winter) / Jun–Sep (summer); funding-dependent. 855-373-4636. [mydss.mo.gov](https://mydss.mo.gov/utility-assistance/liheap)
- **Missouri WIC — Women, Infants, and Children** (Food) — Nutrition support + food benefits for pregnant/postpartum women, infants, children under 5; local certification appointment required. TEL-LINK 1-800-835-5465. [health.mo.gov](https://health.mo.gov/families/women-infants-children-wic)
- **Missouri Temporary Assistance (TANF)** (Family & Childcare) — Cash assistance for low-income families with children; recipients must join Missouri Work Assistance. [dss.mo.gov](https://dss.mo.gov/temporary-assistance/apply)
- **Missouri Driver Licensing** (Transportation) — Driver licenses, permits, non-driver IDs, renewals; document checklists by license type. Fees vary. [dor.mo.gov](https://dor.mo.gov/driver-license/)
- **Missouri Business Filings (Secretary of State)** (Small Business) — Register entities, file annual reports, search business records; portal account needs email confirmation. Fees vary. [bsd.sos.mo.gov](https://bsd.sos.mo.gov/)
- **Missouri Veterans Commission Benefits** (Veterans) — Veterans homes, cemeteries, service-officer VA-claim help, benefits portal. 573-751-3779 / movets@mvc.dps.mo.gov. [mvc.dps.mo.gov](https://mvc.dps.mo.gov/) · [veteranbenefits.mo.gov](https://veteranbenefits.mo.gov/)
- **Access Missouri Financial Assistance Program** (Education) — Need-based grants for undergrads at approved MO schools; FAFSA only, no separate application. STATUS: 2026–27 cycle closed (priority 2026-02-02, final 2026-04-01, correction 2026-07-31 all passed). (800) 473-6757 opt. 4. [dhewd.mo.gov](https://dhewd.mo.gov/ppc/grants-scholarships/access-mo)
- **Missouri Job Centers** (Employment) — Free job-seeker services: job search, résumé help, training. 1-888-728-5627. [jobs.mo.gov](https://jobs.mo.gov/)
- **Missouri Senior Resource Line** (Seniors) — Referrals to Area Agencies on Aging by ZIP. 1-800-235-5503 (unverified). [health.mo.gov](https://health.mo.gov/partners-providers/area-agencies-aging/missouri-senior-resource-line)
- **Missouri Child Care Subsidy** (Family & Childcare) — Child care cost help for eligible low-income families; report changes within 10 calendar days. 573-415-8605. [dese.mo.gov](https://dese.mo.gov/childhood/child-care-subsidy/families)
- **Missouri Vocational Rehabilitation** (Disability) — Employment services when an impairment is a work barrier; valid work permit required, citizenship not required. 1-800-392-8667 (CAP). [dese.mo.gov](https://dese.mo.gov/adult-learning-rehabilitation-services/vocational-rehabilitation/vr-frequently-asked-questions-faq)
- **Missouri Property Tax Credit (Circuit Breaker)** (Money & Taxes) — For certain seniors and 100%-disabled persons; max $750 renters / $1,100 owner-occupants; renters in non-property-tax facilities ineligible. [dor.mo.gov](https://dor.mo.gov/personal/ptc/)
- **Missouri SHIP — Medicare Counseling** (Seniors) — Free, unbiased Medicare counseling statewide (renamed from Missouri CLAIM in 2023); contact form answered in 2–5 business days. 1-800-390-3330. [missouriship.org](https://www.missouriship.org/contact-us/)

## Federal

- **Social Security Benefits (SSA)** (Seniors) — Retirement, disability, survivors, spouse, Medicare, limited SSI applications online; offices statewide. 1-800-772-1213 / TTY 1-800-325-0778. [ssa.gov](https://www.ssa.gov/apply)
- **VA Health Care & Benefits — Missouri** (Veterans) — VA medical centers in Kansas City (816-861-4700), St. Louis (314-652-4100), Columbia (573-814-6000), Poplar Bluff (573-686-4151), plus clinics/Vet Centers. [va.gov](https://www.va.gov/directory/guide/)
- **SBA Kansas City District Office** (Small Business) — 61 western MO counties + 28 eastern KS counties; KC and Springfield offices. Funding, counseling, contracting certifications, disaster recovery. [sba.gov](https://www.sba.gov/district/kansas-city)
- **SBA St. Louis District Office** (Small Business) — 53 eastern MO counties + St. Louis city; Mon–Fri 8am–4:30pm, 1222 Spruce St. Ste. 10.103. 314-539-6600. [sba.gov](https://www.sba.gov/district/st-louis)
- **HUD Housing Help — Missouri** (Housing) — HUD-approved housing counseling (800) 569-4287; public housing locator (800) 955-2232; fair housing complaints 1-800-669-9777; homelessness resources via 211. Resource locator does not show vacancies or maintain waitlists. [hud.gov](https://www.hud.gov/states/missouri/)
- **FCC Lifeline — Phone/Internet Discount** (Utilities) — Up to $9.25/month off phone or broadband ($34.25 on qualifying Tribal lands); one per household. Income ≤135% FPG or SNAP/Medicaid/SSI/federal housing/Veterans Pension participation. 1-800-234-9473. [fcc.gov](https://www.fcc.gov/lifeline-consumers)
