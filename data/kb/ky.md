# Kentucky (KY) — Verified Services (2026-09-19)

## Metro

### Louisville / Jefferson County
- **Metro311 — City Service Requests** — Report potholes, missed trash, other non-emergency issues. Online 24/7. Phone: 311 / 502-574-5000. Apply: https://louisvilleky.gov/tell311
- **TARC Public Transit** — Bus transit for Louisville/Jefferson County; new network began Aug 2, 2026; core routes generally every 15–30 min. 502-585-1234. https://www.ridetarc.org/newtarcnetwork/
- **KentuckianaWorks Job Seeker Services** — Free job search, training referrals, workshops, resume/interview help for Bullitt, Henry, Jefferson, Oldham, Shelby, Spencer, Trimble counties. 502-388-3010. https://www.kentuckianaworks.org/
- **Louisville Metro Public Health & Wellness** — Clinics, immunizations, testing, community health programs for Jefferson County. https://louisvilleky.gov/government/health-wellness/
- **Louisville Free Public Library Card** — Free card; borrowing + digital resources; apply online. 502-574-1611. https://www.lfpl.org/howdoi

### Lexington / Fayette County
- **LexCall 311 — City Service Requests** — Garbage, potholes, debris, signs; interpretation in 150+ languages/dialects. 311 / 859-425-2255. http://www.LexingtonKY.gov/living/lexcall-311
- **Lextran Public Transit** — Bus transit incl. seasonal LexRide downtown circulator (Apr 2–Oct 31, 2026 — currently running). 859-253-4636 / 859-255-7756. https://lextran.com/
- **Lexington Public Library Card** — Free; ebooks, audiobooks, online classes, databases; traditional or digital card. https://www.lexpublib.org/

### Bowling Green
- **GoBG Transit** — City bus transit. Fares: adult $2; ages 7–11 w/ adult $1; 6 and under free; seniors 60+ & people with disabilities $1. Day/multi-ride/monthly/student passes. https://www.bgky.org/transit

### Owensboro
- **Owensboro Transit System** — Fixed routes + paratransit for eligible riders unable to use fixed routes. 270-687-8570. https://transit.owensboro.org/

### Covington
- **TANK Plus Microtransit** — On-demand curb-to-curb, Mon–Fri 8am–6pm, Sat 9am–5pm, no Sundays. Fares: $5 within-zone; $2.50 to transfer point. 859-331-8265. https://www.tankbus.org/microtransit/

## County

### Warren County (Bowling Green)
- **Barren River District Health Dept — Harm Reduction** — Public health services; free Narcan Mon–Fri at 1109 State St, Bowling Green. 270-781-8039. https://www.barrenriverhealth.org/services/harm-reduction
- **Warren County Public Library Card** — Free for Kentucky residents; photo ID for in-person; online/digital cards. https://warrenpl.org/get-a-library-card/

### Daviess County (Owensboro)
- **Green River District Health Department** — Health services, prevention programs across 7 counties incl. Daviess. 270-686-7744. https://healthdepartment.org/
- **Daviess County Public Library Card** — Free for county residents, local employees/property owners/students, KY residents 50+; 18+ may apply online; photo ID required. 270-684-0211 ext. 224. https://www.dcplibrary.org/get-a-library-card/

### Kenton County (Covington)
- **Northern Kentucky Health Department** — Public health for Boone, Campbell, Grant, Kenton counties. Oral health may have a waiting list. 859-341-4264; oral health 859-363-2103; TTY 711. https://nkyhealth.org/aboutus/
- **Kenton County Public Library Card** — Free; online applications mailed to applicants. 859-962-4000. https://www.kentonlibrary.org/library-card-application/

## State

- **SNAP (incl. SAFE)** — Monthly food benefits via EBT; SAFE = simplified SNAP for SSI recipients 60+. Apply via kynect or 855-306-8959; interview required. https://www.chfs.ky.gov/agencies/dcbs/dfs/nab/Pages/default.aspx
- **Kentucky Medicaid** — Health coverage for low-income residents. Note: Anthem exited as a Medicaid MCO 1/1/2025; members reassigned to Humana/UnitedHealthcare; change plans via kynect 855-459-6328. https://www.chfs.ky.gov/agencies/dms/pages/default.aspx
- **KTAP — Transitional Assistance** — Monthly cash aid for low-income families w/ dependent children; 60-month lifetime limit. kynect or 855-306-8959. https://www.chfs.ky.gov/agencies/dcbs/dfs/fssb/Pages/ktap.aspx
- **WIC** — Nutrition, breastfeeding help, food benefits for pregnant/postpartum/breastfeeding women, infants, children through age 5. English/Spanish. 877-597-0367; TTY-to-voice 800-648-6056. https://www.chfs.ky.gov/agencies/dph/dmch/nsb/Pages/wic.aspx
- **CCAP — Child Care Assistance** — Child care help for qualifying low-income families; family shares/fees vary. kynect or 855-306-8959; interpreter 866-291-8931. https://www.chfs.ky.gov/agencies/dcbs/dcc/Pages/ccap.aspx
- **Unemployment Insurance** — Temp income for workers unemployed through no fault of their own. Requires ID.me; 18-month employer history; ~5 weekly work-search activities (3+ formal applications/interviews). 502-564-2900. https://kcc.ky.gov/career/individuals/Pages/File-A-Claim-or-Request-Benefits-Online.aspx
- **Rental Programs — Kentucky Housing Corporation** — Housing Choice Vouchers etc. Not immediate emergency aid; waits depend on HUD funding. https://www.kyhousing.org/page/rental
- **Weatherization Assistance Program** — Free home energy upgrades via local Community Action Agencies; renters need written landlord permission. https://www.kyhousing.org/programs/weatherization
- **Driver Licensing & State IDs** — Licenses, REAL ID, state IDs at KYTC Regional Offices. Fees vary by credential. https://drive.ky.gov/Pages/index.aspx
- **Business Registration — One Stop** — Start/register/manage a KY business via One Stop; SOS filings. Fees vary by filing. https://onestop.ky.gov/Pages/default.aspx
- **KHEAA Scholarships & Grants** — KEES, Work Ready Kentucky, need-based grants, Guard tuition awards, teacher scholarships. File FAFSA annually. 800-928-8926 ext. 67394; studentaid@kheaa.com. https://www.kheaa.com/web/scholarships-grants.faces
- **Vocational Rehabilitation (OVR)** — Employment services for Kentuckians w/ disabilities; eligibility appointment via local office. 800-372-7172 (Louisville 502-595-4173; Lexington 859-246-2537; Bowling Green 270-746-7489; Owensboro 270-687-7308; Covington 859-292-6513). https://kcc.ky.gov/Vocational-Rehabilitation/seeking-services/pages/how-to-apply-for-ovr-services.aspx
- **Veterans Claims Assistance — KDVA** — Free accredited help w/ federal VA compensation/pension claims and appeals. https://veterans.ky.gov/benefits/pages/default.aspx
- **Aging & Disability Resource Center (ADRC)** — DAIL connects older adults & people w/ disabilities to meals, benefits counseling, case management, caregiver support, transportation, legal aid, in-home help. https://chfs.ky.gov/agencies/dail/Pages/aaail.aspx

## Federal

- **Social Security Retirement** — Monthly benefits from age 62 for workers w/ 10+ years of taxed work. https://www.ssa.gov/retirement
- **SSDI** — Monthly disability payments based on work history; beneficiaries may also get Medicare. https://www.ssa.gov/disability
- **SSI** — Monthly payments for people w/ disabilities and older adults w/ little/no income or resources. https://www.ssa.gov/ssi
- **Medicare** — Federal health insurance for 65+, certain people w/ disabilities, ESRD patients. Enrollment periods apply. https://www.medicare.gov/
- **VA Health Care** — Primary, specialty, mental health care for eligible veterans. https://www.va.gov/health-care/
- **VA Disability Compensation** — Tax-free monthly payments for service-connected disabilities. https://www.va.gov/disability/
- **FAFSA** — Federal student-aid form; required for Kentucky state aid. File annually, early. https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa
- **HUD Housing Counseling** — Free/low-cost counseling on buying, renting, foreclosure, credit. https://www.hud.gov/counseling
- **SBA Loan Programs** — Federal loan guarantees via participating lenders; free SCORE/SBDC/WBC counseling. https://www.sba.gov/funding-programs/loans
- **USAJOBS** — Federal job postings incl. Kentucky-based openings. https://www.usajobs.gov/
