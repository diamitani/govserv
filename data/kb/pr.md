# Puerto Rico Services Guide

32 verified services covering San Juan, Bayamon, Carolina, Ponce, Caguas, statewide Puerto Rico, and federal programs. All entries verified 2026-09-19.

## Metro

### San Juan

**Servicios Legales de Puerto Rico — Metropolitan office (San Juan)** — Servicios Legales de Puerto Rico. Free civil legal services for qualifying Puerto Rico residents, through the Metropolitan (San Juan-area) intake office. Centralized intake operates statewide; call 1-800-981-5342 (TTY 711) or the metro line. 787-763-5103. Legal Aid. Free for eligible clients. https://servicioslegales.org/wp-content/documents/bifolder_brochure_SLPR_servicio.pdf

**ATI public transportation (Tren Urbano, buses, ferries)** — Autoridad de Transporte Integrado (ATI). Integrated public transportation for the San Juan metro area and beyond: Tren Urbano rail, 32 fixed bus routes, the Catano-Old San Juan ferry, Ceiba-Vieques/Culebra routes, and Llame y Viaje complementary transportation for people with disabilities. Transportation. Fares apply; check current fare information on the ATI site. http://www.ati.pr.gov/

**Derecho a Techo (homeless services Continuum of Care)** — Derecho a Techo / HUD Continuum of Care (via Sol Por Hoy). HUD-funded Continuum of Care covering the San Juan metro area including Bayamon and Carolina. Its Coordinated Assessment System evaluates people at risk of or experiencing homelessness and refers them to housing and services. HUD lists the toll-free line 1-844-648-3246 and office number (787) 626-1894. 1-844-648-3246. Housing. Free. https://www.hud.gov/states/puerto-rico

Centralized statewide intake is 1-800-981-5342 (TTY 711). 787-785-3858. Legal Aid. Free for eligible clients. https://servicioslegales.org/wp-content/documents/bifolder_brochure_SLPR_servicio.pdf

**Plan Vital (Medicaid) local office — Bayamon** — Departamento de Salud de Puerto Rico / ASES. Local in-person access point for Plan Vital (Puerto Rico Medicaid) in Bayamon. Medicaid's site confirms offices in Bayamon, Caguas, Metropolitana, and Ponce; applications can also be submitted online at medicaid.pr.gov. 787-641-4224. Healthcare. Free to apply; covered services per program rules. https://medicaid.pr.gov/

**SITRAC ADA Paratransit (Carolina)** — Municipio de Carolina — Sistema Intermodal de Transportacion (SITRAC). Shared-ride ADA paratransit for people unable to use fixed-route transit because of a disability or disabling health condition. The Municipality of Carolina provides the service on its rural routes; it is free of charge, operates Monday-Friday 6 a.m.-6 p.m., and requires an approved eligibility application submitted at the SITRAC office (General Services Department, 2nd floor, City Hall). Rides are reserved 1-14 days ahead by phone. 787-757-2626. Transportation. Free (no fares charged). https://www.municipiocarolina.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/ADA-Paratransit-Riders-Guide-Rev-July-2023-DSG-sin-logo.pdf

**Muevete Libre (Caguas municipal transit)** — Municipio Autonomo de Caguas — Departamento de Movilidad Ciudadana. Caguas' free municipal transit network: Trolleys, Transcriollo, and Paratransito, integrated from the Centro de Transportacion Francisco 'Pancho' Pereira. The system runs 4 active trolley routes and 7 Transcriollo routes and moved over 86,500 passengers in fiscal year 2024-2025. The free Muévete Libre app (via QR codes at 285 stops) shows real-time arrivals and route planning. Transportation. Free of cost. https://caguas.gov.pr/

## State

**PAN (Programa de Asistencia Nutricional)** — Administracion de Desarrollo Socioeconomico de la Familia (ADSEF). Puerto Rico's nutrition assistance program for low-income households. Applications are submitted through the ADSEF Digital portal; ADSEF publishes its document requirements guide and reports eligibility decisions within 30 calendar days of a completed application. 787-289-7632. Food. Free to apply; benefits are issued to eligible households. https://serviciosenlinea.adsef.pr.gov/

**TANF (Ayuda Temporal para Familias Necesitadas)** — Administracion de Desarrollo Socioeconomico de la Familia (ADSEF). Temporary cash assistance for families in need, administered by ADSEF. The program covers six assistance categories, each with its own documentation requirements published in the official TANF requirements guide. Applications are submitted through the ADSEF Digital portal. 787-289-7632. Family & Childcare. Free to apply. https://serviciosenlinea.adsef.pr.gov/

**WIC Puerto Rico** — Departamento de Salud de Puerto Rico — Programa WIC. Nutrition assistance for pregnant and postpartum women, infants, and children under 5. Participants must meet income and nutritional-risk criteria; check the current income-eligibility guide on wic.pr.gov, since the figures in the 2024-2025 guide expired June 30, 2025. Family & Childcare. Free to participants. https://wic.pr.gov/

**Plan Vital (Medicaid Puerto Rico)** — Departamento de Salud de Puerto Rico / ASES. Puerto Rico's Medicaid program, Plan Vital, provides health coverage to eligible low-income residents. Applications can be submitted online at medicaid.pr.gov; in-person offices include Bayamon, Caguas, Metropolitana, and Ponce. Enrollment counseling is available through ASES. 787-641-4224. Healthcare. Free to apply; covered services per program rules. https://medicaid.pr.gov/

**LIHEAP Puerto Rico (energy assistance)** — Administracion de Desarrollo Socioeconomico de la Familia (ADSEF). ADSEF administers Puerto Rico's LIHEAP energy assistance: subsidy and crisis payments toward home energy costs. The May 8 to September 30, 2025 application round is closed; check ADSEF for the next announced application window. 787-792-3733. Utilities. Free to apply; payments are issued toward energy costs for eligible households. https://serviciosenlinea.adsef.pr.gov/

**Desempleo Digital (Unemployment Insurance)** — Departamento del Trabajo y Recursos Humanos (DTRH). Puerto Rico's unemployment insurance claims platform. DTRH announced that Desempleo Digital is available starting September 1, 2026 through trabajo.pr.gov, where claimants can manage claims, submit documents, update information, and complete transactions online. Employment. Free to file and manage claims. https://www.trabajo.pr.gov/

**Administracion de Rehabilitacion Vocacional (ARV)** — Administracion de Rehabilitacion Vocacional (ARV). Island-wide vocational rehabilitation services for people with disabilities: individualized rehabilitation planning, counseling, transition services, supported employment, post-employment services, evaluation, and physical/mental restoration services. 787-729-0160. Disability. Free to eligible participants. https://arv.pr.gov/

**Oficina del Procurador de las Personas con Impedimentos (OPDPI)** — Oficina del Procurador de las Personas con Impedimentos (OPDPI). Puerto Rico's protection and advocacy office for people with disabilities. OPDPI provides rights defense, accessibility outreach, and assistance with discrimination and service-access issues affecting people with disabilities. 787-665-2120. Disability. Free. https://opdpi.pr.gov/

**Administracion de Vivienda Publica (public housing)** — Administracion de Vivienda Publica (AVP). Puerto Rico's public housing program for low-income families. Online applications for available public-housing units are submitted through the AVP portal; AVP also operates an online portal for current Section 8 participants. 787-759-9407 ext. 3007. Housing. Free to apply; rents are income-based for eligible residents. https://prphaenlinea.avp.pr.gov/

**Oficina del Procurador de las Personas de Edad Avanzada (OPPEA)** — Oficina del Procurador de las Personas de Edad Avanzada (OPPEA). Puerto Rico's advocate office for older adults. OPPEA provides assistance and service coordination, caregiver support, long-term-care resident advocacy, and administrative complaints concerning older adults' rights. Regional offices include San Juan and Ponce. 787-721-6121. Seniors. Free. https://oppea.pr.gov/

**Oficina del Procurador del Veterano (Veterans Advocate)** — Oficina del Procurador del Veterano (OPV). Puerto Rico's veterans advocate office. OPV provides confidential referrals and guidance on veterans' welfare, health, safety, and rights, and coordinates veterans services including Casa del Veterano admissions and the Atlantic Garden Veterans Cemetery. 787-758-5760. Veterans. Free. https://opv.pr.gov/

**Casa del Veterano (veterans residential home)** — Oficina del Procurador del Veterano (OPV). Puerto Rico's residential home for veterans. Admissions are handled by the Office of the Veterans Advocate; call the admissions line weekdays from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. for eligibility and availability information. 787-837-6574. Veterans. Per admissions guidance. https://opv.pr.gov/

**ACUDEN Head Start / Early Head Start** — Administracion para el Cuidado y Desarrollo Integral de la Ninez (ACUDEN). Free early-childhood education and family services through Head Start and Early Head Start, administered island-wide by ACUDEN with local subrecipient agencies. Applications are submitted through the ACUDEN Digital portal. 787-294-4900. Family & Childcare. Free to eligible families. https://acudendigital.familia.pr.gov

**SURI electronic tax filing (Hacienda)** — Departamento de Hacienda de Puerto Rico. Puerto Rico's electronic income-tax filing: Hacienda confirms returns can be filed electronically through SURI or certified software. The standard deadline for calendar-year individual filers is April 15. Money & Taxes. Free through SURI; certified software may charge fees. https://hacienda.pr.gov/

**CESCO driver and vehicle services** — Departamento de Transportacion y Obras Publicas (DTOP). Driver's license, vehicle registration, and identification transactions at CESCO service stations across Puerto Rico. Appointments are scheduled through the CESCO appointment system; the CESCO Digital app is also available. Transportation. Fees vary by transaction; published by DTOP. https://cesco.turnospr.com/

## Federal

**VA Caribbean Healthcare System (San Juan)** — U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. VA health care for veterans in Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands, anchored by the San Juan VA Medical Center at 10 Calle Casia, San Juan PR 00921. Enrollment in VA health care is required before appointments; apply online, by phone, or in person with VA Form 10-10EZ. Patient registration offices are open Monday-Friday 7:30 a.m.-3:30 p.m. 787-641-7582. Veterans. Per VA enrollment and priority-group rules; copays may apply. https://www.va.gov/caribbean-health-care/register-for-care/

**VA Ponce outpatient clinic** — U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs — VA Caribbean Healthcare System. VA outpatient clinic serving veterans in the Ponce area, part of the VA Caribbean Healthcare System. Located at 1802 Calle Navarra, Ponce PR 00716-2001. Veterans must be enrolled in VA health care (VA Form 10-10EZ) before making appointments. 787-641-7582. Veterans. Per VA enrollment and priority-group rules; copays may apply. https://www.va.gov/caribbean-health-care/register-for-care/

**SBA Puerto Rico and U.S. Virgin Islands District Office** — U.S. Small Business Administration. Federal small-business support for Puerto Rico: funding programs, business counseling, federal contracting certifications, and disaster recovery assistance. The district office is at 273 Ponce de Leon Ave., Suite 510, San Juan. 787-766-5572 (unverified). Small Business. Free counseling; loan terms vary by program. https://www.sba.gov/pr

**USDA Rural Development Puerto Rico** — U.S. Department of Agriculture — Rural Development. Federal rural housing, community-facility, and business programs in Puerto Rico, run from the San Juan state office. The state office and area offices (including Caguas, 30 Calle Padial) assist eligible rural residents and organizations with single-family housing loans, multifamily rental programs, and related assistance. 787-766-5095. Housing. Free to apply; loan terms vary by program. https://www.rd.usda.gov/pr

**USCIS field offices (Puerto Rico)** — U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services. USCIS field offices serve Puerto Rico for immigration interviews and in-person services. Field offices do not allow walk-ins; visits require an appointment. Confirm the office address on the appointment notice and check for office closings before traveling. 800-375-5283 (unverified). Immigration. Fees vary by form and benefit. https://www.uscis.gov/about-us/find-a-uscis-office/field-offices
