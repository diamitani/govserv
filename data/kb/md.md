## State

- **SNAP — Food Supplement Program** — monthly food benefits on an Independence Card; 30-day processing, expedited 7-day review — 1-800-332-6347 — [marylandbenefits.gov](https://marylandbenefits.gov/home/)
- **Maryland Medicaid (Medical Assistance)** — free or low-cost health coverage; year-round enrollment through Maryland Health Connection — 1-855-642-8572 (help in 200+ languages) — [marylandhealthconnection.gov](https://www.marylandhealthconnection.gov/how-to-enroll/medicaid/)
- **Office of Home Energy Programs (OHEP)** — help with heating, cooling, and electric bills; program year Jul 1–Jun 30 — 1-800-332-6347 — [dhs.maryland.gov](https://dhs.maryland.gov/ohep)
- **WIC Nutrition Program** — food benefits, breastfeeding support, nutrition education for pregnant/postpartum women, infants, children under 5 — 1-800-242-4942 — [health.maryland.gov](https://health.maryland.gov/phpa/wic/Pages/wic-apply.aspx)
- **Temporary Cash Assistance (TCA)** — Maryland's TANF cash aid for families with dependent children — 1-800-332-6347 — [dhs.maryland.gov](https://dhs.maryland.gov/?p=17390)
- **Unemployment Insurance** — up to 26 weeks of benefits; file via BEACON or phone; weekly certifications required — 667-207-6520 — [labor.maryland.gov](https://labor.maryland.gov/unemployment-insurance/claimants/)
- **Maryland Mortgage Program (MMP)** — home loans for first-time and Flex buyers plus down payment help; partner-match up to $2,500 — [mmp.maryland.gov](https://mmp.maryland.gov/)
- **Veterans Benefits Claims Assistance** — free VA-accredited help with compensation, pension, education, burial claims — 410-230-4444 (toll-free 800-446-4926) — [veterans.maryland.gov](https://veterans.maryland.gov/benefits-services/how-apply-benefits)
- **Vocational Rehabilitation (DORS)** — career counseling, training, job placement, assistive technology for people with disabilities — 410-554-9442 — [dors.maryland.gov](https://dors.maryland.gov/)
- **Maryland Legal Aid** — free civil legal services (eviction defense, Tenant Right to Counsel); below 125% FPL; intake Mon–Fri 9am–2:30pm — 1-888-465-2468 — [mdlab.org](https://www.mdlab.org/get-help-services/)
- **MVA — Driver's License and ID Services** — new residents must get a Maryland license and register vehicles within 60 days — [mva.maryland.gov](https://mva.maryland.gov/your-mva-guide/new-maryland-residents)
- **Maryland Business Express** — register an LLC/corporation, file annual reports, manage business registrations — 410-767-1330 — [businessexpress.maryland.gov](https://businessexpress.maryland.gov/)
- **Maryland Tax Connect** — free state income tax filing portal (replaced iFile Sep 1, 2026) — 1-800-MD-TAXES (unverified) — [mdcomptroller.gov](https://www.marylandcomptroller.gov/content/dam/mdcomp/md/com-docs/a-new-way-to-file-and-manage-your-state-income-taxes-online.pdf)
- **Maryland State Financial Aid (MDCAPS One-App)** — 27+ state scholarships and grants; FAFSA by March 1 (MSFAA for undocumented students) — 410-767-3300 (unverified) — [mdcaps.mhec.state.md.us](https://mdcaps.mhec.state.md.us)
- **Maryland Access Point** — single entry point for aging and disability services information, referrals, application help — 1-844-MAP-LINK (unverified) — [aging.maryland.gov](https://aging.maryland.gov/)
- **Consumer Protection Division — Attorney General** — mediates disputes with car dealers, landlords, debt collectors; file complaints online — 410-528-8662 (toll-free 1-888-743-0023) — [marylandattorneygeneral.gov](https://www.marylandattorneygeneral.gov/Pages/CPD/Complaint.aspx)
- **Child Support Services** — paternity, support orders, collection, enforcement for custodial and noncustodial parents — 1-800-332-6347 — [dhs.maryland.gov](https://dhs.maryland.gov/child-support-services/parents-and-caregivers/receiving-support-frequently-asked-questions/)
- **MTA Mobility Paratransit** — door-to-door transit for riders whose disability prevents using regular MTA service; $2.20 one-way — 410-764-8181 (option 6) — [mta.maryland.gov](https://www.mta.maryland.gov/mobility)
- **Child Care Scholarship Program** — WAITLISTED; help with licensed child care costs for working families, students, trainees — [earlychildhood.marylandpublicschools.org](https://earlychildhood.marylandpublicschools.org/system/files/filedepot/2/consumer_education_family_portal_user_guide_2025_a11yjr.pdf)
